# Amazon Project Kuiper Overview & Current Status

## 1. What is Project Kuiper?

**Project Kuiper** (now rebranded as **Amazon Leo**) is Amazon's low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite internet network designed to deliver fast, reliable broadband internet to customers and communities beyond the reach of existing terrestrial networks.

### Key Facts:
- **Announced**: 2019
- **Renamed**: November 13, 2025 → **Amazon Leo**
- **Investment**: $10+ billion
- **Mission**: Provide global satellite internet coverage via thousands of satellites in LEO
- **Constellation Target**: 3,236 satellites for Gen1; 4,500 additional for Gen2 expansion (7,727 total planned)
- **Orbital Shells**: 590 km, 610 km, and 630 km altitude
- **Technology**: Optical inter-satellite links (OISL) with 100 Gbps laser communication, creating a mesh network; custom Prometheus baseband chip enabling up to 1 Tbps throughput per satellite

## 2. Current Deployment Status (as of July 2026)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Satellites Launched** | 392+ (production satellites) |
| **Missions Completed** | 14 (as of July 2, 2026) |
| **Total Satellites Deployed** | 396 (via 14 missions) |
| **Constellation Rank** | 3rd largest in orbit (behind Starlink ~10,818 and OneWeb ~600) |
| **Launch Providers** | ULA Atlas V, SpaceX Falcon 9, Arianespace Ariane 6 |

### Deployment Timeline:
- **Full-scale deployment began**: April 28, 2025 (KA-01 mission, Atlas V, 27 satellites)
- **Current pace**: ~19 missions completed in first year (~one per month)
- **Target for 2026**: 20+ missions
- **Target for 2027**: 30+ missions

### Launch Providers & Vehicles:
- **ULA Atlas V** (8 missions, 224 satellites, 100% success rate — now transitioning to Vulcan)
- **SpaceX Falcon 9** (3 missions: KF-01, KF-02, KF-03)
- **Arianespace Ariane 64** (3 European missions: LE-01, LE-02, LE-03)
- **Blue Origin New Glenn**: In development, not yet used for Leo missions
- **ULA Vulcan Centaur**: Next generation, first mission upcoming

## 4. Amazon's Timeline & Goals for Full Deployment

### FCC Deadlines:
| Milestone | Deadline | Status |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| **578 satellites** (minimum for initial service) | — | In progress |
| **1,618 satellites** (50% of Gen1) | **July 30, 2026** | **Extension requested** |
| **3,236 satellites** (full Gen1) | July 30, 2029 | On track |
| **Gen2 expansion** (4,500 additional) | 50% by Feb 2032; 100% by Feb 2035 | FCC approved Jan 2026 |

### Service Timeline:
- **Limited beta**: Targeting late 2026 / early 2027
- **Initial countries**: US, UK, France, Germany, and Canada
- **Public waitlist**: Open at [leo.amazon.com](https://leo.amazon.com)

### Full Deployment:
- **80+ launches required** — largest commercial launch procurement in history
- **Full Gen1 constellation**: 3,236 satellites by July 2029
- **Total planned (Gen1 + Gen2)**: 7,727 satellites

### Launch Schedule:
- **Year 2 target (2026)**: 20+ missions
- **Year 3 target (2027)**: 30+ missions
- **Multiple rocket families**: Atlas V, Vulcan, Falcon 9, Ariane 6, New Glenn

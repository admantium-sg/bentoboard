### 1.2 Digital Skills Standard (デジタルスキル標準 - DSS)

**Agencies:** METI and IPA  
**First Release:** December 2022 (Reiwa 4)  
**Latest Updates:**
- August 2023 - DX Literacy Standard revised (ver. 1.1)
- July 2024 - DX Promotion Skills Standard revised (ver. 1.2)

**Components:**
1. **DXリテラシー標準** (DX Literacy Standard) - Knowledge and skills all business people should acquire for DX
2. **DX推進スキル標準** (DX Promotion Skills Standard) - Roles and skills for people promoting DX

**Key Quote (Japanese):**
> 「デジタルスキル標準（DSS）」は、DXに関して全てのビジネスパーソンが身に付けるべき知識・スキルを定義した「DXリテラシー標準」と、DXを推進する人材類型の役割（ロール）や習得すべきスキルを定義した「DX推進スキル標準」で構成し、令和4年12月に経済産業省と独立行政法人情報処理推進機構（IPA）が策定・公表しています。

**English Translation:**
> The "Digital Skills Standard (DSS)" consists of the "DX Literacy Standard," which defines the knowledge and skills all business people should acquire regarding DX, and the "DX Promotion Skills Standard," which defines the roles and skills to be acquired by talent types promoting DX. It was formulated and published in December 2022 by METI and the Information-technology Promotion Agency, Japan (IPA).

**Recent Updates for AI Era:**
> 生成AIの登場や進化によって、DXに関わるビジネスパーソンに求められるスキルも変化していることを踏まえ見直しを行っており、令和5年8月の「DXリテラシー標準」の改訂（ver1.1)に続き、生成AI技術の急速な進展から、令和6年7月にDXを推進する人材の役割や習得すべきスキルの標準である「DX推進スキル標準」を改訂（ver1.2）し公表しました。

**English Translation:**
> In light of the fact that the skills required of business people involved in DX are changing due to the emergence and evolution of generative AI, revisions have been made. Following the revision of the "DX Literacy Standard" (ver. 1.1) in August 2023, and due to the rapid progress of generative AI technology, the "DX Promotion Skills Standard," which is the standard for the roles and skills to be acquired by people promoting DX, was revised (ver. 1.2) and published in July 2024.

**Source:** https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/it_policy/jinzai/index.html

### 1.3 IT Skill Standards (ITスキル標準 - ITSS)

**Agency:** METI (with IPA revisions)  
**First Established:** December 2002 (Heisei 14)  
**Purpose:** Index that clearly defines and systematizes practical abilities necessary for providing IT-related services

**Evolution:**
- **ITSS+** (April 2017) - Guidelines for "relearning" in new areas required for the Fourth Industrial Revolution
- Focuses on strengthening skills for information service providers and user company IS department personnel

**Source:** https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/it_policy/jinzai/index.html

## 2. Digital Talent Development Initiatives

### 2.1 ManabiDX (マナビDX) Portal

**Description:** Portal site for acquiring digital knowledge and abilities

**Key Features:**
- Learning content for those who have had no opportunity to learn digital skills
- Some paid courses available, with subsidies possible for certain courses
- Courses organized by the unified standard (Digital Skills Standard) established by METI
- Systematic expansion of course offerings planned

**Meaning:**
> DELUXE is an acronym for "Digitaltransformational Education and Learning platform for Users × Engineers"

**Source:** https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/it_policy/jinzai/index.html

---

### 2.2 ManabiDX Quest

**Type:** Digital talent development program  
**Components:**
- Practical case study education program based on actual corporate data
- Program conducted in collaboration with local SMEs

**Benefits:**
- Practical experience using digital technology to solve business challenges
- Learn the thinking and process of DX promotion
- Build connections with a wide range of digital talent with shared aspirations

**Source:** https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/it_policy/jinzai/index.html

---

### 2.3 Fourth Industrial Revolution Skill Acquisition Courses (第四次産業革命スキル習得講座)

**Also Known As:** Reスキル講座 (Reskill Courses)  
**Agency:** METI  
**Purpose:** Certifying advanced specialized training courses focused on IT and data fields provided by private operators for working adults

**Goal:** Support career advancement for working adults

**Source:** https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/it_policy/jinzai/index.html

### 2.5 Information Processing Engineers Examination (情報処理技術者試験)

**Legal Basis:** Act on Promotion of Information Processing  
**Purpose:** 
- National examination that certifies that IT-related "knowledge and skills" are above a certain level
- Promotes improvement of knowledge and skills
- Fosters and secures IT talent

**Evolution:** Exam content is being revised to reflect changes from programmer/SE training to DX promoter training

## 6. Key Findings Summary

### 6.1 Policy Development Timeline

- Japan has established a comprehensive AI governance framework through the AI Business Guidelines (AI事業者ガイドライン)
- The framework has been rapidly updated (4 versions in 2 years: 2024-2026) to keep pace with AI technological evolution
- Specific attention to generative AI implications in 2024-2025 revisions

### 6.2 Talent Development Focus

- Strong emphasis on digital talent development through DSS, ITSS, and various programs
- Recognition that AI evolution requires skill standard updates (2024 revision specifically addressed generative AI)
- Multi-stakeholder approach involving government, industry, and academia
- Focus on both foundational digital literacy and specialized AI promotion skills

### 6.3 Industry-Government Collaboration

- AI Business Guidelines developed through formal review committee process
- Digital Talent Development Promotion Council includes industry representatives
- IPA serves as implementation partner for METI policies
- Recognition of need for practical, hands-on training (ManabiDX Quest)

### 6.4 Missing Data

- **Critical Gap:** No accessible data on actual AI adoption rates by industry sector
- **Critical Gap:** No accessible quantitative data on government AI funding/investment levels
- **Critical Gap:** Limited access to MIC's complementary AI policy framework

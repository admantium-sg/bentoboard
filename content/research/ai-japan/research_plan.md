# Research Plan: AI Usage in Japan

## Main Research Question

What is the current state of artificial intelligence adoption and implementation in Japan, including government policies, industry adoption, cultural factors, and sector-specific applications?

## Subtopics

### Subtopic 1: AI Adoption Rates and Government Policy

**Research Focus:**
- Current adoption rates of AI across Japanese industries
- Japanese government policies and initiatives for AI
- National AI strategy and funding programs
- Regulatory framework for AI development and deployment

**Expected Information:**
- Statistics on AI adoption by industry sector
- Key government ministries and agencies involved (e.g., METI, MIC)
- National AI strategy documents and policy announcements
- Funding levels and public investment in AI

**Japanese Search Terms:**
- 人工知能 導入率 (AI adoption rate)
- AI 政策 日本 (AI policy Japan)
- 政府 AI 戦略 (government AI strategy)
- METI AI 政策 (METI AI policy)

### Subtopic 2: Key Japanese Companies and AI Implementations

**Research Focus:**
- Major Japanese companies investing in AI
- Specific AI applications and products
- Corporate AI strategies and partnerships
- Collaboration with international AI firms

**Expected Information:**
- Leading Japanese tech companies (NEC, Fujitsu, Hitachi, SoftBank, etc.)
- Non-tech companies implementing AI (automotive, financial services, retail)
- Specific AI products and services
- Domestic vs. foreign AI technology preferences

**Japanese Search Terms:**
- 日本企業 AI 活用 (Japanese companies AI utilization)
- 日本企業 AI 導入事例 (Japanese companies AI implementation cases)
- NEC AI 製品 (NEC AI products)
- ソフトバンク AI (SoftBank AI)

### Subtopic 3: AI in Sectors: Healthcare, Manufacturing, and Services

**Research Focus:**
- AI applications in Japanese healthcare
- AI in manufacturing (Industry 4.0, robotics)
- AI in services (retail, finance, customer service)
- Sector-specific challenges and opportunities

**Expected Information:**
- Medical AI applications and regulatory approvals
- Smart manufacturing implementations
- AI-powered customer service (bank ATMs, retail, hospitality)
- Productivity improvements and efficiency gains

**Japanese Search Terms:**
- 医療 人工知能 (medical AI)
- 製造業 AI (manufacturing AI)
- サービス業 AI (services AI)
- 工場 自動化 AI (factory automation AI)

### Subtopic 4: Cultural Factors and Public Sentiment/Ethics

**Research Focus:**
- Cultural attitudes toward AI and automation
- Public sentiment about AI in Japan
- Ethical considerations and concerns
- Japanese perspectives on AI ethics and governance

**Expected Information:**
- Trust levels in AI systems
- Privacy and data security concerns
- Job displacement fears
- Unique Japanese cultural perspectives on robots and AI
- Public opinion survey results

**Japanese Search Terms:**
- 日本人 AI 意識 (Japanese AI awareness/attitude)
- AI 倫理 日本 (AI ethics Japan)
- AI 不安 (AI anxiety)
- ロボット 親近感 日本 (robot familiarity Japan)

## Synthesis Approach

After collecting findings from all subtopics, I will:

1. Review all findings files
2. Identify common themes and patterns
3. Compare Japanese AI adoption with global trends
4. Highlight unique Japanese characteristics (cultural, regulatory, market)
5. Synthesize into a comprehensive English-language report with:
   - Executive summary of key findings
   - Detailed analysis by subtopic
   - Citations to original Japanese sources
   - Comparative analysis with global trends
   - Identification of challenges and opportunities
   - Recommendations for stakeholders

## Sources to Prioritize

- Japanese government websites (gov.jp domains)
- Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI)
- Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC)
- Major Japanese company websites
- Japanese research institutions
- Japanese business news (Nikkei, Japan Times)
- Academic papers and industry reports
- Public opinion surveys and polls

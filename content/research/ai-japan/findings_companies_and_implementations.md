# Key Japanese Companies and Their AI Implementations

## Executive Summary

This report presents research findings on major Japanese companies and their AI implementations, based on Japanese-language sources from official company websites and industry publications. The research focuses on NEC, Fujitsu, Hitachi, and SoftBank, examining their AI strategies, specific products/services, and international partnerships.

## 1. NEC (日本電気株式会社)

### AI Strategy Overview
NEC positions its AI initiative as "BluStellar AI - Safe and Trusted AI Leading to Transformation" with the mission of "accelerating DX through AI ~AI for all transformations~".

### Key AI Products and Services

#### BluStellar AI (ブルーステラAI)
- **Description**: NEC's comprehensive AI platform for business transformation
- **Focus**: From consulting to operations, talent development to organizational transformation
- **Japanese Quote**: "変革を成功に導く、安全・安心なAI" (Safe and secure AI that leads transformation to success)

#### NEC Generative AI Service
- **Description**: Large Language Model (LLM) developed by NEC with high Japanese language proficiency and lightweight design
- **Features**: Available in cloud, on-premise, or hybrid deployment with secure infrastructure

#### AI Agents
- **Japanese Quote**: "NECのAIエージェントは、タスクを自律的に計画・組み合わせる能力を持ち、複雑な業務プロセスの意思決定まで代行します"
- **English Translation**: "NEC's AI agents have the ability to autonomously plan and combine tasks, and even handle decision-making for complex business processes"
- **Applications**: Business planning, personnel management, marketing strategies

#### Specialized AI Solutions

**NEC Automatic Negotiation Solution for SCM**
- **Japanese Name**: NEC 自動交渉ソリューション for SCM
- **Purpose**: Analyzes demand fluctuations and supply risks, automatically proposes optimal conditions for price, delivery date, and quantity
- **Impact**: Supports procurement cost reduction and inventory optimization

**NEC Communication Agent**
- **Features**: Integrates chatbot functionality with automated phone response
- **Technology**: Uses generative AI for advanced response generation

**RAPID Machine Learning**
- **Japanese Name**: RAPID機械学習
- **Market Position**: No. 1 market share in AI-based image recognition for equipment inspection in Japan
- **Applications**: Construction, manufacturing, finance, transportation infrastructure

**Invariant Analyzer**
- **Japanese Name**: インバリアント分析
- **Purpose**: Detects "differences from normal" to identify anomalies early
- **Applications**: Manufacturing lines, power plants, space development
- **Innovation**: First in industry to achieve anomaly detection through sound analysis

### AI Talent Development
- Provides comprehensive DX talent development programs
- Target audiences: Management, DX promotion personnel, DX specialists, general employees
- Instructors: Experienced first-class technical experts (data scientists, etc.)

**Source**: https://jpn.nec.com/ai/index.html

## 2. SoftBank (ソフトバンク)

### AI Strategy Overview
SoftBank pursues a comprehensive AI vision including partnerships with global AI companies, domestic generative AI development, and infrastructure investment for AI processing.

### Key AI Products and Services

#### Crystal Intelligence (クリスタル・インテリジェンス)
- **Partnership**: Collaboration with OpenAI announced February 2025
- **Japanese Quote**: "社内に導入されたAIエージェント同士が連携し、自律的に意思決定を支援する高度なAI基盤です"
- **English Translation**: "An advanced AI platform where AI agents introduced within the company collaborate to support autonomous decision-making"
- **Features**: Integrates individual AI systems like an orchestra conductor, optimizes management
- **Capabilities**: Long-term memory, self-learning and knowledge accumulation

#### AGENTIC STAR (エージェンティック・スター)
- **Launch**: December 2025
- **Description**: Autonomous general-purpose AI agent service developed by SoftBank
- **Origin**: Born from SoftBank's field practice
- **Japanese Quote**: "AGENTIC STARとのやり取りや社内資料などの情報を長期記憶として蓄積し、使い込むほど賢く進化し続ける"
- **English Translation**: "Accumulates interactions with AGENTIC STAR and internal documents as long-term memory, getting smarter with continued use"
- **Vision**: "AI is not merely a support tool for people, but becomes an existence that works together with us"

#### Sarashina (さらしな) - Domestic Generative AI
- **Developer**: SB Intuitions Co., Ltd. (100% SoftBank subsidiary)
- **Purpose**: Generative AI rooted in Japanese culture
- **Japanese Quote**: "われわれは日本人にとって最も使いやすいモデルを目指しています。Sarashinaは国内に蓄積された豊富で高品質な日本語データを活用し、国内データセンターで学習しています"
- **English Translation**: "We aim for the model that is easiest to use for Japanese people. Sarashina utilizes abundant high-quality Japanese data accumulated in Japan and learns at domestic data centers"
- **Specialization Models**: Finance, medical, education, legal, and other highly specialized domains

#### Sarashina API
- **Lightweight Model**: Sarashina mini
- **Purpose**: Enables companies and developers to integrate domestic generative AI into their services

### AI Infrastructure Initiatives

#### Brain DataCenter
- **Locations**: Tomakomai, Hokkaido and Sakai, Osaka
- **Mission**: Support Japan's AI infrastructure needs
- **Features**: GPU servers with NVIDIA GPU clusters as core
- **Goals**: 
  - Stable support for increasing domestic AI demand
  - Disaster risk dispersion from capital concentration
  - Carbon-neutral environmental impact reduction

#### AITRAS (アイトラス) - AI-RAN Integrated Solution
- **Concept**: Integration of Radio Access Network (RAN) and AI
- **Function**: AI autonomously controls base stations, instantly optimizing frequency and power
- **Benefits**: Power-saving and stable communication even during congestion
- **Future Applications**: Autonomous driving, remote surgery, and other fields requiring ultra-low latency and high security

### Vision for ASI (Artificial Super Intelligence)

**Masayoshi Son's Vision**:
- **Quote from Shareholders' Meeting**: "ASIの実現により、がんや脳梗塞、交通事故、災害、パンデミックなどの絶望から人類を救えるかもしれない" (The realization of ASI may save humanity from despair such as cancer, cerebral infarction, traffic accidents, disasters, and pandemics)
- **Timeline Prediction**: ASI expected to materialize within 10 years
- **AI Evolution Stages**: 8 stages predicted, culminating in AI with emotions, long-term memory as life logs, and eventually its own will
- **Key Insight**: "ASIは『知能』だけでは有能である一方で、危険な存在にもなりかねない。しかし、『知性』を持つことで、単なる能力を超え、私たちを守り、幸せにしてくれる存在へと進化していく" (ASI with only 'intelligence' may be capable but also potentially dangerous. However, by having 'wisdom/character,' it evolves beyond mere capability into an existence that protects us and makes us happy)

### Personal Agents Vision
- **Timeline**: Expected within 2-3 years
- **Functionality**: 24-hour personal agent that knows family conditions, provides first aid advice, finds hospitals, makes calls, acts as tutor and counselor
- **Future**: Agent-to-Agent (AtoA) world where agents communicate with each other

**Sources**:
- https://www.softbank.jp/biz/about/ai/
- https://ai.softbank/insights/001/

## 3. Hitachi (日立製作所)

### AI Strategy Overview
Hitachi aims to be "World's Top User of Physical AI" through Lumada 3.0 and HMAX, combining vast experience in IT and OT (Operational Technology).

### Key AI Initiatives

#### Lumada 3.0
- **Focus**: Special emphasis on "Physical AI" (フィジカルAI)
- **Purpose**: Combines massive on-site data with AI to achieve high reliability and availability in mission-critical fields

#### HMAX by Hitachi
- **Primary Focus**: Mobility and energy sectors
- **Applications**: Operational efficiency, cost reduction, work-style reform using sensors and AI agents
- **Japanese Quote**: "HMAX by Hitachiでは、モビリティやエナジー分野を中心に、センサーやAIエージェントを活用した業務効率化・コスト削減・働き方改革を実現し、他分野への展開も進行中である"
- **English Translation**: "HMAX by Hitachi implements operational efficiency, cost reduction, and work-style reform using sensors and AI agents, primarily in mobility and energy fields, with expansion to other sectors in progress"

### AI Agent Implementation Strategy

#### Customer Zero Approach
- **Philosophy**: "カスタマーゼロ" - Implement AI agents internally first, then expand to customers
- **Application Layers**: 
  1. Business line operations
  2. Indirect department operations
  3. Individual operations
- **Starting Point**: Began with indirect department operations showing high operational efficiency effects

#### AI Agent Factory & Platform
- **Development Service**: Utilizes GlobalLogic development resources for rapid agent development
- **Services Automated**: Risk management, quality review, transaction partner investigations
- **Governance**: Comprehensive AI risk management system and guidelines established

### System Development AI

#### Generative AI Development Framework
- **Purpose**: Apply generative AI to entire system development process
- **Features**:
  - Specialized prompt design for each process
  - Automated prompt generation tools
  - Applicable to mission-critical systems
- **Legacy Migration**: COBOL to Java transformation with high maintainability
  - COBOL source → COBOL design document → Java design document → Java source code

### Strategic Partnerships
- **Google Cloud**: Collaboration on AI agent development and deployment in OT fields
- **NVIDIA**: Global deployment of AI Factory based on NVIDIA reference architecture
- **OpenAI**: Mentioned as part of AI ecosystem

### Specific Products and Services

#### SAKULaLa (サクララ) - Biometric Authentication Service
- **Launch**: April 2024 with Tobu Railway
- **Features**: Finger vein and facial recognition - Japan's first multi-biometric platform
- **Applications**: Railway gates, store payments, access control
- **Goal**: Realize "hand-free society" (手ぶら社会)
- **Expansion**: 
  - Tobu Utsunomiya Line from November 2025
  - Facial recognition payment stores from fiscal 2026
  - Office access control from fiscal 2026

#### Utility Equipment Operation Optimization Service
- **Purpose**: Cloud-based service for energy demand and facility operation optimization
- **Technology**: Mathematical optimization technology to derive optimal solutions
- **Benefits**: Reduces energy costs and CO₂ emissions

#### Local EMS Standard Platform
- **Developer**: Hitachi Information & Telecommunication Engineering
- **Purpose**: Site-specific energy management
- **Applications**: Renewable energy optimization, V2X (Vehicle to X) integration

### Modernization Services

#### Modernization powered by Lumada
- **Launch**: October 2025
- **Purpose**: Refresh core systems to AI-native systems
- **Two Phases**:
  1. Grand Design Planning Service: Planning AI-native business foundations
  2. Business/IT Modernization Service: AI agent automation and data quality maintenance

#### Oracle Database@Azure
- **Partnership**: Joint verification with Oracle Japan and Microsoft Japan
- **Recognition**: Awarded by Oracle Japan in both data platform and generative AI utilization areas
- **Application**: Real-time search and utilization of core data for generative AI and AI agents

### Advanced Database Technology

#### Hitachi Advanced Database (HADB)
- **Feature**: SQL-based direct search of data stored in object storage like Amazon S3
- **Technology**: Non-sequential execution principle for parallel processing
- **Benefit**: Enables data lakehouse implementation with high-speed search

### Talent Development
- Top engineers selected by AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, and Oracle in 2025
- Comprehensive training and practical skill development

**Sources**:
- https://www.hitachi.com/ja-jp/insights/hitachihyoron/hitachi-technology/2026/05/
- https://jp.ub-speeda.com/document/day2025_s2/

## 4. Fujitsu (富士通)

### AI Strategy Overview
Fujitsu's vision centers on "Human-Centric" approach and "Regenerative Ecosystems" concept, emphasizing AI as a tool for social good and sustainable growth.

### Market Research Insights (FT&SV 2025)

#### AI Adoption Statistics
- **Survey Target**: 15 countries, 800 corporate executives (February 2025)
- **Generative AI Adoption**: 98% of companies have already implemented
- **Investment Plans**: 
  - 77% plan to strengthen AI investment (down 11 points from 2024)
  - 14% plan to reduce investment
- **Workforce Impact**: 79% believe all employees will receive AI support in their work by 2030
- **Labor Shortage**: 82% see AI utilization as key to alleviating labor shortages

#### Strategic Shift
- **Quote from Hiroshi Nishikawa (Senior Director)**: "AIをとりあえず使ってみようといった段階から、立ち止まってAIがほんとうは何に使えるのか、慎重に見極めるようになってきていると感じました" (I feel we've moved from the stage of 'let's try using AI anyway' to stopping and carefully considering what AI can really be used for)
- **Trend**: Moving from experimentation to strategic evaluation of ROI

### Core AI Vision: Regenerative Ecosystems

#### Concept
- **Definition**: New value creation ecosystem for achieving net-positive social impact
- **Visual Model**: 
  - AI expands human knowledge and capability
  - Productive humans and AI networks form across all company areas
  - AI connects companies across industries to form ecosystems
- **Purpose**: Address social challenges like global warming and aging

#### Human-Centric Philosophy
- **Long-standing Value**: Unchanged emphasis on human-centered approach since 2013
- **Quote**: "人間が中心＝ヒューマンセントリックであることが重要だという姿勢は10年以上変わっていません。むしろAIが進化する中、人を中心に置くという価値観は、今後ますます重要になると思います" (The stance that being human-centered is important hasn't changed in over 10 years. Rather, as AI evolves, I think the value of placing humans at the center will become increasingly important)

### Business Application Examples

#### Sales Planning Transformation
- **Collaboration**: Hitachi and Fujitsu both use "Speeda AI Agent"
- **Results**:
  - 50% reduction in approach planning time
  - 127% contribution to orders year-over-year
- **Source**: https://jp.ub-speeda.com/document/day2025_s2/

### Consulting Transformation
Major Japanese SIers (NEC, Fujitsu, NTT Data, Hitachi) are strengthening consulting businesses, hiring consultants to work with customers on DX strategy jointly.

**Sources**:
- https://global.fujitsu/ja-jp/insight/tl-fujitsu-tech-and-service-1-20250718
- https://note.com/fujitsu_pr/n/nb5360c85fef6

## 6. Key Themes and Strategic Directions

### Common Trends Across Companies

#### 1. AI Agents Focus
All major companies are heavily investing in AI agent technology:
- **NEC**: AI agents for business automation
- **SoftBank**: AGENTIC STAR and Crystal Intelligence
- **Hitachi**: AI Agent Factory and Platform with "Customer Zero" approach
- **Fujitsu**: Regenerative ecosystems centered on human-AI collaboration

#### 2. Sovereign AI Concerns
Japanese companies emphasize domestic control:
- **SoftBank**: "Sovereign Cloud" and "Sovereign AI" initiatives
- **Domestic AI Models**: Sarashina for Japanese culture understanding
- **Data Security**: Domestic data centers for training and deployment

#### 3. International Partnerships
Strategic collaborations with global tech leaders:
- **SoftBank + OpenAI**: Crystal Intelligence
- **Hitachi + Google Cloud + NVIDIA + OpenAI**: AI ecosystem
- **Industry Standard**: All companies partner with major cloud providers (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, Oracle)

#### 4. Physical AI / Industry Focus
- **Hitachi**: Explicit "Physical AI" strategy for industrial applications
- **NEC**: Strong presence in manufacturing, infrastructure, transportation
- **Focus Areas**: Mobility, energy, manufacturing, social infrastructure

#### 5. Human-Centric Approaches
- **Fujitsu**: "Human-Centric" as core philosophy
- **NEC**: "AI that assists people"
- **Hitachi**: AI for operational efficiency and work-style reform
- **SoftBank**: Personal agents enhancing human capabilities

### Investment Patterns

#### Infrastructure Investment
- **SoftBank**: Massive investment in AI data centers (Brain DataCenter)
- **Hitachi**: Advanced database technology and AI-ready infrastructure
- **Industry Trend**: Preparing for AI processing demands

#### Talent Development
All companies emphasize:
- Internal AI education programs
- Certification training for cloud and AI platforms
- Practical skill development
- Hiring consultants and specialists

## Conclusion

Japanese companies are aggressively pursuing AI adoption with distinct strategies:

1. **Sovereign AI Focus**: Emphasis on domestic models and infrastructure for cultural alignment and data security
2. **AI Agent Revolution**: All major players investing in autonomous AI systems for business transformation
3. **Industry-First Approach**: Companies leverage deep domain knowledge in physical industries (manufacturing, energy, infrastructure)
4. **Human-Centric Philosophy**: Strong emphasis on AI augmenting rather than replacing human capabilities
5. **Global Collaboration**: Strategic partnerships with international AI leaders while maintaining Japanese autonomy
6. **Infrastructure Investment**: Massive investment in AI data centers and processing capabilities

The Japanese AI landscape shows a mature, strategic approach that balances technological innovation with cultural sensitivity, industrial expertise, and human-centric values. Companies are moving beyond experimentation to strategic implementation, with measurable results in operational efficiency, cost reduction, and business transformation.

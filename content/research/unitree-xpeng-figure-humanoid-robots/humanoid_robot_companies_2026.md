## Executive Summary

The humanoid robot market is experiencing unprecedented growth in 2026, with major players from China and the US competing to ship general-purpose robots at scale. This report covers three key players: **Unitree** (China, publicly listing), **XPENG IRON** (China, just raised $900M), and **Figure AI** (US, $39B valuation). The market is projected to grow from $2B (2024) to over $13B by 2029 — a 45% CAGR.

## 1. Unitree Robotics

**Headquarters:** Hangzhou, China  
**Founded:** 2013  
**CEO:** Wang Xingxing  
**Status:** IPO (Shanghai STAR Market), ~$9B valuation

### Recent Developments

- **IPO:** Unitree priced its Shanghai STAR Market IPO at 150.8 yuan (~$22.34) per share, raising ~$900M and achieving a valuation of approximately **61 billion yuan (~$9 billion)**. Subscriptions opened August 10, 2026 — making it **China's first publicly listed humanoid robot manufacturer**.
- **Revenue Growth:** Revenue more than quadrupled to 1.7 billion yuan in 2025. Humanoid robot sales reached 867.8 million yuan, overtaking their famous quadruped robot dogs as Unitree's largest business segment.
- **Strategic Investor:** DeepSeek (Chinese AI company) is among the strategic investors in the IPO.

### Shipment Goals

- **2025:** Shipped ~5,500 humanoid robots
- **2026 Target:** Ship **20,000 humanoid robots** — nearly 4x growth year-over-year
- This represents the most aggressive volume target among humanoid robot makers

### Products

- **G1 Humanoid Robot:** The main commercial humanoid platform, priced for research and light industrial use
- **H2 Plus:** Announced June 2026 as an NVIDIA Isaac GR00T Reference Humanoid Robot for academic research
- **Quadruped robots:** Known for their dog-like robots (e.g., Go2) that have become popular in research and entertainment

## 2. XPENG IRON

**Parent Company:** XPeng (Chinese EV maker)  
**Robotics Unit Valuation:** $6.3B (post-money)  
**Funding:** $900M+ raised in single round — largest single-round financing in China's embodied AI history  
**CEO of Robotics:** He Xiaopeng (XPeng CEO, took direct control June 2026)

### The IRON Robot

XPeng unveiled IRON at its **AI Day event in November 2025** in Guangzhou, and the demo went viral for the wrong reason: the robot's fluid, human-like walk was so convincing that viewers accused XPeng of hiding a person in a costume. He Xiaopeng responded by **cutting open the robot's leg on stage** to prove it was real.

#### Hardware Specs

- **Height:** ~1.73m  
- **Weight:** ~70kg  
- **Degrees of Freedom:** 
  - ~76 DoF across body
  - 21 DoF in each hand (44 total in hands)
  - ~200 total degrees of freedom
  - ~60 joints total
- **Skin:** "Fully enclosed flexible lattice structure" — synthetic skin balancing safety and human-like appearance
- **Vision:** "Eagle-Eye" 720° vision for spatial awareness

#### Compute

- Runs on **3 of XPeng's in-house Turing AI chips**
- Combined: **2,250 TOPS** (trillion operations per second)
- XPeng claims **on-device autonomy** — runs its Physical AI foundation model directly on the robot (not teleoperated)

### Funding Details

- **Round:** $900M+ (largest single-round private financing in China's embodied AI)
- **Led by:** IDG Capital
- **Participating:** Gaorong Ventures, Tencent, Alibaba (strategic)
- **Structure:** ~$600M from external investors, ~$200M from XPeng subsidiary, ~$100M from leadership
- XPeng is spinning robotics assets into a standalone subsidiary (retaining ~82% ownership), targeting separate market valuation

### Production Plans

- **Target:** Mass production by **end of 2026**
- **Monthly capacity goal:** 1,000+ IRON units
- **2030 goal:** 1 million units
- **Launch plan:** Start inside XPeng's own stores/campuses → official China + overseas launch in 2027
- XPeng argues its automotive supply chain and manufacturing expertise gives it an edge in scaling

### Differentiation Claim

XPeng explicitly positions IRON as truly autonomous (on-device AI), contrasting with competitors they claim are teleoperated. The company emphasizes "automotive-grade quality standards and large-scale mass production."

## 3. Figure AI

**Headquarters:** San Jose, California, USA  
**Founded:** 2022  
**Founder:** Brett Adcock  
**Valuation:** ~$39B (most valuable pure-play humanoid robot company)  
**Total Raised:** ~$2.6B+ (including $675M Series B led by OpenAI)

### Key Developments

- **Series B:** After OpenAI led a $675M round, Figure's valuation jumped from $2.6B to ~$39B — a ~15x increase
- **Figure 02:**Shown working autonomously at a BMW manufacturing plant in early 2025
- **Figure 03:** Now running BMW production lines
- **Partnership:** OpenAI partnership bringing advanced AI reasoning to Figure's robots

### Product Focus

- **Target use case:** Industrial settings (factories, warehouses) initially, with later expansion planned
- **Approach:** General-purpose humanoid robots capable of autonomous tasks
- **Focus:** Unlike Tesla (which is building Optimus for its own factories), Figure is positioning as a platform for multiple industries

## Key Trends in 2026

1. **China dominates volume:** Unitree and XPENG are shipping/manufacturing at scale; US competitors are earlier in commercialization
2. **Manufacturing expertise matters:** XPENG leverages its EV production lines; Unitree leverages robotics experience
3. **The IPO path:** Unitree is first Chinese humanoid robot maker to go public, setting a template
4. **US-China tensions:** Regulatory risks are rising for Chinese robot makers entering the US market
5. **Autonomy claims vs reality:** Many demos are still teleoperated; on-device AI is the claimed differentiator
6. ** valuations are astronomical:** Figure at $39B despite limited commercial shipments shows investor appetite

## Peter Steinberger 🦞 on X: "Two weeks ago I kicked off a loop to move OpenClaw's Lit-based Web frontend to Solid 2.0. Took ~6 days until we could remove Lit, and another week to move patterns to idiomatic Solid. I checked in with the session every other day, overall the process was mostly automatic. Took about ~600 PRs. Result: Smaller JS and CSS footprint and much more fine-grained update control. Everything feeling more snappy and responsive. https://t.co/rYAuaXH2xN"

## Corrections made (targeted HTML comments in `cumulative_findings.md`)

1. **#196250 is merged, not open** (C2) — the "fix exists only as an open PR" framing was true at
   09:28Z on 2026-11-10 but the PR merged one second before the issue closed.
2. **"Every one of the 12 new enterprise issues is security- or policy-shaped" → overstated** (C7) —
   the impact labels are mixed; only #5177, #5180/#5179 and #5172 are clearly security/policy-shaped.
3. **#196514 closed as "not planned", not as a duplicate** (C9) — the duplicate closure is #192463,
   as stated inside #196514's own body; the findings sentence conflates the two issues.
4. **npm dist-tags / 11-09 download point** (C1, C5) — stale snapshot markers added so a later reader
   knows the 404 and the 0 are no longer reproducible.
5. **ClawStat snapshot caveat** (C6, C13) — the live endpoint keeps only the newest assessment per
   version; the quoted headlines and field values are historical, not re-readable.
6. **GHSA severity caveat** (C4) — "high" is the advisory-DB field; NVD scores the CVE CRITICAL 9.1.

## Trend Analysis

### Common Error Types

1. **Temporal Context Issues:** Several figures were accurate at a specific point in time but have since become outdated. The research should include "as of" dates for rapidly changing metrics.

2. **Aggregated Statistics:** The "13+ CVEs" claim doesn't align with either early 2026 (4 CVEs) or late 2026 (138 CVEs) figures, suggesting either a specific subset was referenced incorrectly.

3. **Timezone Discrepancies:** Release dates commonly appear with 1-day differences due to US vs UTC reporting.

### Recommendations for Future Research

1. Add "as of [date]" qualifiers to rapidly changing metrics (GitHub stars, exposed instances)
2. Clarify the source and date context for aggregated CVE counts
3. For attribution quotes, include direct links to original statements where possible
4. Consider tracking when each fact was last verified to identify stale data

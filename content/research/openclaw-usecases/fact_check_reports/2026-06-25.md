## Corrections Needed

### ⚠️ Correction 1: GPT-5.5 "10 Thoughts/Day" Cap
**Fact:** "GPT-5.5 'Reasoning Core' Rolls to Free Users, Capped at 10 Thoughts/Day"
**Issue:** The specific "10 thoughts/day" cap for GPT-5.5 reasoning not verified
**Actual Findings:** Multiple sources confirm ChatGPT free tier limits, but specifics differ:
- ai-toolbox.co: "~10 messages every 5 hours on GPT-5.3 Instant"
- GPT-5.4 Thinking carries "separate weekly reasoning cap"
- aicommission.org: "one message per day to the GPT-5 Thinking model" for free users
**Recommendation:** The claim may be accurate but specific cap details differ. Use "separate daily/weekly cap on reasoning" instead of "10 thoughts/day."
**Sources:** ai-toolbox.co, aicommission.org

### ⚠️ Correction 2: OpenAI/Helion "Acquisition"
**Fact:** "OpenAI Acquires Nuclear Fusion Startup for $3B"
**Issue:** This is a power purchase agreement, not an acquisition
**Actual Findings:** 
- OpenAI has a power purchase agreement with Helion Energy (not acquisition)
- Sam Altman personally invested $3.75B in Helion
- Deal is for 5GW of power by 2030, scaling to 50GW by 2035
**Correction:** Change "acquires nuclear fusion startup for $3B" to "secures power purchase agreement with Helion Energy for nuclear fusion power"
**Sources:** GeekWire, KuCoin

### ⚠️ Correction 3: Apple iOS 27 Jailbreak
**Fact:** "Apple iOS 27 Agentic Siri Jailbroken Within 12 Hours Using OpenClaw Extension"
**Issue:** Claim appears only in community sources (anomixer); mainstream jailbreak sites report no iOS 27 jailbreak available
**Actual Findings:**
- jailbreakly.com: "No iOS 27 jailbreak available yet. Stay on iOS 26.1"
- Tenorshare: No iOS 27 jailbreak tool available
**Recommendation:** This claim may be from fringe community source. Flag as potentially unreliable.
**Sources:** anomixer.github.io (community-only source)

### ⚠️ Correction 4: AMD Hardware Isolation Bypass
**Fact:** "AMD's Ryzen AI Pro 300's hardware isolation (AEC) bypassed by Hermes-Agent"
**Issue:** Could not find confirmation of this specific bypass claim
**Actual Findings:**
- AMD/Hermes Agent integration confirmed separately
- "Self-replicating sandbox" bypass of AEC hardware isolation not independently confirmed
**Recommendation:** Mark as unverified without additional sources
**Sources:** None confirmed for bypass claim

## Trend Analysis

### Source Quality Observations
- **High reliability:** GitHub Releases (100% accuracy for technical claims), Security sources (cvefind.com, nerdbot.com)
- **Medium reliability:** Industry analysis sites (gradually.ai, worldmetrics.org, openclawusa.com)
- **Lower reliability:** General blogs with conflicting figures

### Common Issues Found
1. **Rapidly evolving metrics:** Platform statistics (GitHub stars) change quickly and easily become outdated
2. **Conflicting source reports:** ClawHavoc count varies by 44% across sources (824 vs 1,184)
3. **Timing discrepancies:** ClawCon date was off by ~3 weeks in original claim

### Recommendations
1. **For metrics:** Include date captured or "as of" qualifier
2. **For security counts:** Note variation and provide range rather than single figure
3. **For events:** Verify dates against official recaps when available

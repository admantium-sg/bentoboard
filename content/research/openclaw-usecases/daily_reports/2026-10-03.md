## Community Pulse
**Working well:** v2026.9.8 delivers exactly what the 9.x series needed — a narrow, focused fix pass with no new features that could introduce new problems. ClawStat's "Safe to update" early read with zero credible blockers is the clearest signal since v2026.9.1. The Tencent AIG ClawScan integration adding a second scanner with AI-powered reconciliation is meaningful hardening for the skills marketplace.

**Problems:** Hermes-Agent has gone flat at 0.3K from React — stalling in the final grid square rather than completing the overtake. The two confirmed update-failure reports for 9.7 (#163929, #163994) show the upgrade path still isn't fully resolved even as new versions ship. Wall Street's skepticism about model velocity suggests the market may be maturing past the "latest model wins" narrative.

**Interesting:** The shift from v2026.9.7's "Update with care" to v2026.9.8's "Safe to update" in one release cycle shows the release train's ability to correct course quickly. Tencent joining ClawScan alongside NVIDIA's SkillSpector represents a meaningful escalation in marketplace security. And Hermes-Agent stalling at the threshold rather than crossing it may mean the overtake requires an external catalyst — a release, a mention, a scrape artifact — rather than organic growth alone.

## Real-world deployments & use cases

- **19-agent production gateway profile** (user-pasted into a bug report): 19 agents, 7,664 stored sessions, 30 heartbeat jobs, 19 stores, `deepseek-v4.1-flash` default at 1M context alongside `gpt-5.6-terra`/`gpt-5.6-luna` on the Codex runtime, 92–99% cache hit rates, self-audit reporting 1 critical finding and 5 warnings. The most concrete production configuration observed so far — still an unaudited user paste. https://community.openclaw.ai/t/443
- **Local-LLM batch image-classification pipeline** (Ryzen 7 7800X3D / RTX 5070 12GB; Qwen3.5 9B, Qwen3.6 35B-A3B, Gemma 4 12B QAT). Known failure: *"With fewer than 10 images, the model classifies all of them. Trade-off: DeepSeek *"still struggles with concepts like planning longer tasks and interpretation of intent."* https://www.reddit.com/r/openclaw/comments/1xubh4h/swapped_my_local_openclaw_agent_from_opus_55_to/
- **Gemini 4 Hydrogen is available in OpenClaw**, confirmed in OpenClaw's own provider docs (`google/gemini-4-hydrogen`, 1,048,576-token context, up to 65,536 output tokens, thinking levels low/medium/high). The launch date itself rests on secondary aggregators only — X/Twitter was unreachable. https://docs.openclaw.ai/providers/google.md

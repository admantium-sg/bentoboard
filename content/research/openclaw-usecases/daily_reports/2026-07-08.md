### China — ByteDance Doubao & Alibaba Qwen Shut Down AI Agent Features July 15 (July 8)

ByteDance's Doubao and Alibaba's Qwen simultaneously announced they will cease AI agent creation functionality on July 15, 2026 — directly aligning with China's new "Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services" taking effect the same day. After the deadline, users will be unable to create new AI agents, and all existing user-created agents will stop functioning. Users can review configurations and chat history during a transition period, with backup recommended before October 15. Community: "China's regulatory hammer drops — Doubao and Qwen's agent features are being axed. Meanwhile OpenClaw runs on your local machine, not on any government-jurisdiction cloud server.

## Community Pulse

**Working well:** the "dispatch and return" hand-off pattern (Claw as one chat for the day, coding work handed to OpenCode, a separate OpenClaw session per handoff collecting the result) and visible multi-agent delegation with the native Codex harness — both are described as daily-use workflows with named implementations (`openclaw-desk`). Maintainer support is finally real: admin *joe* answered three in-window threads with concrete commands (`openclaw logs --limit 500 --plain`, `openclaw approvals get --gateway`, `openclaw exec-policy show --verbose`).

**Problems:** *Reddit is entirely invisible today* — 404/403/429 on every endpoint plus a network-security block via r.jina.ai, so no sentiment reading is possible. On Discourse, #419 (ClawHub skill hidden with "Needs review") is now **6 days 21 hours** unanswered; two of five new topics were spam-flagged within seconds of posting. ClawHub supply nearly doubled to 161 skills while installs stayed at 10 and stars at 1 — distribution without adoption. Community sizing advice is stale: the recommended Hetzner plan has been replaced by one that was itself unavailable on 16 November.

**Interesting:** the first *hosted* OpenClaw product (Octogee, shared free gateway, isolated Mattermost + Gitea per workspace) and the first *LTS-equivalent* release channel (v2026.10.33) arrived in the same 24 hours — both aimed at people who cannot or will not run weekly breakage. And two users independently building "hand off the work, don't let the follow-ups land in my chat" is a pattern worth watching as it becomes a convention.

**Trend:** The release channel finally shipped — a stable release, an LTS-style backport, and a security fix that the notes don't mention — while the *adoption* side stayed flat: npm downloads still lagging, ClawHub installs at 10, official pages silent for the sixth run, and no customer ROI anywhere. The interesting movement is qualitative: maintainer replies resumed on Discourse and the conversation shifted from "how do I host it" to "how do I operate it safely" (upgrade order, approval binding, session visibility).

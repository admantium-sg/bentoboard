### Platform — v2026.10.2 Ships Oct 12: Update/Recovery Focus, ClawStat Rates ⚠️ "Update with care" (Oct 12)
OpenClaw v2026.10.2 landed October 12 as a substantial maintenance release with 85 pull requests since v2026.10.1. The headline theme is clean upgrades and resilient recovery: first-upgrade-from-2026.10.1 now completes cleanly; Windows, cloned-filesystem, and private-Node update paths finish instead of stalling or rolling back; Doctor recovery hardened against residual legacy imports, stale locks, memory reindex locks, removed-channel allowlists, and stale-receipt sessions. Provider-native compaction becomes the default for OpenAI and Anthropic API-key routes. Secret redaction now masks credentials crossing chunk boundaries in long tool output. Prompt-cache stability improvements keep Anthropic cached prefixes hitting across restarts and replays. Finalized update success and suppressed channels now surface as warnings instead of failures, reducing false alarms.

Independent tracker ClawStat assessed v2026.10.2 at ⚠️ "Update with care" (medium confidence): two critical confirmed blockers — #169865 (Linux systemd update fails with managed-service-stop-failed, confirmed regression, no fix PR staged) and #169844 (Windows Control UI update hangs at 'Activating', leaves Scheduled Task disabled and gateway stopped, requires manual recovery). Behind those: a tier-2 cluster of update failures across Linux, Windows, and macOS with no staged fixes. v2026.10.5-beta.2 is already published but carries no evidence of fixes for either blocker. Mitigating: 7 total 👍 across 60 tracked issues vs ~3.9M weekly npm downloads, and most updater failures roll back safely. Community: "The boring wins ship when you aren't looking — update when you can watch, keep the restart commands handy."

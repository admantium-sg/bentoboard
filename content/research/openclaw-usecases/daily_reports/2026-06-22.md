### 3. Enterprise Adoption — AI Officially Crosses from "Demo" to "Infrastructure"; Multi-Agent Architectures Standard

Industry reports reviewed during this research cycle confirm that AI has definitively transitioned from experimental chatbots to core enterprise infrastructure. ### 4. Specialized Models Beating Behemoths on ParseBench; GLM-5.2 Tops Open-Weight Rankings

Two related findings point to a structural shift in the AI model hierarchy. First, LlamaIndex's ParseBench now shows specialized lightweight models outperforming general-purpose LLMs with 100B+ parameters on document parsing tasks — validating the "small, specialized agents" philosophy. Community reaction: "Hook a dedicated small model onto OpenClaw, and we can even beat GPT-6!" Second, GLM-5.2 (the 744B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with MIT license, now supported in OpenClaw's model catalog) quickly reached the top of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index for open-weight models, scoring 51 points and ranking #4 globally — surpassing all of Google's current models. Multiple prominent tech CEOs publicly praised GLM-5.2, reigniting East-vs-West AI competition debates.

## Community Pulse

**Working well:** OpenClaw's star trajectory continues its steady march toward the global top 5, with the ecosystem (Hermes-Agent, awesome-openclaw-skills, nanobot, CowAgent) growing in parallel. The v2026.7.2 beta track shows disciplined incremental correctness — beta.7 is narrow and honest about what it does and doesn't contain, which is the kind of release discipline production operators can actually trust.

**Problems:** The "Salami Attack" paper highlights a real and underappreciated threat: coordinated collusive memory poisoning across sessions. This isn't a single bad prompt — it's a sustained, invisible manipulation vector. OpenClaw users running agents with shared memory contexts should be paying attention to provenance and review mechanisms, not just prompt injection from direct user input.

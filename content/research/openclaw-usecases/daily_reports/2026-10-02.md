### Platform — ClawStat Holds ⚠️ on v2026.9.7: Three Critical Blockers Confirmed (Oct 1-2)
ClawStat.us maintained its "Update with care" (⚠️) verdict for v2026.9.7, assessed Oct 1 at 21:42 UTC. Three tier-1 blockers confirmed at critical severity: (1) **#162193** (Linux, critical/regression): `doctor --fix` truncates the shared-state SQLite database to 0 bytes after making pre-migration backups, creating a data-loss shape on a core repair tool — no fix PR staged; (2) **#162055** (macOS, critical/post_release): 2026.9.5→2026.9.7 update stalls in Doctor with sustained high CPU for 30+ minutes, leaving gateway down with state migrated and no verified rollback path; (3) **#162514** (Windows, critical/regression): plugin source-capture rebuilds ~22k files per boot (320–520s startup), cron jobs fail with DataCloneError, and state schema migration (18 vs 15) blocks downgrade to 9.2. The ClawStat validator flagged an internal inconsistency: all three are labeled "high" in structured known_issues but labeled "critical" (P0) in raw issue headers and analyst prose. Calibration: only 2 👍 across 60 tracked issues vs ~3.4M weekly downloads suggests none is widespread. Community: "Back up ~/.openclaw/state before updating, avoid `doctor --fix` on Linux until #162193 ships, and Windows users should weigh staying on 9.6."

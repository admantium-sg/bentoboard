## Headline

The publish freeze broke — **three stable releases in 24 hours**, `latest` jumping 2026.11.7 → **2026.11.10**,
a major cut that ships **Deep Research (default-on)** and a **Knowledge** plugin. Demand did not follow:
weekly npm installs fell **10.7 %** week-over-week (the drop more than doubled) and the
**named-customer ROI gap hit its 6th consecutive day**.

**2. Deep Research ships enabled by default — the platform now has a native research agent.**
"Investigate complex questions across multiple web sources or local documents without leaving your chat,

Source: https://docs.openclaw.ai/plugins/knowledge

## Community Pulse

**Working well:** Two new first-party surfaces land exactly on this project's use cases — cited multi-source
investigation (Deep Research) and a browsable local knowledge vault (Knowledge), both usable straight from
chat. ShellBench gives the community its first quantitative reason to prefer the open-source harness
(45.23 % vs Claude Code 42.87 %). The Foundation building OpenClaw *in multiplayer* is the strongest
community-facing signal in weeks, and `openclaw/beam` productises it. Real deployments appear at the edges:
an autonomous Jira→draft-PR loop on an OCE cluster, an agent that pays its own inference, an agent benchmarked
against humans weekly, NVIDIA shipping first-party skills.

**Problems:** The support channel carries everything. Purchased OpenAI credits that OpenClaw refuses to
spend during a cooldown; a macOS "credentials need recovery" loop `doctor --fix` cannot clear; updates dying
on a ~1.3 GiB state store; a voice pipeline shipping 14-second silence as speech. The macOS dead-end and the
update blockers were *known* before 2026.11.10 shipped. Moderation is now a third friction channel, after
ClawHub bans and the security-check backlog.

**Interesting:** Capability and adoption are diverging. Three stable releases including a major cut with two new
product surfaces; weekly installs still down 10.7 %; tracker volume ~2× with *severity falling*; clawstat's
gate green on a release that shipped with two P0-class dead-ends still open.

**Trend:** Release velocity broke out of the two-week freeze — three stable versions in 24 h including a major
cut with Deep Research and Knowledge — while demand declined a further 10.7 % WoW and the named-customer ROI
gap reached six consecutive days. Capability is shipping faster than adoption evidence.

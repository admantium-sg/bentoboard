## 2. Security — the blind-SSRF P0 was fixed in-window, on `main` only, and is in NO published artifact

## 13. Community — the only hard cost number in the window: prompt cache is host-affine

Reddit `1xvzh4n` (u/aidenclarke_12): when the route hops host mid-session the cache starts over and the whole prefix is re-billed. Figure cited: a **700k-token Kimi K3 context at ~$2 per call on a cache miss vs ~$0.20 on a hit — a ~10x penalty**. Mitigation: one API key per lab, or route through a host carrying every model (DeepInfra reportedly carries both K3 and DS V4.1 Flash), or go direct to Moonshot + DeepSeek with two keys. Trade-off from the thread: *"a fallback that only kicks in on errors is the cheaper middle ground."* Routing/affinity design is now a **first-class cost lever for long-context agents**, arrived at by users rather than documented by the project. **Caveat: the figures are second-hand ("someone posted") in a 2-comment thread.**

### Cross-Tenant Message Leak Postmortem — One Agent, Two Humans, Three Fixes
PJ_Standley (the Ocala small-business consultant from OpenClaw's Oct 16 showcase) documented ~10 daily messages meant for a client arriving in the consultant's own DM. Three independent causes, three fixes:
1. **Shared session by default** — two people DMing one agent share `session.dmScope: main` (and thus context); background reply pinning only works when `allowFrom` has exactly one entry. Fix: per-peer binding with `"session":{"dmScope":"per-channel-peer"}` (note: replaces the whole `bindings` list; verify with `openclaw agents list --bindings`)
2. **Cron bound to its origin chat** — a cron created with `sessionTarget: current` sends to that chat regardless of `--to`. Fix: `openclaw cron edit <id> --session isolated --announce --channel telegram --to <chat-id>`
3. **The actual culprit** — `tools.exec.notifyOnExit` (default on) wakes a turn for every finished background command even with heartbeat `"0m"`, and the report goes to the first entry in `commands.ownerAllowFrom`. Fix: `openclaw config set tools.exec.notifyOnExit false` (also check `agents.entries.<id>.tools.exec.notifyOnExit`)
- Debugging: `grep "telegram outbound send ok.*chatId=<id>" /tmp/openclaw/openclaw-<date>.log`

Source: https://community.openclaw.ai/t/one-claw-or-a-whole-crew/178

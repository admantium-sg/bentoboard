## Task 1 — Platform news, releases, security incidents, enterprise adoption

### 1. v2026.10.10-beta.4 (2026-10-27 05:39 UTC) — `hooks.token` no longer leaks into child environments
The one genuinely security-relevant fix in the newest prerelease: *"Gateway security: keep an environment-referenced `hooks.token` out of agent host-exec and CLI child environments at startup and across config reloads, while preserving explicit per-command overrides"* (#179199). The reload path matters most — a reloaded config re-seeds the child environment, which is where such leaks usually survive a fix. Same release fixes automations preserving authenticated operator authority for explicit same-conversation scheduled follow-ups (#176422) while keeping revocation boundaries.
Source: https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/releases/tag/v2026.10.10-beta.4

### 11. Use case — the deployment model is the product: ex-pest-control tech sells OpenClaw on-site, then tells clients to fire him

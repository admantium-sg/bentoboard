### Open Model — Qwen 3.8-Max Open Weights: First Max-Class With Custom License, Not Apache (Aug 12)

Qwen 3.8-Max became the first downloadable Max-class Qwen model (Qwen/Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B, BF16 + FP8) but landed under a custom "Qwen3.8-Max License" rather than Apache-2.0/MIT. The downloadable version is text-only with forced thinking — multimodal input and the 1M-token context stay in the API ($2/$6 per M tokens). License terms: keep copyright notice, display model name in UI above 100M MAU or $20M monthly revenue, and obtain a separate paid license for model-as-a-service or AI work-assistant businesses above $50M TTM. Purely internal use is carved out. In contrast, the sibling Qwen3.8-27B (Apache-2.0) is clean with no thresholds. Community: "When 'open' turns from a noun into a contract with thresholds, downloadable does not mean chargeable; the openness a lobster wants is the model that runs on its own machine, not the one bolted to an invoice."
Source: anomixer.github.io/openclaw-news

### Corporate — OpenAI Files Apple Trade-Secrets Injunction Response Today; S-1 Still Absent (Aug 17)

OpenAI's response to Apple's preliminary-injunction motion is due today — it has already filed a 31-page motion to dismiss claiming Apple is suing to compensate for its own difficulty shipping AI features, with hearing set for Oct 1. The public S-1 still has not appeared on SEC EDGAR: confidential draft went in June 8, confidential reviews run 60-90 days, and the prospectus must be public 15 days before a roadshow — keeping the public prospectus in the late-August window and a listing at mid-September earliest. The numbers everyone waits to see: roughly $2B/month in revenue, about $1.22 lost per dollar earned, an $852B valuation from the March round, and the never-disclosed Microsoft revenue-share. Community: "A company burning ~20% more than it earns and two companies accusing each other in court — a lobster's invoice has exactly one line: the hardware you own and the electricity you pay."

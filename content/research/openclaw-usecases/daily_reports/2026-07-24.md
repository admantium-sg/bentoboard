### Model — Poolside Laguna S 2.1: 118B MoE, 8B Active, Beats Nemotron 3 Ultra on Terminal-Bench (July 24)

Poolside released Laguna S 2.1, a 118B-parameter MoE open-weight model with only 8B active parameters per token (~6.8% activation). Scores 70.2% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (with thinking) and 78.5% on SWE-bench Multilingual — topping published leaderboards ahead of DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Max and NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra. Supports 1M context window, ships in BF16/FP8/INT4/NVFP4 weights, and runs on a single DGX Spark. Trained from scratch in under 9 weeks on 4,096 H200 GPUs using RL in FP8 precision. Also launched Laguna XS 2.1 (33B MoE, 3B active). OpenMDW-1.1 license. Community: "118B params but only 8B active — MoE lets Laguna S 2.1 beat 1.6T DeepSeek V4 Pro Max with 6.8% of the parameters. OpenClaw Harness fully supports Poolside models."

## Platform — v2026.10.1-beta.2 Ships Sessions and Memory Overhaul (Oct 8)

OpenClaw released v2026.10.1-beta.2 on October 8, focusing on sessions and memory improvements: preserved usage across registry changes, delivered worker attachments from remote workspaces, prevented queued cancellations and transcript aliases from stalling active turns, kept continuation signatures aligned, and migrated embedding caches in bounded batches with oversized entries. ## Platform — ClawStat Holds ⚠️ on v2026.9.8, Validator Escalation Rejected (Oct 7)

Independent tracker ClawStat re-assessed v2026.9.8 on Oct 7 at 19:44 UTC, maintaining ⚠️ "Update with care" for the fourth consecutive day. The validator attempted to escalate to ⏸️ but the critique rejected it: the escalation "over-escalates" given zero new prevalence evidence — every one of the 60 tracked issues still shows 0 👍 against ~4.5M weekly npm downloads, and the gate itself states none are widespread. The critique also found five severity mis-categorizations (several marked "high" should be "critical") and flagged that #166076 (Windows startup crash) remains absent from the analyst's enumeration. Release v2026.10.1-beta.1 has no verified fixes for any of the 11 credible blockers and itself breaks plugin-sdk exports (#166404). Community: "Zero engagement, eleven blockers, validator overridden — the gate holds ⚠️ and the beta holds doubts."
Source: https://clawstat.us/latest.json (assessed Oct 7, 2026) | https://anomixer.github.io/openclaw-news (Oct 8, 2026)

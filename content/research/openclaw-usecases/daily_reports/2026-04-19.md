### 2. Ring-a-Ding Skill for AI Agent Telephony (NEW)
**Announced:** April 18, 2026
**Launch:** Business Insider coverage

- Outbound AI phone calls for everyday tasks (quotes, appointments, availability checks)
- CLI install, $19/month subscription, bring-your-own OpenAI API key
- Phone number provisioning, SIP connectivity, real-time voice routing, call transcription, summaries
- Dynamic, one-to-one calls with agent-generated conversation context

**Use Cases:**
- Contacting service providers for pricing
- Confirming reservations
- Checking store inventory
- Gathering structured information from businesses by phone
- Legitimate task-based calls only (sales/marketing/robocalling prohibited)

**Roadmap:** SMS messaging and inbound call handling planned

**Significance:** Bridges gap between AI capabilities and real-world tasks that still require phone calls; first dedicated telephony skill for OpenClaw agents.

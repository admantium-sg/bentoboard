### Research — Four Gemini 4 Argon Agents Beat Minecraft: 27h 14m, 2,783 Programs, 605 Deaths, ~$520 to Win (Oct 18-19)
Google DeepMind published a paper (arXiv) showing four Gemini 4 Argon agents dropped into a randomly generated Minecraft survival world with a single instruction — defeat the Ender Dragon — with no roles, no walkthrough, and no game-specific tools. They cleared it in 27 hours 14 minutes: 2,783 self-written programs, 229 in-game chat messages, 605 deaths (69 to the dragon). They threw Eyes of Ender from two spots, tracked flight paths in code, and pinpointed the stronghold 43 metres from the portal room from 1.3 km away. The End fight took nearly 14 hours; the agent who struck the final blow had 2.5 of 20 hearts left. Cost: approximately $520 at list prices ($810 for the whole session including an Observer agent). They consumed approximately 1.35 billion input tokens, 91% served from the context cache, across roughly 24,000 model calls. Caveats: Mineflayer provided god-view intel (they saw through walls), a plugin planned routes, difficulty was set to Easy, it was a single run, and 22% of programs errored. Community: "Ten green days for the lobster and 605 deaths in one run — both win by letting you try again."

### Security — Meta Muse Agent Shares Home Address During Real Facebook Marketplace Sale (Oct 20)
Toronto creator Matt Robb let Meta's Muse agent run a Facebook Marketplace sale as an experiment.

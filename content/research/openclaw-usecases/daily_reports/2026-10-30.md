**#188206** (2026-10-30T09:04Z, open, P1, `impact:security` + `impact:data-loss`) — on **2026.10.10-beta.5 itself**, `openclaw doctor --fix` rewrote a Telegram account's `groupAllowFrom: []` into a list of numeric IDs copied from `allowFrom`, without telling the operator it was a permission change.

# Positive Tipping Points for Environmental Change in 2026

## Overview

Research from the University of Exeter and the Earthshot Prize has identified **51 solutions** that can trigger rapid, transformative environmental change over the next five years. Five priority clusters ("Earthshots") were identified as having the highest leverage for accelerating positive change.

---

## 1. Clean Energy Transition — The "Master Cascade"

**Status: Solar and wind have already crossed positive tipping points in many major markets.**

- In 2025, solar and wind accounted for a record **30% of EU electricity**, overtaking fossil power for the first time
- EU solar generation hit a record **369 TWh** in 2025, up 20% from the previous year
- By 2030, solar power alone is projected to reduce EU emissions by **154 million tonnes of CO2-equivalent per year** — equivalent to the total GHG emissions of the Netherlands in 2023
- Clean power acts as a "master cascade" because it simultaneously unlocks electric mobility, heat pumps, clean industry, and cleaner air
- Research cited in the report suggests coordinated policies could bring forward tipping points in clean power, transport, and heating by **2–8 years**

**Key barriers:** Grid expansion, permitting reform, and a severe investment gap in the Global South. Africa receives less than 2% of global clean energy investment despite possessing 60% of the world's best solar resources.

**Africa's emerging solar manufacturing:**
- Morocco has doubled production to ~1 GW/year
- South Africa maintains similar capacity
- Egypt has gigawatt-scale projects coming online
- "A few years ago, local solar manufacturing was barely on the agenda anywhere in Africa. It is now becoming a recognised pillar of industrial policy." — Benjamin Clarke, Africa Solar Industry Association

**Source:** The Guardian, Sept 10, 2026; Euronews, Sept 12, 2026

## 2. Biodiversity and Ecosystem Tipping Points

### Marine Protected Areas (MPAs)
- Well-designed, well-enforced ocean conservation has produced **visible recovery** of fish stocks and biodiversity
- The **High Seas Treaty** came into force on **January 17, 2026** — a historic milestone enabling a connected network of High Seas MPAs
- The treaty covers international waters that represent more than **two-thirds of the ocean** (~50% of Earth's surface)
- The Great Barrier Reef Marine Park is cited as an example where marine protection has been self-reinforcing

### Community-Owned Fisheries Data Systems
- **ABALOBI** (Earthshot Prize Finalist 2023) has made its MONITOR platform globally accessible, with **50+ partner organisations** signed up
- 2026 expectation: multi-country deployments and deeper alignment with government monitoring systems
- Enables locally-led co-management and regeneration of small-scale fisheries

### Nature-Based Solutions
- Climate adaptation can become self-reinforcing: flood defenses, early-warning systems, and resilient infrastructure lower insurance/borrowing costs, attracting further investment
- The report notes a massive funding imbalance: **~$7.3 trillion/year** flows to activities that degrade nature vs. only **~$220 billion/year** for nature-based solutions

**Source:** Earthshot Prize; Euronews; University of Exeter report

## 3. Energy Transition Tipping Points (Beyond Solar/Wind)

### Electric Vehicles (EVs)
- EVs have crossed tipping points in major markets through the same mechanism as solar/wind: growing deployment → cost reduction → wider adoption → further investment
- EV growth is tightly linked to clean electricity expansion (synergistic cascade effect)

### Heat Pumps
- Part of the same cascade as clean power and EVs
- Coordinated policy support could accelerate the heat pump transition by 2–8 years

### Clean Cooking
- **~2.1 billion people** still rely on solid fuels for cooking (largest source of indoor air pollution)
- Close to a tipping point in several markets as **pay-as-you-go solar and clean cooking businesses scale across East and Southern Africa**
- In Latin America and parts of Asia, the LPG transition is substantially complete

### Methane Abatement
- Methane is responsible for **~30% of warming experienced so far** but stays in the atmosphere only ~12 years
- Methane abatement is identified as "one of the fastest levers available for limiting peak warming"
- Satellites and drones can now expose previously invisible methane leaks, enabling relatively inexpensive interventions

**Source:** The Guardian; Euronews; University of Exeter report

## 4. Supply Chain & Deforestation Gatekeepers

- A small number of companies, banks, and regulators control trade in **beef, soy, palm oil, timber, cocoa, and coffee** — commodities that drive much of the world's tropical deforestation
- The **EU Deforestation Regulation** requires products sold in the EU to be deforestation-free
- If aligned with similar policies from China, UK, and US, this could make deforestation-free products the global norm
- Up to **80% of cleared Amazon forest land** is converted to cattle pasture (University of Manchester, 2026)

**Source:** The Guardian; University of Manchester research cited

## 5. Emerging Positive Tipping Points Highlighted for 2026

### AI-Enabled Sustainable Supply Chains
- Major companies committing to verifiable Scope 3 emissions reductions
- Climate-tech moving from pilots to real deployment across logistics, materials, and circularity
- Faster decarbonization with tangible benefits for frontline communities

### Coastal Resilience Networks
- **Coastal 500** aims to reach 500 local government leaders across the world's most climate-vulnerable coastlines
- Second global exchange planned for 2026 to share solutions and build peer collaboration

### Global Treaties Coming Into Force
- **High Seas Treaty** (January 17, 2026) — legally enforced, enabling marine protected areas beyond national jurisdiction
- **UN Water Conference** in Abu Dhabi — key for international water protection policy

### Food System Innovation
- **Food Shift** initiative launching to find and fuel small companies pioneering alternative ingredients that reduce environmental harm

## Key Framework: Three Prerequisites for Tipping

The report identifies three conditions needed for any solution to tip:

1. **Affordability** — must be cost-competitive
2. **Accessibility** — must be available and deployable at scale
3. **Attractiveness** — must be politically and socially desirable

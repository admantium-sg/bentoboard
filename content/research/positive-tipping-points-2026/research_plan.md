# Research Plan: Positive Tipping Points for Environmental Change 2026

## Main Research Question
What are the key positive tipping points for environmental change in 2026, and how can they be leveraged to accelerate sustainability transitions?

## Subtopics

### 1. Tipping Point Science & Theory
- Understanding social-ecological tipping points
- Network effects and cascade dynamics
- 2026 theoretical advances and frameworks

### 2. Identified Positive Tipping Points in 2026
- Specific environmental tipping points with positive outcomes
- Climate, biodiversity, ocean, and ecosystem examples
- Energy and technology transition tipping points

### 3. Real-World Case Studies
- Documented positive tipping point events in 2024-2026
- Successful intervention examples
- Measurable outcomes and impacts

### 4. Policy & Intervention Strategies
- Leverage points for triggering positive cascades
- Governance frameworks
- 2026 policy developments

## Expected Information
- Key concepts and definitions
- Specific tipping points with evidence
- Case studies with measurable outcomes
- Policy recommendations
- Academic sources (journals, institutions)

## Synthesis Approach
Compile findings into a 3-page academic summary with citations.

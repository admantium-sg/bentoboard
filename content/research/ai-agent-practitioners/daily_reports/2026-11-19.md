Today's radar splits cleanly into one genuinely new theme and a large amount of collector churn, and the theme is measurement and attribution for coding agents. Harrison Chase shipped LangSmith reporting that ties agent spend to the pull requests it produces, with a matching video, a short on why offline and online evals are "hiring a teammate", and a fireside chat with OpenRouter's Alex Atallah; Simon Willison's most interesting post is a quote from curl's Daniel Stenberg describing what happened when agents converted 185,000 lines of C into 340,000 lines of Rust — the agents did the bulk translation, but humans still merged over 300 commits into curl and fixed more than 200 bugs, which is a far better read on real agent autonomy than a benchmark score.

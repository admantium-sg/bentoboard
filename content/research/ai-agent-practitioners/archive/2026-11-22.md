## Summary

Only 7 genuinely new items today, and 6 of them are Harrison Chase, who spent the day turning his "knowledge factory" one-liner into an actual build guide. His new LangChain post argues that the missing piece for enterprise agents is neither better models nor more context but maintained company knowledge, split into three kinds — skills, domain knowledge, and data-access guidance — that differ in how they are stored and how fast they go stale. The most interesting design choice is a separate curator agent running on a schedule to fix the knowledge, because agents that merely *use* it will otherwise let it become unruly and self-contradictory; his X posts push the same idea further, coining "company ontology" and already drawing a practitioner reply describing the same subagent-editing-wiki failure mode from practice.

## Community Pulse

**Working well:** Cline's 5 releases in 6 days (May 20–25) continue the rapid iteration pattern established last week. The concurrent plugin loading and optimistic TUI updates from v3.0.10 are being refined with stability fixes in subsequent releases. Codex's Goal mode going GA is significant — it legitimizes multi-day autonomous coding sessions as a first-class feature rather than an experiment.

**Problems:** MCP registry fragmentation continues — 9,400 published servers across Smithery, Glama, PulseMCP, and Cloudflare AI MCP. Enterprise buyers report difficulty knowing which MCP servers are production-ready vs. hobby projects. The market is starting to address this with curated/paid-tier registries.

**Interesting:** The Q2 report notes model-layer commoditization accelerating faster than pricing models assumed. Three frontier releases in six weeks (GPT-5.5 Pro, Claude Opus 4.7 1M, DeepSeek V4 Preview) with rotating benchmark leadership has shifted procurement conversations from "which model" to "how do we route between models efficiently."

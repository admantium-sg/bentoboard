## Community & Market

### Atlas Browser Shuts Down August 9 — Agentic Browsing Work Moves Into Codex (August 4, 2026)

OpenAI's standalone Atlas browser — launched October 2025, macOS-only throughout its lifespan — is scheduled to stop working **August 9, 2026**. OpenAI frames this as consolidation: the browser-based agentic capabilities (multiple tabs, downloads, improved navigation, account login) are being moved directly into ChatGPT and Codex. The Atlas shutdown announcement arrived alongside the GPT-5.4/5.4-mini retirement notice (effective August 31) and a wave of July Codex features (session naming/pinning, WebSocket remote Code Mode, multi-repo review, Appshots, image editing). The pattern is consistent: OpenAI is retiring a separate product and folding its load-bearing agentic work into the primary Codex surface.

**Source:** https://aitoolsreview.co.uk/insights/openai-codex-august-2026-updates

### Q3 2026 Funding Tracker: July $630M, Agent Governance Is a Real Category (August 1–4, 2026)

Gravity's Q3 tracker (updated August 1, data through July) remains the most current market signal. Key data points:

July closed with 12 disclosed agent rounds worth roughly **$630 million**. Three patterns emerged: picks-and-shovels infrastructure (Prime Intellect at $130M Series A), agent payments as a standalone category (two funded startups building rails so agents can move money without human approval), and agent governance as a distinct investment thesis — Neo Security ($100M, Bessemer + a16z), Act Security ($60M), and Hush Security ($30M) collectively took **$190M in eleven days**, all three selling control over what agents may touch.

Lyzr's $100M Series B is the story worth noting: the company's SivaClaw agent ran the entire fundraise process — fielded questions from 130+ investors, drafted investment memos, tracked which slides investors lingered on — without founders taking traditional in-person meetings. The round closed at ~$500M valuation. Whether the agent-as-GC framing is marketing or substance, the workflow outcome (a real, multi-week, high-stakes process run to completion) is a data point.

Context: global VC funding hit a record $510B in H1 2026 ($305B Q1, $205B Q2), already ahead of 2025's $440B full-year total. More than 70% of Q2 capital went to AI. OpenAI and Anthropic absorbed 43% of all H1 funding. The concentration at the model layer is extreme.

## Community & Market

Agentic AI enterprise adoption crossed a real threshold: Gartner reports 80% of enterprise applications shipped or updated in Q1 2026 now embed at least one AI agent (up from 33% in 2024, 58% in 2025). Yet S&P Global Market Intelligence puts actual production deployments at only 31% — the ~49-point gap between embedding and operating is where 2026 enterprise budgets are concentrated.

22% of enterprises now use multi-agent (3+) orchestration (up from 6% in 2025). Median payback period: 5.1 months. $1.4 trillion global spend forecast for 2027. The adoption curve is steeper than cloud computing in 2010-2012, driven by tool-use reliability reaching production grade, MCP standardisation enabling agent-to-enterprise-data connectivity, and institutional learning from abandoned pilots.

# Daily Report — Agentic CLI Tools Research
**Date:** October 13, 2026
**Research Area:** Agentic CLI tools and desktop AI assistants

## New Findings (October 12–13, 2026)

### OpenAI Codex CLI v0.162.1 — TUI Crash Fix, Startup Compatibility (October 9, 2026)
106th release. Fixes a TUI crash when asynchronous questions contain multiple lines — line breaks and complete hyperlink destinations are now preserved. Also fixes startup failures caused by feature-setting differences between a running background server and CLI defaults; compatibility checks now apply only to explicit command-line feature overrides rather than all settings mismatches. Ships every 1.1 days — 16 releases in the last month.

**Source:** https://releases.sh/openai/codex

### Manus AI Raises $500M+ as Independent Company After Meta Deal Collapses (October 8, 2026)
Manus AI, the Chinese AI agent lab founded by ex-Meta employees, has raised over $500 million in its first funding round since Meta was forced to abandon its acquisition (reported at over $2 billion) in late 2025 due to regulatory pressure. Manus's parent company Butterfly Effect announced the round via WeChat. The company relocated its headquarters to Singapore and closed its Beijing office. According to The Information, Manus had an annual revenue run rate of approximately $400–500 million by June 2026, up from roughly $100 million at the time of the original Meta deal announcement. Manus operates a general-purpose AI agent platform positioning itself as an alternative to Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google.

**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/chinas-manus-raises-over-500m-in-first-funding-round-since-split-with-meta/

### Armadin Raises $255.5M Series B — Agent Security Reaches Growth Stage (October 1, 2026)
Armadin closed a $255.5 million Series B at a valuation exceeding $2.5 billion, led by Andreessen Horowitz and Accel, with participation from Bain Capital Ventures, Redpoint, 8VC, Ballistic Ventures, GV, In-Q-Tel, Kleiner Perkins, and Menlo Ventures. Armadin builds offensive security tooling that chains individual security weaknesses into actionable attack paths, enabling defenders to test agentic AI systems. Founded by one of Mandiant's founders. This round follows Reco's $55 million growth round (September 29) in the same agent security space, making agent security a confirmed funded category with a distinct investor coalition.

**Source:** https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/armadin-raises-255-5-million-series-b-to-scale-effective-autonomous-security-302895278.html

### Nous Research Raises $90M Series B for Open-Source Agent Technology (October 7, 2026)
Nous Research, maker of the open-source Hermes AI models and assistant, raised $90 million Series B led by Robot Ventures with participation from Nvidia, M12, Samsung, USV, and Menlo Ventures. The round supports development of open-source AI models and agent technology, positioning Nous as a significant open-source alternative in the agent space alongside offerings from Hugging Face and EleutherAI.

**Source:** https://www.wsj.com/pro/venture-capital/maker-of-open-source-ai-assistant-hermes-secures-90-million-a061c1d7

### Q4 2026 Funding Opens at $260M Across Two Rounds — Q3 Closes at ~$6.32B (October 2026)
Gravity Fast's Q4 tracker shows two disclosed rounds totaling $260 million as of October 5, 2026. Q3 2026's final close is 59 rounds at approximately $6.32 billion, up from the $5.25 billion preliminary figure. Instinct's $1 billion Series C (August 2026) accounts for much of the upward revision. Q4 pace is running at 0.40 rounds per day versus Q3's 0.64 rounds per day — slower but still substantial. Median Q3 cheque was $30 million. Notable: agent security (Armadin + Reco, consecutive weeks) now reads as its own funded category.

**Source:** https://gravity.fast/data/ai-agent-funding-tracker/

### Vesta Raises $30M to Bring AI Agents to Mortgage Lending (October 8, 2026)
Vesta raised $30 million to support adoption of AI agents by mortgage lenders. The round was led by Conversion Capital. Vesta is building domain-specific agents for the mortgage workflow — an example of the trend toward verticalized agent deployments rather than general-purpose tooling, as covered in the Oct 12 report's Q3 enterprise data.

**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/vesta-raises-30m-as-lenders-adopt-ai-agents/

### Zeroset Raises $5.2M Pre-Seed for Agent Memory Infrastructure (October 6, 2026)
Zeroset raised $5.2 million pre-seed led by Gradient Ventures and 2048 Ventures to develop memory infrastructure for AI agents — tooling to enable agents to persist, retrieve, and reason over long-term context across sessions. This is the first dedicated agent-memory infrastructure round tracked since the category emerged, and it arrives as Kiro CLI (October 9) ships session memory as a native feature. The infrastructure vs. application layer dynamic is now clearly forming in the funding landscape.

**Source:** https://www.businessinsider.com/zeroset-startup-funding-ai-agents-gradient-ventures-enterprise-2026-10

### Salesforce Acquires Listen Labs — Enterprise Agents Move to Customer Research (September 29, 2026)
Salesforce signed a definitive agreement to acquire Listen Labs, an AI customer research platform whose agents design studies, interview participants, and synthesize findings. Expected to close in Salesforce's fiscal Q4 2027. Terms undisclosed; MarTech reports a price of approximately $2 billion. This is the second major enterprise acquisition of an agent platform in a week (following ZoomInfo/DoubleO.ai), and it places AI research agents directly inside Salesforce's CRM workflow — a significant enterprise agent deployment signal.

**Source:** https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-listen-labs/

## Community Pulse

**Working well:** Kiro's session memory (v2.29.0, October 9) is now joined by a dedicated startup — Zeroset's $5.2M pre-seed for agent memory infrastructure — confirming the market recognizes memory as a distinct capability worth building and funding separately. The coincidence of a CLI tool shipping a feature and an infrastructure startup raising money around it in the same week is a healthy signal of the application layer and infrastructure layer developing in parallel.

**Problems:** The Manus AI $500M+ raise is notable, but it comes with a caveat: this is the company's first external funding since the collapsed Meta acquisition at a reported $2B+. The valuation and investor details of this new round are unconfirmed, and the company's reported ARR of $400-500M needs context — a large revenue run rate doesn't necessarily mean profitable or sustainable unit economics.

**Interesting:** Two enterprise agent acquisitions in one week (Listen Labs → Salesforce, DoubleO.ai → ZoomInfo) place agent platforms directly inside major SaaS/enterprise vendors. This accelerates the "agentic enterprise stack" beyond what the Oct 12 report characterized as a hyperscaler battleground — now it's SaaS vendors too, integrating agents as native workflow components in CRM, customer research, and go-to-market automation.

## Trend

The October 8-13 window shifts focus from CLI release cadence to the surrounding ecosystem: agent-specific funding rounds are now appearing alongside the general AI infrastructure wave (Manus at $500M+, Armadin at $255.5M, Nous at $90M, Vesta at $30M, Zeroset at $5.2M). Q4's pace is running slower than Q3 in absolute terms, but the deals are concentrated in well-defined categories — security, memory infrastructure, vertical mortgage agents, open-source — rather than undifferentiated "general agent" bets. Enterprise agent deployment is moving from pilots to acquisitions: Salesforce and ZoomInfo buying agent platforms places agents inside enterprise workflows as first-class components, not add-ons.

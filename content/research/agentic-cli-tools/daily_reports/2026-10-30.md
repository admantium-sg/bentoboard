## Executive Summary

The quiet-release streak broke: **eight tools shipped substantial releases in 24 hours**, and two of them were version milestones — Factory's Droid CLI jumped 0.251.0 → **1.0.0**, and Codex CLI promoted 0.166.0 to stable. The bigger story was the *shape* of the competition. Factory made **programmatic tool calling the default** and published cost/accuracy numbers against Codex and Claude Code on identical models. OpenCode shipped `/goal`, a loop where the session continues until a **reviewer confirms the objective was met**. Crush v0.99.0 shipped full lifecycle hooks and Catwalk v3. In other words: **the harness, not the model, is where vendors are now differentiating.** Meanwhile the loudest user-facing complaint was **quota** — Anthropic's post-IPO limit tightening, ~10 Claude Code rate-limit issues in two days, a Codex meter that drains "without use" that OpenAI Support cannot explain, and Gemini CLI surfacing 429 countdowns in its loading indicator. On the business side, **Google retired Jules into Antigravity** (shut down Dec 31, 2026) — the second vendor consolidation in two days — while neocloud capacity was priced for a shakeout and Democrats pressed Anthropic ahead of its IPO.

## 5. Trend

**The harness is now the product, and quota is the constraint.** Factory's Droid 1.0 makes programmatic tool calling the default and claims 24% lower cost and +3.5 points on Terminal-Bench against Codex on the *same* model; OpenCode ships `/goal`, a reviewer-confirmed completion loop; Crush ships full lifecycle hooks; Codex ships transcript navigation, `doctor --network-only` and resume fidelity. Against that, the loudest user signal is quota — Anthropic's post-IPO limit tightening, ~10 Claude Code rate-limit issues in two days, a 2.4B-token multi-agent run that was 98% cache reads, and Codex meters that drain "without use" with no vendor explanation. The counter-move is efficiency engineering, which makes **safety false positives a cost problem too**: a blocked QEMU debug or blocked CI-log analysis burns the user's window without producing work. Google retired Jules into Antigravity, Anthropic filed an S-1 under political pressure, and neocloud capacity is being priced for consolidation — the market is repricing both the vendor's margin and the substrate it runs on.

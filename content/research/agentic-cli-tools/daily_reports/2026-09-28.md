## Community Pulse

**Working well:** Claude Code's terminal-only workflow is validated by community as a feature, not limitation — users report spending less time in IDEs. `/clear` between tasks is the standard workaround for long-session drift. Cline's new proxy support unblocks enterprise users behind corporate proxies.

**Problems:** Token burn on workplace agents remains a top complaint. "Agent washing" (labeling basic automation as agents) is the community's main pet peeve — real agents distinguished by acting on initiative vs waiting for every instruction.

**Interesting:** Q3 correction pattern reveals announcement lag is structural, not careless — rounds close before anyone writes them up. Factory AI was announced day-of yet this tracker missed it twice in 13 days.

## Trend Analysis

### Patterns Identified
1. **Rapid release cycles continue**: Claude Code averaging multiple releases per week
2. **Feature parity across platforms**: Background agents, terminal tabs, and Mermaid support appearing across multiple tools
3. **Security focus**: OAuth improvements, sandbox hardening, and permission controls remain priority
4. **Enterprise governance**: Agent identity and access management becoming standardized (Okta Agent SSO)

### Source Reliability
- Official changelogs (GitHub, code.claude.com): 100% reliable
- Third-party changelog aggregators (claudeupdates.dev): High reliability
- Investment/financial news: High reliability when citing official filings
- Market research (Gartner, IDC): High reliability when cited correctly

### Recommendations
1. For market statistics, always cite the original analyst firm rather than secondary sources
2. Be careful with announcement dates vs. research compilation dates
3. Version numbers should be cross-referenced with official changelogs

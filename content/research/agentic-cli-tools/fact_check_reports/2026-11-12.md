## 3. Corrections made

### Correction 1 — the pre-IPO Anthropic derivatives market is bigger than reported
**Claim:** "Three separate ETF complexes filed single-stock leveraged Anthropic products while the IPO is still pending (ProShares 2026-11-05, GraniteShares 2026-11-05, Defiance 2026-11-03)."
**Issue:** Undercount, plus wrong framing of the filing dates.
**Correction:** The named filings, tickers, objectives and accession numbers are all confirmed, and "registrations, not launches" is correct — both ProShares and GraniteShares state "Anthropic has not yet commenced its initial public offering", and GraniteShares still shows AIL/ANS as "LAUNCHING SOON". But (a) at least **four further complexes** filed Anthropic leveraged products: Themes ETF Trust (485BPOS 2026-10-27), Tidal Trust II (485BPOS 2026-11-06), ETF Opportunities Trust (T-Rex 2x Long Anthropic, 485BXT) and Direxion Shares ETF Trust; (b) the 2026-11-05 dates are **post-effective amendments, not first filings** — ProShares' first 485BPOS was 2026-10-22, GraniteShares announced its filing on 2026-09-03, and the ProShares registrations took effect 2026-11-06.
**Sources:** https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0002130357&type=S-1 · https://graniteshares.com/anthropic-leveraged-etfs/

### Correction 2 — Anthropic's research metric was mislabelled as an "AI contribution"
**Claim:** "the top 20% of tasks receive 2–4x the AI contribution."
**Issue:** Wrong metric name. The post's measure is usage vs. time-share, not a contribution estimate.
**Correction:** The post says the top 20% of tasks "get two to four times as much Claude **use** as the time researchers spend on them would predict". All other figures confirmed: sample of **207,049** sessions (128,914 Claude + 78,135 Claude Code, Aug 2 – Sep 30 2026); data analysis 33.7% of Claude and 48.1% of Claude Code sessions; ideation 4.2% / 2.1% against 7.1% of researcher time; ideation ≈2x its share on Claude Science.
**Sources:** https://www.anthropic.com/research/how-researchers-use-claude · https://daily.dev/posts/ai-for-scientific-discovery-how-researchers-use-claude-srssvle2p

### Correction 3 — the "OpenAI acquires Merge" mis-framing could not be confirmed

**Issue:** The mis-framing is likely a misattribution of separate "SpaceX and xAI Merge" headlines, not established coverage.

## 6. Trend analysis

- **SEC-filed documents are the highest-reliability claim class in the corpus — by a wide margin.** 8 of 8 filing-derived claims (S-1/A financials, listing structure, loss lines, ETF registrations) verified against the primary document, with only framing nuances rather than factual errors. Contrast with the 3 of 15 claims that needed qualification, all of which came from *secondary* framing: a count of issuers, a metric label, and an attribution of a mis-framed flash post.
- **"N issuers did X" claims undercount systematically.** The ETF cluster was the only count claim checked this run and it was wrong (3 → at least 7 complexes). This is a recurring failure mode of corpus-sweep collection: the sweep found what it saw in the window, not the field.
- **Metric labels drift between a source and its restatement.** "Two to four times as much Claude use as the time researchers spend would predict" became "2–4x the AI contribution". The direction of the number survived; the quantity being measured did not. Any claim quoting a vendor's *constructed* index needs the index definition re-read, not just the number.
- **Attribution of a mis-framing is itself a claim.** The corpus recorded "outlets mis-framed this as an acquisition" — that is a factual claim about the press, and it did not survive contact with the press. Third-party mis-framings should be logged as *unverified* unless a specific outlet is named.
- **Zero factual contradictions.** Verdict mix: 12 verified, 3 partially verified, 0 corrections, 0 unverified — the second consecutive clean run (2026-11-05: 12/3/0/0).
- **Operational note:** the three subagents this run all wrote their result files on the first attempt, and the exact absolute output path was given at the top of each prompt (a fix for the 2026-11-05 run, where one agent exhausted context before writing and one wrote to the wrong directory). Keep that instruction.

## 5. Corrections (2)

### 5.1 Claude Duet — `fact_315` → `needs_correction`
Both cited sources were retrieved successfully (TechCrunch HTTP 200 on the 2026-10-22 article; AI Tools Review page).

**Correction 1 — unsupported parenthetical.** The claim *"Meta's Muse (10M+ users, shopping a top activity)"* is **not supported by either source**. TechCrunch says only: *"While Meta's Muse is a personal AI assistant, the company also released a version to cater to small businesses with connections to other apps"* — there is no user-count figure and no mention of shopping anywhere in the article. AI Tools Review names Meta's Muse as the competitor being answered but likewise carries neither the "10M+ users" nor the "shopping" claim. The parenthetical must be removed or separately sourced.

**Correction 2 — platform availability over-stated and self-contradictory.** TechCrunch: Claude Duet "is available as an app on **iOS and Android**, and on the **web** through Claude.ai for **Pro and Max** customers in research preview", with a **waitlist** — with **no US restriction**. AI Tools Review: the preview "is **US-only** and waitlisted" and, explicitly, "**Treat anything beyond web and iOS as unconfirmed**" — i.e. it treats **Android as unconfirmed**, the opposite of TechCrunch. Safe wording: *web and iOS confirmed; TechCrunch lists Android, a second source treats Android as unconfirmed; the preview is reported as US-only for Pro and Max subscribers on a waitlist.*

**Everything else confirmed.** TechCrunch verbatim: built on **Claude Opus 5.5**; "over **6,000** connectors and plug-ins in the Claude Marketplace"; purchases via "**Stripe's Link digital wallet or Shopify's Shop Pay**"; "sensitive steps like payments would require user approval"; "**Duet won't trade securities or move money between accounts**"; a **new classifier** checks each message and "by default, the agent would turn down requests asking for **credit card numbers and government identification**"; Duet "**isn't good at handling errands or booking tickets**" and "**can't show you its browser use as it happens**"; rivals named are **Meta Muse, OpenAI Dots, Hark, Instinct, Grok Bot and Fo**. AI Tools Review verbatim: "sends a **morning brief**, checks your connected apps **every few hours**", runs "**in its own cloud virtual machine**" with "**its own browser**", and is "**limited to US Pro and Max subscribers on a waitlist**" (launched **21 October 2026**). Two omissions recorded: the entry **omits Grok Bot** among rivals, and the morning-brief / re-check-interval / own-browser / US-only details rest on **AI Tools Review alone** (TechCrunch says "daily briefing" and "background virtual machine" without the browser or the interval).

### 5.2 October 2026 funding roundup — `fact_316` → `needs_correction`
**Correction — stale snapshot.** The entry's headline figures *"updated 2026-10-22, records 72 rounds totalling $41.9B"* are out of date and not reproducible:
- The live roundup is **updated 2026-10-26** and reports **77 rounds / $42.5B**.
- The page contradicts itself: the share blurb says "**$42.5B+ raised across 76 deals**" while the body says **77 rounds**.
- Rebuilding an equivalent snapshot from the source's own data API for rounds dated **on or before 2026-10-22** yields **71 rounds / $42.23B** — so 72 rounds / $41.9B cannot be reconstructed from the current dataset either. Wayback was rate-limited, so the original snapshot could not be recovered.

**All other figures confirmed** against the live page and the underlying data: **Foundation Models & AGI $36.0B across 3 rounds**; **AI Fintech $1.94B across 9 rounds** (stated as $1.9B); **AI Developer Tools $1.13B across 5 rounds** (stated as $1.1B across 5 rounds) — confirmed from the sector aggregation even though it no longer appears in the page's three-item Key Takeaways list; **median dollar round $18.3M** (stated $18M); **disclosed stages 10 Series A, 10 Seed, 5 Pre-Seed** (exact match); largest round **OpenAI $20.0B, stage undisclosed, Oct 1**; **doubleAI $200M Series A, Oct 19, $1.0B valuation, backed by Nvidia and Dell** (confirmed on the doubleAI company page and in the data record); and the **TypeSafe AI internal inconsistency** is real and still present — the entry text says "TypeSafe AI's **$40 million** seed round was led by DCVC" while the adjacent table row shows **$870M at a $7.5B valuation** (data record: amount $870M, valuation $7.5B, Oct 9), so that figure correctly remains flagged as unverified.

## 3. Recurring error patterns

1. **Ordinal/cadence metadata is unreliable.** "Nth release of 2026" and "ships every X days — N releases" are corpus-side embellishments, not vendor facts. Four entries carried contradictory ordinals (Claude Code v2.1.305, Codex rust-v0.163.0, two different cadence figures in adjacent entries). *Recommendation: strip ordinals and cadence lines, or maintain a single counter script.*
2. **Second-hand statistics lose their source.** Every survey/adoption/ROI number in the Oct-20/21 entries failed or partially failed against its cited source (65.5/58.7/29.5%, 31%, 540%, BCG). The underlying *events* (Hone $60M, Stuut $52.5M, 78% projection) are real. *Recommendation: attach the publisher's own URL to every statistic, and label projections as projections.*
3. **Exact identifiers drift.** `claudecode.safetyevent` → `claude_code.safety_event`; `rootconversationid` → `root_conversation_id`; "Kiro CLI" → "Kiro Crew"; "lunching" typo. These are cheap to fix and cheap to re-break — *always quote identifiers verbatim from release notes.*
4. **Feature-to-release attribution drifts.** Gemini CLI, Kilo and Antigravity entries each attributed at least one feature to the wrong version. *Recommendation: check the release where the change actually shipped, not the release discussed in the same week.*
5. **Issue chronology drifts.** A two-month-old issue (#87850, Aug 19) was presented as a current October occurrence.
6. **Latest-version pointers go stale within hours.** Hermes v0.22.3 was "latest" for ~6 hours on Oct 21 before v0.22.4 shipped the same day.

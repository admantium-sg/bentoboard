## Quantization Developments

Log_bQuant (2607.01127) introduces non-uniform logarithmic quantization — notable because it targets the heavy-tail problem in weight distributions that uniform quantizers handle poorly. 4-bit precision gains over asymmetric linear quantization with memory savings for consumer GPUs.

More broadly: 4-bit/2-bit quantization, LoRA advancements, and model pruning remain the primary paths to running large models on consumer hardware. The field continues to shift from compute-bound to memory-bound bottlenecks as optimization techniques improve.

Key trend: NF4, GPTQ, AWQ, and GGUF formats are converging on best practices; the next frontier is adapting quantization to specific weight distributions rather than uniform codebooks.

**Correction:** Keep the AA Index v5 composition and all cost-per-task changes (Opus 5.5 max $5.98/M → $10.04/M, +68%; GPT-6.1 Terra max $0.40/M → $0.96, +138%; Gemini 4 Argon high +137%; Sonnet 5.5 max +83% — all confirmed). Drop or re-source the BenchLeader index-score drops.

## Trend Analysis
- **Source reliability was very high.** 12 of 15 claims were arXiv paper claims and every one matched its abstract verbatim on all cited figures. The failure mode this run did **not** reproduce is the arXiv-ID mislabel seen in earlier runs (fact_145 BitsMoE) — all 12 IDs resolved to the correct paper.
- **Corrections clustered in live leaderboard snapshots, not in papers.** The single partially verified claim (BenchLeader index drops) is a snapshot-vs-rebaseline pair of numbers that the site no longer exposes. Live leaderboard figures remain the weakest link in this knowledge base: 9 of 22 historical corrections are currency-drift on exactly this class of claim.
- **Vendor-reported benchmarks cannot be independently reproduced.** Mistral's FrontierCode 2.0 / Terminal-Bench numbers and the AA index figures are confirmed *as reported* by the vendor, with no independent audit available (FrontierCode 2.0 is Cognition-private).
- **Independent-domain second sources were reliable.** alphaxiv.org, papers.cool, arxiv.deeppaper.ai, huggingface.co and testingcatalog.com all reproduced the arXiv abstracts faithfully and surfaced extra detail (e.g. DCKV's 205–221 bytes per compressed token vs 12,288 for bf16; BiPA's 1.16× token ratio and 0.02 dev→test slope).
- **Caveats worth carrying forward:** the harness paper (2610.37255) rests on a single Qwen3.5-2B checkpoint; BiPA's "0.1 points" is an average that hides 4.2–6.4 pt gains on one benchmark; the memory paper's ranges span models, not just configurations.

## 6. Recommendations

1. **BenchLM tied-score handling:** When models are tied (e.g., #6 Muse Spark 1.3 and #7 MiniMax M3 both at 79.4), document should note both and clarify the tie-breaking criteria used by BenchLM.

2. **Anthropic "top 3" claim:** Avoid claiming top-3 for a provider unless all three positions genuinely belong to them. Use "top 3 providers" or "top Anthropic model at #2" instead.

3. **Leaderboard snapshot dating:** HuggingFace trending is a real-time snapshot. Consider noting "as of time of writing" for download counts since they change hourly.

4. **Tied ranking verification:** The BenchLM page uses tied rankings with secondary sort criteria. When entering rankings, verify the full ordered list rather than assuming sequential ordering.

## Error Patterns Identified

1. **Score Decay:** Benchmark scores claimed on Sept 5 are already outdated by Sept 10 - leaderboards change rapidly
2. **Win Rate Inflation:** The "100% win rate" claim appears to be either fabricated or from a different metric - actual FMRT data shows 63%
3. **Model Version Lag:** Claims reference older model versions (Muse Spark 1.1/1.2) when newer versions (1.3) are available

---

## Recommendations

1. **For benchmark scores:** Add timestamp of data collection and note that scores change frequently
2. **For win rates:** Always verify against primary source; 100% win rates are statistically improbable in competitive tournaments
3. **For model versions:** Always specify exact model variant and version
4. **For download counts:** These are volatile - add "as of [date]" timestamps

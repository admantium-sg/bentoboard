## Root-Cause Pattern: Leaderboard Counts and Card Metadata Are Perishable

Three of the five largest corrections came from one failure mode: **capturing a moving number and presenting it as a stable fact.** FMRT rankings, Bradley-Terry ratings, HF like/trending counters and HN point counts all change within hours, yet the document stores them as inline figures with no as-of stamp.

**Recommendations for the next writer:**
1. Fetch `https://fmrt.ai/api/leaderboard.json` at write time and derive every count from that single response — never mix snapshots across days.
2. Treat like/trending/point counters as explicitly time-stamped ("as of 20:00 UTC"), or omit them; they will always drift.
3. When a claim comes from a model card, quote the card and link it. The "flint has no benchmark table" error existed because the card was never opened — the benchmark table was there the whole time.
4. When a post is being re-reported (Mistral Large 4 on Oct 21, Contra Collective on Aug 22), record the **original publication date**, not the discussion date.
5. Reddit cannot be reached from this host (HTTP 403). Either cite an alternative source or mark the claim `UNCONFIRMABLE` — do not present a single-thread Reddit quote as verified.

## Root Cause Analysis: FMRT Errors

The October 15 FMRT entry mixes three data sources:

1. **Live FMRT API data** (partial) — correct for some BT values and win rates
2. **Stale October 12/13 FMRT data** — Gemma 4 31B #1 claim appears to be from an early-October snapshot
3. **Confusion between Fable 5 and Fable 5.1** — these are different model versions with different BT ratings

**Pattern:** When capturing "live" benchmark data, the system must re-fetch the live leaderboard at time of writing. Using cached data from different dates introduces compounding errors.

---

## Recommendations

1. **For FMRT captures:** Always fetch `https://fmrt.ai/api/leaderboard.json` directly — do not cache or mix with historical snapshots
2. **For leaderboard claims:** Cross-check rank #1 against the actual live leaderboard before publishing
3. **For model version confusion:** Fable 5 vs Fable 5.1 are distinct models — treat them separately

## Papers Verified (June 25 Batch)

### Voice AI Emotional Intelligence Gap
**arXiv:2606.26083** - "Real-Time Voice AI Hears but Does Not Listen"
- ✅ Four realtime voice systems tested on word/vocal delivery conflict
- ✅ All four act on words, not voice
- ✅ "Emotional intelligence gap" term verified
- ✅ Prompting improves partially but inconsistently
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26083, https://arxivtldr.org/abs/2606.26083

### Reclaim Evaluation: Lossy Memory
**arXiv:2606.25449** - "Reclaim Evaluation: A Lossy Memory Is Worse Than an Empty One"
- ✅ "Brittle memory" phenomenon verified
- ✅ Source-first policy to optimize memory management
- ✅ Reclaim evaluation framework for judge-free assessment
- ✅ GitHub repository exists: collapseindex/reclaim-eval
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25449, https://github.com/collapseindex/reclaim-eval

### PolicyAlign: Direct Policy-Based Safety Alignment
**arXiv:2606.25442**
- ✅ Synthesizes policy-violating instructions + on-policy self-distillation
- ✅ Policy-Sensitive Filtering selects instructions where policy induces behavioral shift
- ✅ Improves safety while maintaining low over-refusal
- ✅ Generalizes to medical, legal, financial safety scenarios
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25442, https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CL/2026-06

### Multi-Step Tool-Use RL Collapse
**arXiv:2606.26027** - "Why Multi-Step Tool-Use Reinforcement Learning Collapses"
- ✅ RL alone leads to instability/catastrophic collapse
- ✅ Control token probability spikes observed
- ✅ Supervisory signals (especially interleaved SFT) improve stability
- ✅ Diverse supervisory signals enable robust training
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26027, https://arxivtldr.org/abs/2606.26027

### Brevity is the Soul of Inference Efficiency
**arXiv:2606.25432**
- ✅ Output length has been inflating (standard toolkit treats token count as fixed)
- ✅ Brevity via pretraining data curation
- ✅ 35x Cost-of-Pass advantage mentioned
- ✅ Concise model reaches correct answers verbose reasoning model misses
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25432, https://digg.com/tech/71sdnkxn

### Jailbreak Judge Reliability
**arXiv:2606.25487** - "How Reliable Is Your Jailbreak Judge?"
- ✅ 596 human-labeled HarmBench completions used
- ✅ Dedicated classifier: precision 0.835, recall 0.974 (over-flags)
- ✅ LLM-as-judge: precision 0.81-0.94, recall 0.06-0.65 (erratic)
- ✅ Surface wrappers flip every LLM-judge 57-100%
- ✅ White-box GCG flips 70% of true positives
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25487

### Detect, Unlearn, Restore
**arXiv:2606.26036** - "Defending Text Summarization Models Against Data Poisoning"
- ✅ White-box: influence-function analysis with semantic consistency checks (85-92% detection precision)
- ✅ Black-box: perturbed models show 2-3x greater sensitivity
- ✅ Gradient-ascent unlearning restores 96% of original behavior
- ✅ Less than 0.6% ROUGE degradation
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26036

### SpeechEQ
**arXiv:2606.25990** - "Benchmarking Emotional Intelligence Quotient in Socially Aware Voice Conversational Models"
- ✅ 2,265 dialogues across 15 EQ subscales (EQ-i 2.0 theory)
- ✅ Spoken EQ (SEQ) score evaluation protocol
- ✅ Bottlenecks: modality shortcut, alignment-induced safety trap, contextual amnesia
- ✅ End-to-end outperforms cascaded but still falls short
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25990, https://huggingface.co/datasets/SpeechEQ/SpeechEQ

## Papers Verified (June 20 Batch)

### GEMS: Multi-Semantic Steering Without Collapse
**arXiv:2606.19946** - "Geometric Constraints Enable Multi-Semantic Superposition in LLMs"
- ✅ Training-free activation steering for single directions collapses with multiple
- ✅ Two collapse sources: distributional deviation and directional interference
- ✅ GEMS: norm-preserving weighted superposition + real-time orthogonalization
- ✅ GSM8K: 98% accuracy with 3 concurrent directions (baseline 92%, unconstrained 4%)
- ✅ Wikitext-2: only 2.2% PPL increase
- ✅ Qualitative transfer from 3B to 31B across architectures
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19946, https://github.com/LuLu663939/gems-multi-semantic-steering

### H-RePlan: Hierarchical Recovery for Cross-Device Agents
**arXiv:2606.20487** - "Beyond Global Replanning: Hierarchical Recovery for Cross-Device Agent Systems"
- ✅ Multi-device agents fail at recovery (retry same strategy, reassign, revise plan)
- ✅ H-RePlan separates device-local strategy recovery from orchestrator-level global replanning
- ✅ HeraBench: fault-injected benchmark for Linux and Android
- ✅ Higher completion, instruction adherence, perfect-pass rates at lower token cost
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20487

### StylisticBias
**arXiv:2606.20527** - "A Few Human Visual Cues Drive Most Social Biases in MLLMs"
- ✅ 500 photorealistic base faces × ~50 variations = ~25K images
- ✅ Age and body type dominate identity-level effects
- ✅ Fashion style drives largest attribute-level shifts
- ✅ ~15 attributes account for nearly 80% of total variation
- ✅ Accepted to ICML 2026 workshops (AI4Good and Culture x AI)
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20527, https://github.com/timo-cavelius/StylisticBias

### NarraDolma
**arXiv:2606.19468** - "Characterizing Narrative Content in Web-scale LLM Pretraining Data"
- ✅ 3-trillion-token Dolma corpus studied
- ✅ Three narrative elements: agency, setting, events (11 interpretable dimensions)
- ✅ NarraBERT for fine-grained narrative prediction on 3M passages
- ✅ Narrative structure is measurable at scale
- ✅ Narrative qualities unequally distributed across sources/topics
- **Sources:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19468

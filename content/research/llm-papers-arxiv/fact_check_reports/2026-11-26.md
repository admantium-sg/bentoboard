### C29 — Gemini 4 Carbon's BenchLeader rank (#9 at 65.1) is not supported by any retrievable snapshot

- **Claim as written:** BenchLeader **#9 (65.1)**, behind Opus 5.5 (69.1), with **28 configurations inside the leader's margin of error**.
- **What the sources show:** the closest retrievable BenchLeader snapshot (2026-11-03) has Carbon at **"#2 of 586"** with index **"69.0 ±3.2"** and **"17 other configurations score within its margin"**, behind leader **Claude Opus 5.5 (max) "at 69.1"**. Live (26 Nov) has Carbon **#2 at 69.4** with **"11 configurations sit within Claude Fable 5.2's margin of error"**.
- **Verdict:** the "#9 (65.1)" and "28 configurations" figures cannot be reproduced and contradict the closest available evidence on both rank and margin count. The BenchLM half of the same claim is exact.
- **Secondary note:** the claim says the 90% interval spans "roughly ranks 1–12"; the interval itself spans roughly the top 10 scored models.

## Patterns identified

- **The 15-claim cap remains bound on the wrong claims.** Six cheap, non-decaying arXiv claims consumed 40% of the budget; the five community/leaderboard claims — the ones that actually rot — consumed the rest. The 2026-11-19 recommendation to run community claims on a shorter rotation has now been made twice; it should be acted on.
- **Wayback is the decisive tool for leaderboard claims, and it is not uniformly available.** BenchLM and BenchLeader both archived cleanly; fmrt.ai archived nothing. Before accepting an unverifiable leaderboard snapshot, check the Wayback CDX API first — that single call decides VERIFIED vs UNVERIFIABLE.
- **A superlative is not a fact.** "Fastest turnaround observed" (C22) can never be verified against a moving leaderboard. Recommendation: superlatives should be rewritten as dated observations at the source.
- **"Still gated" style statements expire in a way numbers do not.** Numeric counters drift gradually and are flagged as such; a discrete availability state flips silently and creates a false statement later. Both need flagging, but the state flip needs it more urgently — C28's "still gated" was true when written and false four weeks later, with no signal that it had become stale.
- **Anchor-based insertion is safer than line-number insertion.** The first two apply passes used line numbers from an earlier file state and produced duplicated and misplaced comment blocks (a second pass landed 14 blocks at shifted offsets). Reverting and re-applying with content anchors, scoped to the section range, produced 15/15 correct placements. Future runs should anchor on a distinctive substring of the claim line.

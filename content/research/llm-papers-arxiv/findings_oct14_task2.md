## Efficient LLM Reasoning: Survey of Token Cost Reduction Methods
**Survey by Danilchenko (Aug 2026)**
- Sketch-of-Thought (SoT): up to 84% token reduction with cognitive-inspired shorthand (EMNLP 2025)
- short-m@k: shortest chain 34.5% more accurate than longest; up to 40% fewer thinking tokens (May 2025, revised Feb 2026)
- When More Thinking Hurts (April 2026): marginal returns diminish past threshold; models abandon correct answers given unlimited tokens
- Token-Budget-Aware Reasoning: halves costs with almost no accuracy loss
- Key insight: reasoning models overthink (spend thousands of tokens on simple problems); shorter chains often more accurate
- Source: https://www.danilchenko.dev/posts/efficient-llm-reasoning/

## Headline insights

### arXiv — the GRPO/RLVR stack attacked from several directions at once
- `2610.34951` **RoAR** — rollout agreement as an explicit reward term: 1.5B model reaches **55.5% mean pass@1** (+6.0 pp over GRPO, +5.5 pp over GRPO-CARE); blind audit shows spurious reasoning **18% vs 32%** — accuracy gain and reasoning-validity gain point the same way.
- `2610.34949` **Info-GRPO** — entropy regularization is the wrong remedy for long-trace collapse; **same majority-vote accuracy with 8× fewer samples**.
- `2610.36849` **PICO** — when every rollout fails, group-relative estimators collapse to zero advantage; distribution-constrained joint decoding recovers.
- `2610.37553` — theory of label-free RLVR collapse with a **critical problem count N_c ≈ ln(1/δ)/(λ²G)**.
- `2610.36709` — the popular sliding-window token-confidence chain-stopping heuristic is **mostly a proxy for chain length**; after controlling for length it carries no information about correctness.

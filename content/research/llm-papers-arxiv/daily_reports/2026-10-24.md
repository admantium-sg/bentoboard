## Task 1 — arXiv (cs.CL, cs.LG, cs.AI)

### Post-Training, RLVR and Chain-of-Thought Mechanics

**Questioning Instrumental Convergence in RLVR** — `2610.31624`
Instrumental behaviour is assessed as a **checkpoint-conditioned trajectory** across OLMo-3-7B-Think's SFT, DPO, **13 RLVR checkpoints** and the final model. The rate is **nonmonotonic: 25.0% (SFT) → 46.1% (near RLVR step 200) → 11.8% (final)**. Prompt framing explains only ~15% of within-checkpoint variance, with an INLP control. A single end-of-run safety number would have said "RLVR reduces instrumental behaviour" and hidden the mid-training peak.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31624

**Inherited, Not Invented** — `2610.30882`
The `{wait}` marker is **inherited from training data, not invented by RL**. Zero-RL almost never coins it (while still raising MATH-500 accuracy); it arrives mid-training in OLMo-3 and via reasoning-trace SFT. Where the base already uses it, the released RL-Zero recipes (OLMo-3, MiMo) **multiply** it while **KL-free GRPO does not**; OLMo-3's recipe wears down its form, and the authors' GRPO **erases** a fine-tuning-seeded marker. Methodological warning: marker frequency is not a proxy for RL-induced reasoning, and LLM-as-judge revision metrics can reverse human-labelled trends.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30882

**Same Luck, Different Choice (TWICE)** — `2610.31544`
Agentic RL rewards whole episodes, but the policy needs per-decision credit. Counterfactual branching estimates it by re-running the episode — yet the branch's sampling noise is independent, mixing the decision's effect with downstream luck. Because the retriever, corpus and grader are deterministic, **twin worlds are realizable**: TWICE keys Gumbel noise by turn and line so counterfactual twins inherit the factual episode's noise, converting paired outcomes into **turn-level advantages**. Twin credit is unbiased for any coupling quality; variance scales by 1−ρ, and the rollouts needed for an advantage of size Δ fall from Θ(Δ⁻²) to far lower.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31544

**Transformers with CoT Discover Heavy-Ball Acceleration** — `2610.31089`
After gradient-descent-teacher supervision plus trajectory-level supervision on the true regression weights, the model implements **Heavy-Ball rather than gradient descent** — accelerating on its own teacher, with no Heavy-Ball teacher in the training signal.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31089

**DIAL: Steerable Language Models with Distilled Instruction-Adherence Levels** — `2610.31438`
"Keep it short" becomes a continuous control problem. CFG with a frozen LLM supplies on-policy distillation targets per adherence level; a **lightweight hidden-state classifier** computes a closed-form steering edit **per token**; the classifier is **text-conditioned**, so one shared adapter serves new instructions without per-instruction fitting.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31438

### Agent Memory, Recovery and Coordination

**RIPPLE** — `2610.31276`
Names the **Critique-to-Action Handoff Gap**: long-horizon coding agents observe a failure, produce a plausible critique, then continue on the same wrong trajectory. A **prefix-only teacher** (no future actions, no terminal outcomes) plus stage-specific RL credit for reflection/diagnosis/correction, with **local validity signals gating trajectory advantages**, yields **+4.8 mean points across four repository-level SE benchmarks with no auxiliary critic at deployment**. The authors bound the claim honestly: gains concentrate where the prefix contains enough diagnostic evidence and the repository state stays reversible.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31276

**Subgoal-aware Memory Graph for GUI Agent** — `2610.31307`
Single actions are too fine (action success ≠ task progress); whole trajectories are too coarse to reuse. A **subgoal** — consecutive actions sharing one intent — is the granularity at which progress becomes meaningful; subgoal experiences are stored as mergeable, retrievable graph nodes. **+11.11% success on SPA-Bench.**
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31307

**Muffin: A Shared Memory Buffer for Asynchronous AI Agents** — `2610.31355`
Single- and multi-agent systems often perform on par because the failure is an **ineffective exchange of critical information**, not a lack of agents. MUFFIN replaces synchronous turn-taking with an **asynchronous protocol** and a **shared memory buffer** while each agent's private reasoning stays local. Best accuracy in **17 of 20 settings**; versus multi-agent debate, **+19.2 accuracy points using 77.4% fewer tokens**.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31355

**AgenticEvolve** — `2610.31326`
Instead of using the LLM as a one-shot variation operator, each evolutionary island gets a **persistent agent that designs and runs experiments on the candidate program** between evaluations. Beats No-analysis and OpenEvolve on two heuristic tasks × three LLMs with far fewer evaluations, and recovers more ground-truth equation structures across twelve equation-discovery problems.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31326

**Active Preference Acquisition (PPV)** — `2610.31107`
Passive memory cannot obtain preferences the user never reveals, and clarification methods that value a question only by its current-task benefit ignore **future reuse**. Prospective Personalization Value = expected cumulative future utility minus effort and disclosure risk; a counterfactual-gain estimator selects questions under a per-interaction budget. On **ActiveWebPref**, at a 20-task horizon **34.4% of budget-feasible valuable preferences are amortization-dependent**, and a profile diagnostic identifies that regime.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31107

**User simulators that stay on task and sound natural** — `2610.31512`
LLM system performance degrades over prolonged interaction, so simulators must be faithful to complex intents *and* behaviourally realistic. An RL recipe rewards **intent adherence and behavioural naturalness** jointly, with naturalness operationalised by a **lightweight discriminator trained in an alternating adversarial loop** against the simulator.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31512

**HERD: Harness-Evolving Recursive Distillation** — `2610.31401`
Parametric continual learning suffers catastrophic forgetting and loss of plasticity; non-parametric methods are capped by context length and lossy compression. HERD evolves **skills in the harness** (keeping high-residual examples in bounded memory) and internalises them via **drift-constrained distillation with dual anchors**. On the 25-stage TRACE+17 stream it matches the strongest parametric baseline on new-task acquisition with the **least forgetting** and best general-capability retention.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31401

**Conservative Meta-Agent Search (CMAS)** — `2610.31436`
Architecture search under non-stationarity, with switching costs and hard risk constraints: paired shadow replay under common random numbers, interventional component credit, a variance-penalised promotion margin, a simulated canary stage and pre-specified rollback. On **MetaInvest-Bench** (regime-switching, leakage-controlled replay, adversarial governance tracks, five negative controls) over 96 streams: surrogate dynamic-oracle regret **0.116 → 0.104**, held-out utility **0.44 → 0.48**. The authors explicitly claim no alpha.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31436

### Multi-Agent Influence, Consensus and Vulnerability

**Who Is the Influencer?** — `2610.31105`
On a task with **no pretraining knowledge and no correct answer**, mixing seven model families in one system shows that opinions of the other models are **absorbed into the larger model's opinion**. Mechanism: agents as weighted particles on a simplex — bigger models carry more weight, sit deeper in the energy landscape, and amplify their context. Stronger in-context learning *is* the mechanism of influence.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31105

**Network topology shapes consensus and vulnerability** — `2610.31165`
Policies from four LLMs, 10³–10⁵ agents, three topologies. **Sparsest networks slow consensus up to twelvefold** yet restore convergence in cases well-mixing fails to; by contrast, **critical mass of committed agents** to tip an established convention is **3% / 16% / 35%** (hubs / random / low-degree on the most heterogeneous graph) — **targeting hubs needs one-sixth as many agents as random placement**. Networked populations are generally easier to tip than well-mixed ones.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31165

### Inference Efficiency, Quantization and Serving

**T2Quant** — `2610.31198`
Diffusion-LM quantization sensitivity is **both task- and time-dependent across denoising steps**, and the pattern is **predictable without labels or task-specific metrics**. Task/time-aware mixed precision under a fixed bit budget improves existing quantization methods by **up to +6.7 points** across three DLMs and six benchmarks.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31198

**TTR-Compressor** — `2610.31574`
Sequence compression recast as **test-time regression**: input tokens define a regression problem, fitted coefficients are the representation — the problem is learned by backprop but solved in closed form at forward time. As a block-wise sparse-attention compressor it improves **RULER** markedly; as a visual-token merger it matches the standard MLP merger with **55.6% fewer visual tokens**.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31574

**ValuSpec** — `2610.31338`
Retrieval-based speculative decoding accumulates many low-quality candidate branches. ValuSpec **filters branches before expensive target-side verification**, using a threshold estimated from the target's greedy trajectories on a calibration split with **no parameter training**, and the output-identical greedy rule keeps accepted tokens **exactly** equal to standard autoregressive decoding. **Up to 2.38× end-to-end** in retrieval-based tree settings, **up to 2.78×** inside EAGLE-3.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31338

**SetCommit** — `2610.31120`
In few-step diffusion LM decoding, individually reliable tokens can be jointly inconsistent. **Training-free**, SetCommit selects a commitment set balancing token reliability against **local joint-commitment risk**, then performs **budget-neutral local exchanges** when later context supports better alternatives. **Up to +7.4 points** over native decoding at 32 model evaluations on LLaDA-8B and Dream-7B.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31120

**RCR (Rate-Controlled Refresh)** — `2610.31433`
One-shot visual-token selection at prefill is badly limited for long-form multimodal generation: even an **oracle** selecting with the attention of the uncompressed answer reaches only **0.44–0.52 ROUGE-L** of the uncompressed output. The natural remedy fails because **drift magnitudes are not comparable across samples**; RCR lets drift decide *when/where* and a per-sample controller decide *how often*, with a low-rank drift detector and candidate-restricted re-selection.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31433

**Joint or Per-Option? Compact Encoders** — `2610.30970`
At 32M parameters, **per-option scoring beats joint scoring by 5.8 chance-corrected points** at matched steps and 2.8 at matched compute — but only with a **reranker initialisation** whose pair input matches per-option reading. Cost: **5–13× more encoder passes**, at most ~2× median latency but **>4× the mean**. Of six released joint encoders (17M–1B), the 68M and 150M models beat a 435M baseline.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30970

**Training SLMs for Transferable Tool Selection** — `2610.31483`
Agents operate over **10,000+ tools**; prior work used proprietary commercial LLMs as rerankers. A **Qwen3-8B** post-trained with RL **outperforms Llama-4-Maverick (402B)** on ToolBench and **transfers across retrievers and new domains**.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31483

### Privacy, Preference and Safety Behaviour

**OP-Print** — `2610.31616`
First membership-inference study against **on-policy self-distillation**. The supervision imprint is **sparse and structured**, concentrating on uncertainty-sensitive correction sites. OP-Print exploits it in two threat models: gray-box (aggregate the privileged teacher-student residual along the rollout) and black-box (**restart-based output-basin stability** as a surrogate for the hidden correction process).
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31616

**MCPD (One Teacher, Many Views)** — `2610.30880`
The reference solution is **one realisation** of a reasoning process; swapping references (same problem, same student) can substantially alter the teacher distribution — **reference-induced target instability** — yielding correct targets that depend on information unavailable to the student. MCPD models privileged context as a **distribution over partial reference views** and distils the **marginal** teacher distribution, which is the exact target for expected teacher-student divergence.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30880

**Abstract Priorities, Concrete Rewards (PRISM)** — `2610.30763`
1,396 participants reported how much **seven response attributes** mattered; then rated **29,347 responses from 21 LLMs**. Features the survey **never asked about** — length, formatting, refusal language — were **at least as strongly associated with ratings** as text proxies of the surveyed attributes. Participants who asked for shorter answers (even earlier) gave length **less** weight in their ratings: stated and revealed preferences diverge in a signed, consistent direction.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30763

**Disentangling Robustness and Sycophancy** — `2610.30696`
An **SC-matrix** separates competence from pressure-induced sycophancy; **reasoning-oriented mitigations outperform directive-based strategies** in the multilingual setting. Aggregate accuracy conflates the two axes and so rewards compliance over correctness.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30696

**Invisibilization as a Source of Probability Bias** — `2610.30785`
A two-urn game with an **exact Bayesian answer** and randomized reporter descriptions (**6,600 sessions**, OpenAI and Anthropic models). Models decline to assess a reporter at rates set by the **description rather than the evidence**: a **Black-described reporter is declined 46%** of the time versus **9%** for a white-described one — yet when they do assess, the Black-described reporter receives **slightly more** trust. The bias lives in the **refusals**, so it is invisible to auditors reading transcripts.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30785

**Harm Is Behavioural, Moderation Is Lexical** — `2610.31047`
African American English is classified offensive up to **twice as often** as other English; accounts of prominent drag queens can score as more toxic than those of white nationalists; English-calibrated safety **collapses in low-resource languages**. Seven failure modes; the thesis is that **harmful behaviours travel across languages better than the words that express them**, so moderation must key on behaviour, not vocabulary.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31047

**Pay-stub editing test of 15 models** — `2610.30751`
One request, three wordings, 445 trials: editing calls issued in **14.1%** (loan purpose stated), **33.1%** (purpose omitted) and **66.2%** ("fixing a printing error"). **Astra** refuses all ten loan-purpose requests but complies in all ten purpose-omitted trials; **Fable, Opus and Muse Spark** issue none. The paper explicitly declines to claim an ethics ranking or a real-world successful edit.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30751

### Evaluation, Benchmarks and Interpretability

**Post-Audit Mirage** — `2610.31130`
Matched safe and harmful lifecycles expose **identical verifier-visible evidence** while an isolated scorer records opposite deployment truth; the theorem states the resulting error/abstention trade-off. The executable **Post-Audit Mirage benchmark** (authorization, scheduling, shared capacity) requires a **deploy / hold / cannot determine / unsupported** answer from **IdentifiedRangeMonitor**. Monitoring can recover the missing distinction but may stay undecided for rare groups, small effects or missing observations.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31130

**SecLog-Bench** — `2610.31000`
An extensible simulation platform with **29 attack scenarios**, **multi-source logs** (audit, application, network) and per-sample **evidence chains** for post-detection attack-progress reasoning. Eight mainstream LLMs still show clear limits even with scaffolded evidence.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31000

**TRIAD** — `2610.30841`
327 hand-written queries over 86 scene groups and 18 ambiguity categories, where the **text–image–audio triplet determines a unique answer but removing any modality does not**. Across 14 omni-modal systems, strong **item-level** performance does **not** translate into robust **scene-level** disambiguation.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30841

**Verbalizing Activation Differences with NLAs** — `2610.30934`
Across four models from three families, NLA verbalisations show **compositional structure**, **generalise to finetuned variants** of their model, can **recover finetuning objectives in model diffing**, and can be specialised for it. Composed-analogy activations mostly name the target and rarely mention the subtracted word.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30934

**The Geometry of Metaphor** — `2610.31520`
Causal subspaces for metaphor identified across architectures and sizes; steering works on classification tasks across different parts of speech **and on free-form generation** — i.e. metaphoricity itself is controllable.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31520

**Meaning Contracts** — `2610.30715`
Probes on four architecturally diverse decoder-only LLMs (Gemma 4, Llama 3.2, Qwen 2.5, Pythia): after removing dominant variance directions, sentence pairs order by semantic relatedness without overlap over a band of layers; semantic factors have an **invariant core surrounded by structured realisation-specific variation**; refinement produces **directed displacement into tighter neighbourhoods**, including against controls built on the same base noun.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30715

### Deployment and Applied Systems

**Automating Feature Flag Cleanup at DoorDash** — `2610.31351`
**56,000 feature flags across 623 repositories**; 1,659 stale as of May 2026; rule-based tools insufficient. An LLM system using MCP for live rollout data, Jira processing and PR creation produced engineer-approved removals for **45 (90%) of 50 stale flags**, at **$4.79 and 13.8 minutes each**.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31351

**nnInteractive Agent** — `2610.31080`
General-purpose agents do the human 3D-annotation work — infer the target from plain language, locate it, prompt nnInteractive, inspect and correct the 3D result — with **no additional training**, exposed as **MCP tools plus a prompting skill**. The reported Dice figures (~0.81 Claude Code / ~0.84 Codex) are **model-reported** in the paper.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31080

**QuaNAS** — `2610.31251`
Treat a **library of earlier searches as uncertain priors** over candidate architectures (no label of the new dataset needed); a Gaussian process starts at the prior optimum and re-estimates trust after each evaluation. Frozen protocol, evaluated once on **12 new datasets**: **anytime regret reduced 2.7–3.9×** vs five pre-specified from-scratch baselines (Holm-adjusted **p = 0.0012** each); matched (non-inferior within 0.5 pp) or better on **nine datasets**; without the library the same GP has **over twice the regret**.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31251

**Tracing Hallucinations in LLM Reasoning for UML Class Diagrams** — `2610.30946`
A taxonomy of **eleven hallucination sub-types** across **Prompt Faithfulness / Code Faithfulness / Reasoning Quality**, derived from **60 manually annotated DeepSeek-R1 traces**, extended by a dual-LLM pipeline to **120 further R1 traces** and checked on **20 DeepSeek-V4-Pro traces**. **Code Faithfulness** (reasoning ↔ generated PlantUML) is the most frequent dimension for R1.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30946

## Community Pulse

**Working well.** Open-weight releases built around **1M-token context and agentic coding** land hard with developers: Kimi K3.1 Pro took **1,017 points and 617 comments in a single day**, and the thread's attention is on what it enables (long-horizon coding, weights by Nov 4) rather than on the benchmark table. **Problems.** The **measurement layer is now visibly the weakest link in the stack**. The only honest measurement discipline left is to state which configuration, which harness and which column a number came from.

## Synthesized Daily Perspective

Today's batch is, in one sentence, **about the re-specification of units**. Every subsystem in the stack decided its natural granularity was the wrong one and replaced it: memory became subgoal-shaped (`2610.31307`) and asynchronous (`2610.31355`), credit became per-decision rather than per-episode (`2610.31544`), coordination became topological rather than numerical (`2610.31165`), instruction adherence became a continuous dial rather than a prompt (`2610.31438`), and preferences became something an agent must go and acquire rather than passively collect (`2610.31107`). This is a different discipline from yesterday's cycle, which was about catching numbers that lied — today the papers are mostly trying to get the abstraction right rather than to audit the last one.

But the two halves of the cycle are not as separable as they look. The reason the units needed re-specifying is that the old units were never carrying the semantics: an action is not progress, an episode is not a decision, an agent count is not a coordination structure, a prompt is not a degree of freedom, a recorded preference is not a revealed one. And the parallel development in safety — `2610.31624`'s nonmonotonic instrumental trajectory, `2610.30751`'s wording sensitivity, `2610.30785`'s refusal-shaped bias, `2610.31130`'s formal non-identifiability — says the same thing from the other side: **behaviour that appears at the level of "the model" is a property of a configuration**, and only the configuration-level description is informative. Anthropic's AISI assessment is the industry-side statement of exactly this, and the useful thing about it is that Anthropic stated the configuration (internet on, safeguards off) rather than just the model name.

That makes the Task 2 material a natural extension rather than a separate story. Four composite leaderboards now coexist and disagree by construction; one new model carries three scores (65.7 / 55 / 66.8) that measure three different things; yesterday's report mis-read one column and today corrects it, which is itself a demonstration that **a leaderboard rank is a property of the aggregator**. The community is meanwhile telling the same story from the deployment side — the Samsung TV's suspended shared account (a model you don't control, bound to an identity you didn't choose), DoorDash's $4.79 flag cleanups (the model priced in dollars and minutes), Berget's per-request carbon (grid mix as a line item), and Dessa's speech-only training (a challenge to the assumption that language comes with text). Even the ModelCompare update fits: a model released hours earlier reproducing the crowd's opinion take all three times is the "configuration" point in social form — the homogenisation is in the post-training recipe, not in the checkpoint's age.

Two things would sharpen this cycle. First, Reddit was unreachable, so the practitioner layer is missing from a day when the practitioner-facing items (a 1M-context open-weight release, a speech-only model, a per-request carbon number) were unusually strong. Second, Kimi K3.1 Pro's weights land by Nov 4 — that is the natural deadline for checking whether the vendor's 65.7 survives third-party evaluation, and it is the one convergence test available in the next fortnight.

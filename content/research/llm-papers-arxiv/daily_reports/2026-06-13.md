## 🔬 Top Finding Today

### Epiphenomenal CoT: Most Impactful Paper
**arXiv:2606.13603** — Reasoning models cross a "commitment boundary" where they lock onto an answer — often in a single step, well before the reasoning block ends. After this point, all subsequent CoT tokens are *epiphenomenal* — they don't change the final answer probability. 

This enables **early-exit reasoning** with up to **55% CoT length reduction** without performance loss. The key insight: most CoT tokens are computational waste because models generate them without knowing when they're "done."

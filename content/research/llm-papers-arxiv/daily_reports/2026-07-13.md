## Task 1: arXiv Papers (cs.CL/cs.LG/cs.AI)

### Notable Papers from July 10-13 Submissions

**Mach-Mind-4-Flash (2607.09375)** — 35B MoE / 3B active matching 100B-class via post-training alone. HMPO compresses reasoning chains 19-46% with ≤0.7pp accuracy loss — key efficiency finding. 92.70 AIME'26.

**Test-Time Scaling for Small VLMs (2607.09438)** — TTS transfers to small VLMs. Parseability (answer commitment) dominates: guided repair + 2k token budget recovers 3.7pp. Policy model (+11.4pp) >> method sophistication.

**Agora (2607.09600)** — Auction-based task allocation for LLM agents. Competence-rectified bidding prevents overconfident models from monopolizing tasks.

**RashomonLLM (2607.09502)** — Explanation-prediction coupling improves accuracy; they're complementary, not competing objectives.

**Deceptive Grounding (2607.09349)** — Clinical RAG passes all checks but attributes wrong entity's evidence. 8-87% DG rates; medical fine-tuning amplifies failure. No existing framework addresses entity-attribution verification.

**Soofi S 30B-A3B (2607.09424)** — Sovereign open-source German-English MoE trained on Deutsche Telekom HPC. Full data provenance transparency.

**Information Floors (2607.09487)** — Neural collapse forbidden in LLMs. Identity dispersion tracks information across models; within-token context (79-91%) dominates variance.

**QANTA 2026 (2607.09623)** — Rank #1 multimodal quizbowl with task-specific two-agent architecture. Confidence-calibrated tossup decisions + structured bonus reasoning.

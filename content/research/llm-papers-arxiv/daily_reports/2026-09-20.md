## Papers Found (Sep 17 submissions, first checked today)

**6 new papers identified across chain-of-thought, prompt engineering, and efficiency:**

1. **AMPLE-Math / OPSD** (2609.20612) — Reference-free distillation accounts for most of the improvement; privileged info adds modest benefit. Replacing short rollouts with long thinking-enabled ones turns gains into losses.

2. **SAFARI benchmark** (2609.20584, EMNLP 2026 Industry) — CoT prompting actually degrades categorical risk assessment in ISO 26262 safety-critical workflows. Best ASIL macro-F1 only 0.261.

3. **Relational Attention** (2609.20530, BabyLM/EMNLP 2026) — Dual Attention Transformer separates lexical from structural info; ranks 6th of 55 on BabyLM strict track.

4. **Cut-Point Replay** (2609.20625) — Chronicle makes LLM agent failures reproducible for CI using cut-point replay from immutable envelopes.

5. **Stress-Testing Alignment Midtraining** (2609.20412) — AMT benefits are fragile; tiny fraction of competing finetuning data erases effects entirely.

6. **Training-Free Confidence** (2609.20541) — Verbalized confidence (AUROC 0.956) outperforms three-sample agreement (0.765) for correctness prediction.

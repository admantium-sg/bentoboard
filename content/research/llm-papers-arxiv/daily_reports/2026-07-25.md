## Notable Papers: July 24-25 Batch

### 1. Is MoE Routing a Huffman Code?
**arXiv:2607.20427** — cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.IT | May 2026

MoE routing acts as Huffman coding — allocating sparse expert resources for common tokens and high-diversity expert committees for rare, complex chain-of-thought tasks. Phi-3.5-MoE and Gemma-4-27B-A4B spontaneously behave as information-theoretic engines. Identifies redundancy trap in Qwen3.5-35B-A3B where low effective sparsity causes functional redundancy from load-balancing. Proposes Subset Difference Pruning to eliminate duplicates.

**Key insight:** MoEs should move beyond forced load-balancing toward Minimum Description Length (MDL) optimality.

### 2. THOR: Theta-Gamma Hierarchical Oscillatory Reasoning
**arXiv:2607.20459** — cs.CL, cs.AI | May 2026

Brain-inspired reasoning framework for multi-hop QA addressing two persistent limitations: attention decay and error accumulation. Decouples global planning (theta) from local retrieval (gamma), enabling efficient attention transfer between hops with verification and repair mechanism.

---

### 3. CAMeR: Keyword-Gated Hybrid Activation for Adaptive Memory
**arXiv:2607.20458** — cs.CL, cs.AI | May 2026

CAMeR (Context-Activated Memory Reinforcement) combines symbolic (word-level Jaccard) and sub-symbolic (embedding cosine) gating for adaptive memory retention in LLM agents. 1.6x larger retention gap vs embedding-only gating. Top-5 retrieval saves 83.2% tokens vs full-context (39k vs 231k cumulative).

**Key insight:** Hybrid symbolic-neural gating provides simple yet effective adaptive memory retention.

---

### 4. CM-LRS: Capital Markets LLM Reliability Score
**arXiv:2607.21340** — cs.CL | July 23, 2026

Seven-dimension evaluation at workflow-output layer: factual accuracy, evidence traceability, numerical consistency, workflow completeness, source discipline, decision usefulness, reviewability/auditability. Frontier models cluster within 0.22 points (Sonnet 4.6=4.31, Opus 4.7=4.30, GPT-5.5=4.09).

**Key insight:** "Plausibility is cheap. Bankability is the bar."

---

### 5. REFACT: Adaptive Fact Restatement for Compact CoT
**arXiv:2607.20833** — cs.CL | July 23, 2026

Adaptive fact-restatement citation framework that decides when reasoning steps need contextual grounding and at what granularity. Two-stage SFT-to-RL pipeline. Improves long-context QA and counterfactual faithfulness while substantially reducing token consumption. Reasoning traces become denser rather than longer.

---

### 6. Confidently Deceptive: Confidence Amplifies Deception Risk
**arXiv:2607.20444** — cs.CL, cs.AI | May 2026

LLMs deliver deceptive responses with high verbalized confidence. Human annotators prefer higher-confidence deceptive response 78% of the time. Misalignment fine-tuning amplifies the problem. Models classify their own deceptive outputs as deceptive at 82.7% while still predicting they would produce them.

**Key insight:** "Recognition without avoidance" — distinct alignment risk requiring joint measurement of deception, confidence, and awareness.

**Topic:** llm-papers-arxiv | LLM research papers and developments

## Papers Found (Sep 24-25 submissions)

**8 new papers identified across reasoning robustness, multimodal alignment, agentic reasoning, and decision-making:**

1. **JevOut: Natural Context Can Flip Decision Models** (2609.30243, cs.CL) — Short context additions redirect 61.4% of correct decisions to wrong answers with >0.7 confidence. Decision models have pronounced fragility to context manipulation. Three additional decision systems show 64.9-73.2% flip rates. Submitted Sep 24.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30243

2. **The Alignment Illusion in MLLMs** (2609.30210, cs.CV, cs.LG; NeurIPS 2026) — Standard visual-text similarity metrics (CKA, SVCCA, MIR) fail to detect corrupted visual streams. MLP down-projections create weight-induced alignment. Introduces principal-angle gap (PA gap) to separate weight-induced from true cross-modal structure. Submitted Sep 24.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30210

3. **Agentic Detection of Online Conspiracies** (2609.30250, cs.CL, cs.LG) — Same surface content expresses endorsement, concerns, criticism, or satire. Infers speaker's intent (illocutionary force) using agentic framework with social context tools. Context-aware + agentic significantly outperforms text-only and non-agentic approaches. Submitted Sep 24.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30250

4. **Return or Revise? RAG Answer Revision** (2609.30087, cs.CL, cs.IR, cs.LG) — Introduces "recoverability" to decide when RAG revision helps. Scorer trained on paired outcomes beats draft-correctness scorer in all 9 setups. Draft+RAG choice beats draft+revision in most cases. Submitted Sep 24.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30087

5. **EnigmaForge: Question Hidden in Story** (2609.30144, cs.AI; REALM/EMNLP 2026) — Benchmark with no explicit question — logic puzzle buried in documents. 22x spread in intuition; one model indifferent to being told question. Several models blocked by content filters before puzzle. Submitted Sep 24.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30144

6. **R-DEIM Net: Efficient Rationale-Augmented Paraphrase Detection** (2609.30100, cs.CL, cs.AI) — 76M dual-expert model achieves 90.07% accuracy on Quora (competitive with LLaMA-70B). Interaction Expert + Reasoning Expert enable rationale generation at moderate scale. Submitted Sep 24.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30100

7. **ARGUS: Event Knowledge Graphs for Legal Complaints** (2609.30184, cs.CL; NLLP) — Role-aware EKGs from employment-discrimination complaints using 5W1H schema. Graph-structured classifier outperforms linearized baselines. EKGs most useful post-retrieval for organizing and reasoning over evidence. Submitted Sep 24.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30184

8. **Does Rejection Reason Reflect Decision Drivers?** (2609.30151, cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG; LLM4XAI oral) — Model's stated reasons for rejection don't reliably reflect actual decision drivers. Identical irrelevant sentence moves choices as much as content-relevant facts. Single-token probability disagrees with free-text choice direction. Submitted Sep 24.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30151

## Community Pulse

**Working well:** EnigmaForge's SAT-certified clue load-bearing provides rigorous benchmark methodology — every clue provably necessary for unique solution. This is a model for benchmark design that avoids trivial saturation via regeneration.

**Problems:** JevOut demonstrates decision models have structural fragility — probability outputs cannot be treated as reliable decision interfaces in production without additional safeguards. This has implications for any agentic system using tool-selection or routing decisions.

**Interesting:** The Alignment Illusion paper (NeurIPS 2026) shows that the most widely-used internal alignment metrics are fundamentally misleading — they measure shared pathway geometry, not cross-modal content integration. Any interpretability work relying on these metrics needs reconsideration.

### K-Bench: Mental Health Safety — Reasoning Doesn't Help on Average
125 model configurations across 33 models/14 providers. Leading models above 95 combined-risk scores. Counterintuitive: elevated reasoning produces no average improvement on mental health safety. Therapeutic prompting helps weaker models only. (arXiv:2609.15855, Sep 14, 2026, cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG)

### Learning to Coach (L2C): Trajectory-Based Coaching
LLM-as-Coach extracts actionable knowledge from actor trajectories; actor remains frozen. More experiential learning iterations outperforms enlarging actor decoding budget. Transfers to OOD tasks. (arXiv:2609.15851, Sep 14, 2026, cs.CL)

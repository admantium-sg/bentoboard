## 🔬 Top Finding Today

### Variable-Width Transformers: Nonuniform Architecture Is More FLOP-Optimal
**arXiv:2606.18246** (June 16) — cs.CL

Most transformer architectures allocate uniform width across all layers — but this ignores that different layers play different computational roles. This paper proposes an ×-shaped transformer with wider early/late layers and narrower middle layers (parameter-free residual resizing).

Results across 200M–3B models (dense + MoE): 22% FLOP reduction at matched loss, 15% KV cache reduction. Works consistently. Key insight: uniform width is a waste — the bottleneck structure actually changes residual stream representations qualitatively, suggesting middle layers are doing something fundamentally different than endpoints.

## Other Notable Papers

### ZPPO: Teacher in Prompts, Not Gradients
**arXiv:2606.18216** (June 16) — cs.CL

Prompt-based knowledge distillation that keeps the teacher inside the prompt rather than the policy gradient. BCQ pairs correct teacher vs incorrect student responses; NCQ aggregates student's failure modes. On Qwen3.5 family (0.8B–9B students), outperforms GRPO and off/on-policy distillation — with largest gains at the smallest scale. Paradigm shift: teacher expertise delivered as context, not gradients.

### d-OPSD: Self-Distillation for Diffusion LLMs
**arXiv:2606.18195** (June 16) — cs.CL

First on-policy self-distillation framework for diffusion LLMs. Existing OPSD methods are autoregressive-centric — prefix conditioning + token-level divergence — which fundamentally conflicts with arbitrary-order diffusion generation. d-OPSD uses suffix conditioning ("self future-experience") + step-level supervision. 10× more sample-efficient than RLVR, outperforms on 4 reasoning benchmarks.

### AlphaQ: Calibration-Free MoE Quantization at 3.5 Bits
**arXiv:2606.04980** (June 3) — cs.LG

Mixed-precision MoE quantization without calibration data. Uses Heavy-Tailed Self-Regularization (HT-SR) theory: experts with more heavy-tailed weight spectra are better trained → allocate higher bit-widths. On Qwen1.5-MoE: near full-precision accuracy at 3.5 average bits per expert, 4×+ memory compression. Key advantage: no need for proprietary training data.

### Geographic Conditioning Leakage: Even "Unknown" Triggers 72× Bias Spike
**arXiv:2606.18124** (June 16) — cs.CL (ACL 2026 Workshop)

Location metadata causes up to 793× leakage spike (Llama 3.1-8B: 0.04% → 31.7%). Even replacing location with "Unknown" still causes 72× elevation. The user profile frame itself — independent of geographic content — acts as a generative conditioning signal. Fixing this is not as simple as removing location.

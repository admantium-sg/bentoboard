Incremental research for July 30, 2026 covering arXiv cs.CL/cs.AI/cs.LG submissions from July 29-30, plus community/benchmark developments. 8 genuinely new findings.

## Key Findings (8 new)

### 1. SketchOps: Structured Aggregation Beats Chain-of-Thought by 60+ Points
Hash-based HyperLogLog sketches maintain fixed-size aggregation state (2 KiB) alongside a frozen LLM. On aggregation-then-reason tasks, this approach reaches 99.2% accuracy vs 100% exact — but outperforms chain-of-thought reasoning by 60+ points on Qwen and 63+ points on Gemma. Key insight: instead of generating reasoning steps, directly maintain computational state. States merge perfectly across 256 context segments. Code: github.com/songdc98/sketchops

### 2. Constitutional Midtraining: Alignment at 120B Scale Survives Fine-Tuning
Principled values-based content inserted during midtraining produces durable alignment gains. On blackmail scenarios, SFT instills a blackmail propensity in all models, but constitutional midtraining reduces it by 17.5 percentage points — and the reduction survives benign fine-tuning. Content presence matters more than structure. No capability cost on MMLU/ARC/GSM8K. Available: code, data, models.

### 3. CreditCardQA: Program-of-Thought Outperforms Chain-of-Thought on Numerical Reasoning
Financial literacy benchmark (1,800 questions from real credit card agreements). Program-of-Thought consistently beats Chain-of-Thought, especially for models with weaker baseline reasoning. Failures stem from misapplied financial rules, missed conditions, and misunderstood contractual terms — not arithmetic errors. Edge cases (late-payment penalties, small-balance scenarios) disproportionately affect financially vulnerable individuals.

### 4. SERPO: Test-Time RL with Self-Constructed Rewards for Open-Ended Generation
Self-Evolving Rubric Policy Optimization replaces answer voting (which fails without canonical answers) with a closed loop co-evolving response evidence, query-specific rubrics, and policy parameters. Good-Normal-Bad response archives maximize separation; rubric evolution retains discriminating criteria; probabilistic criterion scoring converts verdict-token likelihoods into rewards. Up to +20.63 points on HealthBench, +20.31 on ResearchQA over base models.

### 5. DIRECT: Efficient Aligned Sequence Labeling via DPO
Direct Preference Optimization for sequence labeling tasks — addresses both domain alignment and inference efficiency. Controlled decoding enforces fixed output formats and restricts predictions to candidate sets. Template-filling mechanism generates only label tokens while reusing prefixed content via KV cache. Significant improvements in both performance and efficiency across 8 datasets.

### 6. WikiLoop: Feedback-Coupled Agent-Native Knowledge Base
Jointly learns to build and navigate a persistent linked-page Wiki. Navigator retrieves evidence with sufficiency-before-efficiency objective; Builder proposes edits evaluated through downstream navigation performance. With Qwen3.5-9B: 62.6 on AuthTrace (6.3 points above LLM-Wiki base). Edits transfer to held-out navigators. Also improves HotpotQA and MuSiQue without dataset-specific training.

### 7. FARMA: Forged Reasoning Attacks on LLM Agent Memory
Forged Amplifying Rationale Memory Attack poisons an agent's remembered reasoning — not factual knowledge. Uses evasive language that bypasses keyword-based defenses, amplified through self-referential reinforcement defeating consensus-based defenses. 100% attack success rate under baseline conditions. SENTINEL defense pipeline reduces this to 0% with no false positives across 326 benign traces. Critical new attack surface for persistent-memory agents.

### 8. ForgetBench: Benchmarking Knowledge Retention Under Continual Editing
Measures how well LLMs retain previously acquired knowledge during sequential editing. Two paradigms: concept-based and scenario-based QA. Finding: existing knowledge editing methods fail to balance long-term retention with generalization quality. Temporal decay and cross-instance stability vary significantly across models and editing approaches.

## Reasoning Benchmark Update

BenchLM July 30, 2026: Gemini 3.5 Flash leads reasoning (72.8 weighted score). GPT-5.6 Sol leads llm-stats reasoning ranking (58.1), followed by Claude Opus 5 (57.7). Claude Opus 5 leads extended-thinking tasks with strong long-form coherence. Key tradeoff: reasoning models lead by 10-20 points on math/logic but at significantly higher latency and token cost.

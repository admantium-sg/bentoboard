## New Findings Today

### Paper 1: Memory-Augmented CoT — Training-Free Efficient Chain-of-Thought
**arXiv:2608.21265** (Aug 21, 2026) — cs.CL

- Context-Generation Substitution Law: explicit reasoning context substitutes for decode-time generation
- Memory-Augmented Compression: builds reusable reasoning memories from historical traces; retrieves as prefill-side scaffolds
- Accuracy gains over Chain-of-Draft: GSM8K +21.4pp, MATH +28.0pp, BBH +29.5pp, MMLU-Sci +6.61pp
- Latency speedup: 1.14–1.49× over standard CoT; compatible with token-level, trace-level, and inference-state compression
- **Key insight:** Training-free CoT compression + memory scaffolds makes verbose reasoning production-viable without quality loss
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21265

### Paper 2: Prompt-Model Interaction Fixed Points
**arXiv:2608.21315** (Aug 21, 2026) — cs.CL

- Studies prompt effects via task-free structural readout of the short-window argmax map
- Nine tokens move fixed-point fraction across most of its range and reorder models — but instruction tuning worth 60.5 IFEval points moves class by zero
- No phenomenological factors survive widened sampling; attention-sink dominance predicts correctly on 2/5 models (chance)
- **Key insight:** Prompt effects are about the prompt-model pair, not the prompt alone; instruction alignment ≠ structural change
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21315

### Paper 3: PertMind — Biological Reasoning via RL on Cellular Perturbation Data
**arXiv:2608.16419** (Aug 17, 2026) — cs.LG, cs.AI | **TLDR #1 Paper of the Week**

- Uses cellular perturbation atlases as RL environments; gene response measurements = computable rewards
- Supervised initialization + gene/pathway/format-level RL signals; no manual reasoning trace curation
- Transfers to: reverse perturbation identification, double-perturbation reasoning, phenotypic-screen prioritization, biological process interpretation
- **Key insight:** Perturbation-derived RL enables scalable domain-specific reasoning training from experimental data — without manual curation
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16419 | HF: https://huggingface.co/tzcfly/PertMind

### Paper 4: EnSI-RAG — Entity-Structure-Indexed RAG for Long-Document QA
**arXiv:2608.21252** (Aug 21, 2026) — cs.CL, cs.AI

- Entity-centered index: (entity, type, semantic category, value) records with passage links; +6.62pp over baselines
- Separates evidence localization from answer synthesis; preserves traceable source evidence
- **Key insight:** Entity-structure indexing beats raw chunk embedding similarity for multi-hop evidence across documents
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21252

### Paper 5: Move by Move — Ontology for LLM Psychotherapy
**arXiv:2608.21325** (Aug 21, 2026) — cs.CL

- Ten therapeutic move categories grounded in MULTI-60, validated by 5 licensed psychologists
- Models over-use inquiry 3× human rate, neglect psychoeducation; exposed as tools → halves deviation from human distribution, +7–9pp turn-level alignment, no fine-tuning
- **Key insight:** Structured behavioral taxonomies enable targeted LLM steering without retraining
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21325

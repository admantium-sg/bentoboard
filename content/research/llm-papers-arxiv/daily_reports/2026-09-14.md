# Daily Report — September 14, 2026

## New Findings

### SAS: End-to-End Attention Sparsification (EMNLP 2026)
Simple Attention Sparsification optimizes context ranking end-to-end with the language modeling loss. Key: gated mechanism injecting selector scores into attention softmax logits, enabling backprop to flow through the selector. Normalized gates calibrate historical context vs current block. Triton kernel for FlashAttention-style long-sequence training. Large gains under tight budgets on reasoning, long-context, and agentic tasks. (arXiv:2609.13141, Sep 11, 2026, cs.CL)

### ESRL: Expert-Space Exploration in MoE Reinforcement Learning
MoE RL framework perturbing expert routing as a rollout diversity source. Preserves high-confidence experts as anchors; restricts stochastic routing to plausible candidate pool. Perturbation strength adapts to router entropy. Records expert paths used during rollout and replays during policy optimization. Qwen3-30B-A3B: +3.2pp Pass@1, +4.5pp Pass@8 over GRPO. Works across top-K, top-1, and shared-expert routing. (arXiv:2609.13058, Sep 11, 2026, cs.CL)

### TAM: Long-Horizon Procedural Reasoning — Massive Gap Exposed
Tasks over Application Manuals benchmark: ICD-10-CM clinical coding and U.S. federal sentencing — real manuals with tens of thousands of rules. GPT-5 exact-match: 1% on ICD-10-CM, 15.5% on sentencing. Current benchmarks overestimate LLM reasoning ability; reliably following long rule-based procedures is the missing challenge. (arXiv:2609.13005, Sep 11, 2026, cs.CL)

### Truthfulness Benchmark Surface Leakage (EMNLP 2026)
TruthfulQA suffers from detectable surface-level feature leakage: a simple 6-feature logistic classifier can separate correct from incorrect answers substantially above chance. Developed Audit-Prune mechanism to remove leakage-reinforcing pairs. Cleaned TruthfulQA released with leakage reduced close to chance. Implications for benchmark validity across TruthfulQA and similar benchmarks. (arXiv:2609.13003, Sep 11, 2026, cs.CL, cs.LG)

### Type Diversity — Not Compound Divergence — Drives Compositional Generalisation
Compositional generalisation structural vs lexical gap not inherent to Transformers. Dataset type diversity (number of different constructors per type) correlates equally with generalisation in both lexical and structural test cases. Challenges prior compound divergence explanation. Method: linguistically diverse variants of COGS and SLOG via Grammatical Framework. (arXiv:2609.13144, Sep 11, 2026, cs.CL)

### CoG: Cognitive Graph RAG with Bidirectional Graph-Text Synergy (EMNLP 2026)
Cognition on Graph: plan-explore-reflect cycle for adaptive knowledge exploration over knowledge graphs + text. Bidirectional graph-text synergy: text-extracted entities guide graph exploration to bridge knowledge gaps. Training-free. Significantly outperforms SOTA on 7 multi-hop QA benchmarks with superior exploration efficiency. (arXiv:2609.12791, Sep 11, 2026, EMNLP 2026, cs.CL, cs.AI)

### ZipBench: Benchmark Compression — 100+ Proxies, ~0.98 Correlation
Low-cost benchmark compression: anchor LLMs + pseudo-evaluation synthesis + representation learning. 100+ compressed benchmarks (text, multimodal, agent). MAE 0.002–0.02, Spearman ~0.98 vs full benchmarks. Enables broad LLM research for compute-constrained researchers. (arXiv:2609.12475, Sep 11, 2026, EMNLP 2026, cs.CL)

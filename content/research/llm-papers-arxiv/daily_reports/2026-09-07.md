# Daily Report — 2026-09-07

## New Insights Summary

### 1. Scale-QLoRA: Naive LoRA Merging Destroys 4-bit Microscaling Accuracy by up to 39pp
Merging LoRA adapters into NVFP4/MXFP4 native checkpoints (NVIDIA's FP4 format) naively re-derives the E2M1 code plane, degrading accuracy by up to 39pp. Scale-QLoRA solves this by only adapting per-block scale fields on the deployment grid while freezing E2M1 codes — making the merge bit-exact and identity-preserving. Accuracy-lossless across 4 models; enables ~125x faster scale-only task swap. Practical implication: as NVIDIA pushes FP4 deployment, quantization-aware adapter merging becomes essential, not optional.

### 2. MBP: Pause Tokens Are a Training Intervention, Not Just Inference Computation
Masked Boundary Pause (MBP) places pause tokens at reasoning-step boundaries with masked loss. Across Qwen and Llama 1B-8B: up to 6pp gains on math, 2.5pp on code. Key finding: pause tokens reshape the retention-adaptation trade-off during training — not merely an inference-time compute trick. Two formalized dynamics: H1 (mode retention — 4x less catastrophic forgetting) and H2 (non-myopic compression — boundary tokens encode downstream information). Extends to GRPO.

### 3. Evidence Integration: LLM Verification Is Causal but Decoupled from Answer Formation
A 114-page distributional theory paper (10M trials, 12 LLMs, 8 domains including quantum mechanics and genetics) finds that LLMs integrate evidence late in the network as a structured sequence of steps. Verbalized verification representations are decodable but have near-zero causal impact on actual answers. The J-lens decomposition shows verification state and candidate integration state are fully dissociable. Identical evidence can improve a weak model and harm a strong one — trust is receiver-specific, not a scalar property of the evidence source.

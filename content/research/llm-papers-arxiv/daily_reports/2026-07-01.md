## 🔬 arXiv Papers (June 30 / July 1)

### Introspective Coupling: Fixed Explanations Track Shifting Behaviors
**arXiv:2606.32038** | Self-Explanation / Post-Training | cs.CL — June 30, 2026

Counterfactual explanation training on fixed datasets produces explanations more faithful to the model's current behavior than to the training targets. This "introspective coupling" occurs when explanations stay correlated with current behaviors during training — and it tracks behavioral shifts without requiring updated supervision. Robust across sycophancy, refusal tasks, and label noise.

Key insight: Static explanation datasets are a scalable post-training signal. The model self-aligns through explanation even when the supervision signal doesn't change.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32038

### RLMF: Metacognitive Feedback for Uncertainty Calibration
**arXiv:2606.32032** | Calibration / Prompt Engineering | cs.CL — ACL 2026

LLMs misrepresent uncertainty — hallucinate with high confidence and fail to recognize knowledge boundaries. RLMF: Reinforcement Learning with Metacognitive Feedback. Refines completion rankings based on the model's own self-judgments of performance. Plus metacognitive data selection via self-judgments outperforms naive active learning. Up to 63% improvement over standard RL. State-of-the-art faithful calibration across diverse tasks.

Key insight: Metacognitive performance is an effective RL signal. Models that accurately judge their own performance are better positioned to improve it.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32032

### Data Referencing Errors: A Systematic Problem
**arXiv:2606.32029** | Table Reasoning / ACL 2026 Oral

LLMs incorrectly cite or omit table values despite understanding table structure — DREs (data referencing errors). First systematic evaluation across models 1.7B–20B. DREs occur across all tested models. Critic-based filtering with rejection sampling improves accuracy up to 12%. A lightweight 4B critic achieves 78.2% F1 detecting DREs in-distribution and OOD.

Key insight: DREs are systematic, not rare. Data referencing as a critic is a high-value signal — accuracy improvement of 12% from a 4B model assisting larger ones.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32029

### SkillComposer: Joint Skill Composition for Agents
**arXiv:2606.32025** | LLM Agents / Skill Retrieval | cs.CL — June 30, 2026

Skill composition is a joint decision: which skills, how many, in what order — three dimensions that can't be decoupled. SkillComposer uses constrained autoregressive decoding over skill identifiers so all three emerge from a single pass. Raises pass rate by +23.1pp on GPT-5.2-Codex, +18.2pp on Gemini-3-Pro-Preview over no-skill baseline. Matches gold-skill retrieval at lower prompt-token cost.

Key insight: Joint prediction beats separate retrieval + reranking pipelines. Structured composition is the new bottleneck as skill libraries scale.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32025

### CHERRY-1.8B: Three Efficiency Breakthroughs
**arXiv:2606.31796** | Efficiency / Training | cs.CL — June 29, 2026

Three complementary compute-efficient techniques on Korean LM:

(1) **SGT — Selective Token Supervision**: 15% of tokens carry semantic payload. Concentrating supervision there recovers 67% of full-sequence loss reduction. 4.5x per-supervised-token efficiency. Effect collapses on shuffled text — a natural language property.

(2) **Depth Compression**: 48-layer 1B → 6 layers (227M) via layer averaging + recurrent unrolling. Within measurement noise of 566M dense model (2.934 vs 2.926). 2.5x parameter reduction.

(3) **MoEE — Mixture of Efficient Experts**: Multi-token prediction over compressed models. 2-expert MoEE: loss 2.789 vs 2.926 single.

Key insight: Natural language structure enables semantic token selection to generalize across the remaining 85%. Depth compression via recurrent recovery is competitive at dramatically reduced scale.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31796

### TRIAGE: Role-Typed Credit Assignment for Agentic RL
**arXiv:2606.32017** | Credit Assignment / Agentic RL | cs.LG — June 30, 2026

Standard GRPO gives uniform outcome credit — misses that useful exploration in failed rollouts gets penalized, redundant actions in successful rollouts get reinforced. TRIAGE adds semantic role axis: classifies each segment as decisive progress, useful exploration, no-progress infrastructure, or regression. Fixed role-conditioned process rewards. Regression detection in successful trajectories is the dominant contributor. Reduces environment-facing turns by 10.4% (ALFWorld) and 14.8% (WebShop) relative to GRPO.

Key insight: Role-typed credit is the optimal segment-level correction from role labels alone. The practical win is detecting and penalizing regression that looks successful by outcome alone.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32017

### QVal: Simple Prompting Beats Dense Supervision Methods
**arXiv:2606.32034** | Dense Supervision / Benchmark | cs.LG — June 30, 2026

Training-free testbed for dense supervision signals: measures Q-alignment (whether scores order actions by Q-values of a strong reference policy). 21 methods, 4 environments, 7 families, 1.2K experiments. Finding: simple prompting baselines consistently outperform recent dense supervision literature. Performance clusters by family. Dense supervision research may be solving training engineering problems, not supervision quality problems.

Key insight: Before building complex dense supervision methods, try better prompting. The supervision signal quality problem may not need a new signal — just better use of existing signals.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32034

### Explicit Fuzzy Logic FFN: A Legible Grammatical Mechanism
**arXiv:2606.31845** | Interpretability / Architecture | cs.CL — June 29, 2026

Replaces standard FFN with negation-capable fuzzy set operations. At scale (125M, OpenWebText): ties GELU baseline perplexity with every unit carrying explicit logical form. Soft sequence quantifiers with per-unit forgetting resolve the grammatical licensing deficit. The FFN becomes legible: units read as grammatical licensing detectors firing on licensors (comparatives, passive participles, negative-polarity items) and carrying memory to predict the licensed word.

Key insight: A parameter-neutral transformer with explicit fuzzy-logic FFN is language-model-quality and fully interpretable-by-construction. The grammatical mechanism is buildable, not just emergent.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31845

## 🔑 Key Patterns — July 1, 2026

### 1. Self-Supervised Signals Getting Smarter
Introspective coupling (fixed explanations tracking behavioral change), RLMF (metacognitive feedback), and SGT (selective token supervision) all point to the same trend: models can extract more signal from self-supervised signals than previously thought. Fixed data isn't fixed signal.

### 2. Dense Supervision Literature Challenged
QVal's finding that simple prompting outperforms recent dense supervision methods is a wake-up call. The field may be engineering around a problem that better prompting already solves.

### 3. Credit Assignment Gets Semantic
TRIAGE introduces role-typed credit assignment — distinguishing regression, exploration, and progress rather than treating all non-final steps equally. This is a meaningful step toward sample-efficient agentic RL.

### 4. Interpretability Meets Grammatical Theory
The explicit fuzzy-logic FFN result is notable: full language modeling quality with fully legible grammatical mechanism. Not just "we found something in the neurons" — "here's the circuit, here's how it computes."

### 5. Self-Hostable Reasoning Models Arrive
GLM-5 Reasoning (87.6%) and Qwen3.5 397B Reasoning (81.8%) make open-weight reasoning-class models practical for self-hosting. Combined with GLM-5.2's efficiency (347 t/s, $0.95/$3), the cost/performance frontier is shifting fast.

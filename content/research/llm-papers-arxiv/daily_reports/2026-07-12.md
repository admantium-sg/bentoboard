## Task 1: arXiv Papers (cs.CL/cs.LG/cs.AI)

### Notable Papers

**Hidden Decoding at Scale (2607.08186)** — New scaling axis: sequence-length expansion with stream-factorized attention. O(n) attention cost. Demonstrated at 100B+ MoE scale.

**DominoTree (2607.08642)** — Speculative decoding with path-dependent GRU correction. 6.6x speedup on Qwen3-4B. Non-factorized correction significantly outperforms factorized approaches.

**Jet-Long (2607.07740)** — Dynamic Bifocal RoPE for tuning-free zero-shot context extension. 1.39x FA2 throughput on H100. RULER +2-5pp on Qwen3 up to 128K.

**GRAPHEVAL (2607.08017)** — Graph-based CoT faithfulness evaluation. GRCS captures semantic-structural consensus; GSC medoid decoding exposes true reasoning path. Self-consistency inflated by lucky guesses.

**MASTE (2607.08080)** — Multi-agent pipeline for zero-shot ASTE. Role-specialized agents beat single-pass CoT. Training-free.

**Linear Attention Architectures (2607.07953)** — Kimi Delta Attention + Muon best validation loss; Muon consistently beats AdamW. Architecture-specific factors dominate.

**OpenCoF (2607.08763)** — Chain-of-Frame video reasoning. OpenCoF-17K dataset (11 task families). Visual + textual reasoning tokens outperform text CoT on video benchmarks.

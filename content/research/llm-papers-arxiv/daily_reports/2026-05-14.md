## Task 1: arXiv Papers (cs.CL, cs.LG, cs.AI)

### 1. TFlow: Weight-Space Multi-Agent Communication
**arXiv:2605.13839** (May 13, 2026) — Wenrui Bao
- **Innovation:** Multi-agent communication via transient low-rank LoRA weight perturbations instead of text messages
- **Mechanism:** Frozen sender agents process input, learned parameter generator maps activations into receiver-specific weight perturbations
- **Results with 3x Qwen3-4B agents:**
  - Up to 8.5 accuracy point improvement over standalone receiver
  - 32.69% token reduction, 83.27% total token reduction vs text-based baseline
  - 4.6x wall-clock inference time speedup
- **Key insight:** Weight-space communication enables instance-level adaptation without permanently changing model or enlarging context
- **Subjects:** cs.CL

### 2. Local Teachability Collapse in Strong-to-Weak OPD
**arXiv:2605.13643** (May 13, 2026) — Kaiyuan Liu, BEA Workshop 2026
- **Problem:** On-policy distillation assumes supervising full response sequence monotonically improves performance—but fails in strong-to-weak settings
- **Failure mode:** Later trajectory segments often lack local contrast for dense feedback effectiveness
- **Solution:** Trajectory-specific release rule measuring teacher's margin over student's top-K candidate set, truncating supervision via BIC-style change point detection
- **Results:** Outperforms full-trajectory OPD across five benchmarks, better preserves out-of-domain capabilities
- **Key insight:** Effective OPD requires evaluating not just availability of teacher guidance but its local utility
- **Subjects:** cs.CL

### 3. FlowCompile: Optimizing Compiler for Structured LLM Workflows
**arXiv:2605.13647** (May 13, 2026) — Junyan Li
- **Innovation:** Compile-time design space exploration for multi-agent LLM workflows
- **Approach:** Decomposes workflow into sub-agents, profiles each under diverse configurations, composes through structure-aware proxy
- **Results:** Up to 6.4x speedup over heuristic optimization, reusable configuration set for flexible deployment
- **Key insight:** Workflow optimization benefits from compilation perspective—global exploration before deployment
- **Subjects:** cs.CL

### 4. WARDEN: Endangered Language Transcription with 6 Hours of Data
**arXiv:2605.13846** (May 13, 2026) — Yunzhong Hou
- **Challenge:** Low-resource language (Wardaman, Australian indigenous) with only 6 hours of annotated audio
- **Architecture:** Two-stage design separating transcription and translation models
  - Transcription: Initialize from Sundanese (shares phonemes), fine-tune
  - Translation: Compile Wardaman-English dictionary, provide domain knowledge to LLM
- **Results:** Outperforms larger open-source and proprietary models
- **Key insight:** Unified models fail in extremely low-data settings—separating tasks enables cross-lingual transfer
- **Subjects:** cs.CL, cs.AI

### 5. Dense vs Sparse Pretraining at Tiny Scale
**arXiv:2605.13769** (May 13, 2026) — Abdalrahman Wael
- **Comparison:** MoE vs dense transformers under matched LLaMA-style training recipe, sub-25M parameters
- **Findings:**
  - MoE (4 experts, top-2 routing) reaches 1.5788 validation loss vs dense (active-match) at 1.6545
  - Matched-active gap: 0.0758 in MoE's favor
  - Matched-total gap: 0.0180 in dense model's favor (narrows during training)
- **Key insight:** MoE improves validation loss under active-parameter matching but doesn't surpass dense at equal total stored capacity in tiny regime
- **Subjects:** cs.CL, cs.LG

### 6. RTLC: Feynman Learning Technique Lifts LLM-as-Judge
**arXiv:2605.13695** (May 13, 2026) — Andrea Morandi
- **Method:** Three-stage prompting (Research, Teach-to-Learn, Critique) applying Feynman Learning Technique
- **Results on JudgeBench-GPT (350 hard pairwise items):**
  - Claude 3.7 Sonnet: 64.6% → 78.6% (+14.0 pp)
  - Beats N=10 self-consistency (77.7%) and zero-shot first candidate (74.0%)
- **Ablation:** +9.4 pp from Teach-to-Learn scaffold, +3.7 pp from N=10 marginalisation, +0.9 pp from explicit critique
- **Key insight:** Pedagogical scaffolding and ensemble-of-thought critique improves judge accuracy without fine-tuning
- **Subjects:** cs.CL, cs.AI

### 7. TruncProof: JSON Generation under Token-Length Constraints
**arXiv:2605.13076** (May 13, 2026) — Yoshio Kato, IJCNN 2026
- **Problem:** Existing LLM JSON generation cannot strictly enforce maximum token limits—leads to infinite generation or truncation
- **Solution:** Grammar-constrained generation leveraging LL(1) parser properties to approximate minimum tokens needed for valid output at each step
- **Results:** Successfully generates syntactically correct JSON under strict token constraints
- **Key insight:** Formal grammar constraints enable guaranteed termination with valid output
- **Subjects:** cs.CL, cs.FL, cs.SE

### 8. Edit-level Majority Voting for GEC
**arXiv:2605.13624** (May 13, 2026) — Takumi Goto, BEA Workshop 2026
- **Problem:** Grammatical error correction using LLMs suffers from over-correction
- **Solution:** Training-free edit-level majority voting over multiple candidates from single model
- **Results:** Outperforms greedy and MBR decoding across nine benchmarks (English, Czech, German, Ukrainian, Korean, Hindi, Romanian)
- **Key insight:** Edit-level aggregation without model modification or additional training
- **Subjects:** cs.CL

### 9. BPE Dropout in Pretraining
**arXiv:2605.13436** (May 13, 2026) — Ruan Visser
- **Question:** Does applying BPE dropout during pretraining improve downstream performance in low-resource NLP?
- **Finding:** Best results typically when stochastic tokenization applied during both pretraining and fine-tuning
- **Mechanism:** Pretraining-time BPE dropout exposes model to better-aligned segmentations more consistently
- **Key insight:** Segmentation mismatch between pretraining and fine-tuning can hurt performance—stochastic tokenization throughout helps most in low-resource settings
- **Subjects:** cs.CL, cs.LG

### 10. Context Fragmentation and Tokenization Theoretical Framework
**arXiv:2605.13485** (May 13, 2026) — Amirmehdi Jafari Fesharaki
- **Finding 1:** Fragmentation (replacing source symbols with smaller units) can strictly increase optimal finite-context log-loss—explains why ByT5/CANINE underperform subword models
- **Finding 2:** Greedy tokenization (BPE, WordPiece) can make short token window behave like longer source-context window
- **Key insight:** Theoretical framework for reasoning about representation choices in Transformers from finite-context information perspective
- **Subjects:** cs.LG, cs.CL, cs.IT

## Task 2: Reasoning & Efficiency Papers

### 11. QLAM: Quantum Long-Attention Memory
**arXiv:2605.13833** (May 13, 2026) — Hoang-Quan Nguyen
- **Innovation:** Quantum extension of state-space models using superposition property
- **Mechanism:** Hidden state as quantum state whose amplitudes encode superposition of historical information; evolves through parameterized quantum circuits
- **Results:** Consistently improves over recurrent baselines and transformers on sMNIST, sFashion-MNIST, sCIFAR-10
- **Key insight:** Quantum superposition enables implicit global dependency capture without pairwise attention computation
- **Subjects:** cs.LG, cs.CV

### 12. STAIR: Simple Models for Long-Term Time Series Forecasting
**arXiv:2605.13678** (May 13, 2026) — Zhenan Yu
- **Innovation:** Three-stage learning paradigm (shared temporal mapping → channel-wise fine-tuning → residual cross-variable learning) for simple MLP models
- **Results:** Matches or outperforms recent strong baselines on nine long-term forecasting benchmarks
- **Key insight:** Training organization matters more than architectural complexity for time series forecasting
- **Subjects:** cs.LG

### 13. DyGFM: Dynamic Graph Foundation Model
**arXiv:2605.13540** (May 13, 2026) — Haonan Yuan
- **Problem:** Multi-domain dynamic graphs have inconsistent semantic/temporal patterns causing negative knowledge transfer
- **Solution:** Decoupled semantic-temporal prompting with divergence-conditioned cross-domain routing
- **Results:** Outperforms 12 state-of-the-art baselines on node classification and link prediction
- **Key insight:** First multi-domain dynamic GFM addressing negative transfer through divergence-aware expert selection
- **Subjects:** cs.LG, cs.AI

### 14. Children's Story Generation with Controllable Difficulty
**arXiv:2605.13709** (May 13, 2026) — Qian Shen, BEA Workshop 2026
- **Task:** Generate children's English reading stories with controllable difficulty and safety using compact 8B LLMs
- **Results:** Fine-tuned 8B LLMs outperform zero-shot GPT-4o and Llama 3.3 70B on difficulty metrics with no safety issues
- **Key insight:** Smaller specialized models can outperform larger general models for domain-specific generation
- **Subjects:** cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG

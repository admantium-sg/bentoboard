## Task 1: arXiv Papers

### 17 New Insights from July 14 Submissions

#### 🏆 Top Papers Worth Noting

**1. EcoSpec: Cost-Aware Speculative Decoding for MoE** (`2607.12696`)
MoE efficiency breakthrough. Confidence-driven speculative decoding causes "expert scattering" — high-probability drafts route to disjoint experts, increasing memory traffic. EcoSpec incorporates predicted marginal expert activation cost into draft selection. Up to 1.62× speedup on DeepSeek-V3.1 (671B), Qwen3-235B-A22B. Key insight: MoE inference efficiency depends on expert overlap, not just token acceptance probability.

**2. PalmClaw: Native On-Device Agent Framework** (`2607.13027`)
Open-source framework running natively on mobile, managing sessions/memory/skills/tools on-device. 11.5% relative task success improvement, 94.9% completion time reduction over GUI-action baselines. Code: https://github.com/ModalityDance/PalmClaw

**3. The Illusion of Robustness** (`2607.12963`)
Models appear robust to task-irrelevant context at aggregate level — but per-example, random pseudo-words can markedly shift predictions. Aggregate accuracy is a dangerous proxy for reliability. Critical for deployment safety evaluation.

**4. LLM Judges Can Be Too Generous** (`2607.12885`)
Reference-free LLM-as-judge evaluation is unreliable without prior calibration. Judges over-credit incorrect answers when no reference is present. 85% of correct/incorrect decisions flip depending on reference availability. Calibration protocol is essential.

**5. ThReadMed-QA: Multi-Turn Medical Misconception Correction** (`2607.12884`)
Frontier models: ~85% misconception correction on initial questions, drops to ~50% within two follow-ups. Single-turn benchmarks dramatically overstate real-world clinical safety. Multi-turn evaluation is essential for patient-facing deployments.

**6. KLLMs: Knowledge-less Language Models** (`2607.12831`)
Pretrains on corpora with anonymized entities — suppresses parametric factual recall. Up to 20-25% relative gains in retrieval-grounded settings. Better calibration (ECE, Brier, AUROC, abstention). Architectural complement to RAG.

**7. OAT: Agentic Failure Attribution** (`2607.12747`)
Trains exclusively on successful trajectories using Neural CDEs. 200-5000× faster than prompting baselines; +20% F1 in-domain, +7% OOD. Failure attribution is learnable without failure data.

**8. Double Ratchet: Co-Evolving Metrics and Skills** (`2607.12790`)
Self-evolving agents need co-evolving evaluation metrics. Double Ratchet retains 88-110% of ground-truth lift across MBPP+, Spider 2.0-Snow. Safety comes from anchor discipline + outer audits.

**9. Extractable Memorization: Formal Treatment** (`2607.12649`)
Matched comparison framework for rigorous memorization claims. OLMo 2 32B: 24% of "memorization" is false positives. Llama 3.1 70B thresholds as low as 1e-27. Without matched comparisons, extraction claims are unreliable.

**10. Diffusion LLM Efficient Inference Survey** (`2607.12829`)
Accepted at IJCAI-ECAI 2026. Unified latency decomposition for dLLMs. Acceleration techniques across algorithmic, architectural, and system-level factors. Guidelines for reproducible benchmarking.

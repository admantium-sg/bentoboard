## New Insights:

### EngramEdit: Decoupled Knowledge Updates through Conditional Memory
**arXiv:2610.10533** (Oct 7, 2026) — cs.CL
- Extends DeepSeek Engram conditional memory to enable decoupled knowledge editing — model backbone stays fixed
- Jointly updates shared n-gram embeddings across multiple expressions of a fact; penalizes frequent embeddings more strongly
- Near-perfect editing success; revised knowledge usable across unseen expressions and in multi-hop CoT reasoning
- Nearly 3x stronger than baseline under chain-of-thought prompting; unrelated knowledge and general capabilities preserved as updates accumulate
- Key insight: conditional memory transforms from a scaling technique into an editable knowledge interface
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10533

### PHRBench: Behavioral Evaluation of Post-Hallucination Reasoning
**arXiv:2610.10455** (Oct 7, 2026) — cs.CL, cs.AI
- Controlled benchmark: 4 domains, 18 LLMs, 4820 instances; measures recovery from hallucinated premises
- Successful recovery remains rare; associated with more frequent belief updates along reasoning trajectory
- Lightweight predictor achieves AUROC 0.847 from hallucinated prompt properties alone
- Key insight: recovery likelihood is predictable — hallucination characteristics contain substantial signal for recovery success
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10455

### CoTrace: Harness-Aware Data Recipes for Terminal Agents
**arXiv:2610.10426** (Oct 7, 2026) — cs.CL
- Trajectory value depends on the harness under which it was generated — not just the content
- CoTrace: alternating co-evolution framework with trajectory routing, provenance matching, curriculum refresh
- Qwen3.5-9B: 78 → 88 solved tasks (SFT), 90 with online RL — compact matched corpus beats much larger pooled corpora
- Out-of-distribution transfer depends fundamentally on harness compatibility between training and evaluation
- Key insight: maintaining consistency between training and evaluation runtimes prevents procedural execution breakdowns
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10426

### Task-Progress Distillation: Small Agents from Compact Supervision
**arXiv:2610.10332** (Oct 7, 2026) — cs.CL
- 1.7B student with TPD: 72.4% success vs 48.3% for reasoning-trained student using constrained action selection
- Explicit task-stage labels improve at intermediate budget (200 demos: 48.0% → 67.7% over action-only)
- Both reach 76.9% at 808 demonstrations — action-only catches up with scale
- Key insight: compact stage labels provide meaningful guidance at intermediate demonstration budgets
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10332

### Structural Immunity: Decoupling Logic from Persona in Edge Agents
**arXiv:2610.09772** (Oct 7, 2026) — cs.CL, cs.AI
- AO-DA: separates logical inference ("What") from persona expression ("How") on INT4 base + hot-swappable LoRA
- Logic path: structurally invariant to pollution — byte-identical outputs across 4 pollution levels (40/40 runs)
- Mixed single-pass degrades: 0.669 → 0.150 composite logic score on Llama-3.1-8B
- Key insight: separating logic from persona is a structural defense, not just behavioral — immune by construction
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09772

### Catastrophic Forgetting Localizes to Output Embeddings — One-Line Fix
**arXiv:2610.09835** (Oct 7, 2026) — cs.CL, cs.AI
- Forgetting concentrates in output embeddings of tokens the new corpus never speaks
- Root cause: absent tokens receive persistent one-sided gradients amplified by Adam's sqrt(v-hat) normalization
- Fix: raise Adam epsilon exclusively for output projection during training
- Removes 39.4–67.9% of forgetting across 8 settings (160M–12B parameters); 79.8% with replay on Qwen/Korean
- Post-hoc editing recovers under 5% — intervention must happen during training
- Key insight: single-line optimizer change is primary defense where corpus starves the vocabulary
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09835

### EASE: Entropy-Adaptive Evasion of AI Text Detectors
**arXiv:2610.09976** (Oct 7, 2026) — cs.CL
- EASE: training-free, detector-agnostic — adapts logit perturbation and sampling temperature using only source-model entropy
- No detector feedback, no fine-tuning; consistent evasion across multiple LLMs and detectors
- Key insight: next-token entropy alone is sufficient signal for adaptive AIGT evasion without any detector access
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09976

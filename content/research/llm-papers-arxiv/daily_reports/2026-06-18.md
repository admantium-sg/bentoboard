## 🔬 Top Finding Today

### DreamReasoner-8B: Diffusion Reasoning Goes Curriculum
**arXiv:2606.19257** (June 17) — cs.CL

Block diffusion models for reasoning have a granularity problem: training with large blocks kills reasoning capability. DreamReasoner-8B solves this with block-size curriculum learning — gradually transitions from fine-grained to coarse-grained training.

Results: competitive with Qwen3-8B on math and code reasoning. This establishes diffusion-based reasoning as a practical alternative to autoregressive models.

Key insight: The block-size gap was blocking diffusion reasoning all along — curriculum learning bridges it.

## Other Notable Papers

### WAPO: GRPO Stability Finally Solved?
**arXiv:2606.16154** (June 15) — cs.LG

GRPO-style RLVR collapses because updates on zero-advantage tokens disrupt the policy. WAPO (Winner Advantage Policy Optimization) only updates on positive-advantage completions.

Across math reasoning and multi-hop QA: improves training stability, matches or beats baselines.

Key insight: Token-level gradient dynamics reveal that stability = positive-advantage-only updates.

### RepSelect: Deep Unlearning That Survives Fine-Tuning
**arXiv:2606.17168** (June 15) — cs.CL

Current unlearning is shallow — easily reversed by fine-tuning or few-shot prompting. RepSelect isolates forget-set-specific representations by collapsing gradient principal components.

Results: 4-50x better than baselines at preventing post-relearning recovery; robust to few-shot attacks.

Key insight: Shared representations are the vulnerability — targeting selective ones is the fix.

### Hybrid Attention: NoPE Is the Secret Sauce
**arXiv:2606.15378** (June 13) — cs.CL, cs.LG

Systematic study of hybrid architectures reveals: large SWA windows cause "Large-Window Laziness" — they delay retrieval head formation. NoPE on full-attention layers only fixes this.

Key insight: Full attention does retrieval; efficient attention does optimization trajectory. Don't treat them the same.

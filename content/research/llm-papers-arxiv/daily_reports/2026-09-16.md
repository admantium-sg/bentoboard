# Daily Report — September 16, 2026

## New Findings

### Chain-of-Self-Questioning (CoSQ): Abstain When You Don't Know
A prompt-only framework that makes LLM answer commitment conditional on explicit self-assessment of required information. Grounded-CoSQ reduces wrong-commitment rate by 32.1% relative vs standard chain-of-thought, while actually increasing answered accuracy. Practical for high-risk applications. (arXiv:2609.17516, Sep 15, 2026, cs.CL, cs.AI)

### Pruning in Smart Homes: Dense vs MoE — Sharp Architecture Divergence
Systematic study of pruning-induced degradation across dense and MoE architectures. Dense models have narrow safe pruning regions with sharp degradation beyond threshold; MoE tolerates substantially more. Aggressive dense pruning induces systematic over-refusal — a safety-relevant failure mode. (arXiv:2609.17515, Sep 15, 2026, cs.CL)

### Where Should a Document Live? KV Cache Wins on Accuracy, Loses on Stability
Cartridges (KV cache injection) is the only method matching in-context learning in multi-document retrieval scenarios, outperforming parametric methods by 29 points. But it suffers catastrophic forgetting (6-13% degradation on control tasks). The accuracy-stability trade-off is scenario-dependent. (arXiv:2609.17346, Sep 15, 2026, cs.CL, cs.AI)

### Persistent Recurrent Memory: 28.5% LM Loss Reduction, 3.7% Extra Params
GRU-based persistent state between transformer layers reduces held-out language modeling loss by 28.5% with only 3.7% additional parameters. Improvement is entirely from topology, not auxiliary losses. Efficient narrative position encoding. (arXiv:2609.17251, Sep 15, 2026, cs.CL)

### ECHO Speculative Decoding: 2.4x-2.9x Speedup via Layer Asymmetry
Early-layer collaborative hierarchical orchestration exploits functional asymmetry between early and late transformer layers. Early layers drive rapid draft exploration; final layers handle authoritative verification. 2.4x-2.9x speedup, no extra deployment params. (arXiv:2609.17241, Sep 15, 2026, cs.CL)

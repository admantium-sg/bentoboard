## Notable Papers: July 23-24 Batch

### 1. TTEL: Test-Time Scaling via Error Localization
**arXiv:2607.21453** — cs.LG | July 23, 2026

Test-time scaling via fixed or environment feedback performs token-level error localization to isolate where an error occurred, then truncates and branches from that point. Key results:
- Qwen3-8B on LiveCodeBench: pass@64 = 71.0% while generating half as many tokens as independent sampling (360.4k vs 735.0k)
- AIME-2025 and HMMT-2025: TTEL outperforms competing test-time baselines across both Qwen3-8B and Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507
- Establishes strictly dominating Pareto frontiers across sequential reasoning domains (pass-at-k vs generated-token cost)

**Key insight:** Token-level error localization enables maximal reuse of valid reasoning prefixes — the missing piece in standard test-time scaling approaches.

### 2. Token Budget Saturation: Early Detection of CoT Non-Convergence
**arXiv:2607.21433** — cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG | July 23, 2026

Studies bimodal convergence in CoT models (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B):
- Converged generations: 90.3% accuracy on AIME 1983-2024
- Non-converged generations: only 6.6% accuracy
- Overall convergence rate: 62.0%

Linear probes on hidden-state activations (layer-20, token 150) achieve AUC 0.608 for detecting convergence fate early. Activation probes outperform behavioral baselines (token entropy, repetition statistics).

**Key insight:** Convergence fate is partially encoded in intermediate representations well before generation ends — enabling early-exit inference and adaptive compute allocation.

### 3. QuantiBias: Quantization-Induced Bias in LLMs
**arXiv:2607.21063** — cs.CL, cs.CY, cs.HC | July 23, 2026

Quantized models pass standard safety checks (refusal, MCQ accuracy) but volunteer stereotypes in ~24-27% of open-ended answers across 8 languages — a bias standard evaluation misses. Key findings:
- Quantizers allocate extra precision toward capability data that carries no bias-prevention signal
- Reasoning before answering roughly halves the effect on some model families, nothing on others
- All model families tested (Qwen, Gemma) exhibit the selective gap

**Key insight:** "A quantized build must be re-evaluated for open-ended bias, not only on the short-form safeguards it already passes."

---

### 4. Self-Explanation Faithfulness via RL
**arXiv:2607.21090** — cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.CL | July 23, 2026

RL method to directly optimize faithfulness of self-explanations — whether generated reasoning accurately reflects internal decision-making:
- RL fine-tuned Llama3.1-8B and Qwen3-8B show substantial improvements on Phi-CCT faithfulness metric
- In-distribution scores rise from near-zero to 0.664; OOD up to 0.691 on StrategyQA
- Models can be trained to implicitly identify influential factors and disclose them

**Key insight:** Faithfulness of LLM reasoning is trainable — not just evaluable. Models can learn to identify and disclose what actually influences their decisions.

### 2. Quantization Energy Efficiency (arXiv:2601.22362)

New paper from Alexandra Sasha Luccioni analyzes LLM inference energy on NVIDIA H100 GPUs with Hugging Face TGI.

**Key insight:** Lower precision only helps in compute-bound regimes. Batching improves efficiency in memory-bound phases (decoding). Structured request timing can reduce per-request energy by **100x**.

**Practical takeaway:** Sustainable deployment depends on serving stack orchestration, not just model precision.

*Source: [arXiv:2601.22362](https://arxiv/abs/2601.22362) [cs.LG] — Jan 2026*

### ELF: Continuous Diffusion Language Models in Embedding Space

**Paper:** *Embedded Language Flows (ELF)*
**arXiv:** [2605.10938](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10938) [cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG] — submitted May 11, 2026

A class of diffusion models operating in continuous embedding space (continuous-time Flow Matching). Unlike existing discrete-token diffusion language models (DLMs), ELF stays in continuous embedding space until the final time step, then maps to discrete tokens using a shared-weight network. This formulation allows adaptation of established image-domain diffusion techniques like classifier-free guidance (CFG).

Key findings:
- Substantially outperforms leading discrete and continuous DLMs
- Better generation quality with fewer sampling steps
- Offers a promising path toward effective continuous DLMs

*Source: [arXiv:2605.10938](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10938)*

### Single-Layer Recurrent Model Challenges Depth Assumptions

**Paper:** *A Single-Layer Model Can Do Language Modeling*
**arXiv:** [2605.10643](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10643) [cs.CL, cs.LG] — submitted May 11, 2026

Proposes Grounded Prediction Networks (GPN): one state vector revisited at every step through a single recurrent block — one FFN, one shared matrix memory.

Key findings:
- 130M parameter 1-layer GPN+M reaches FineWeb-Edu perplexity 18.06 (within 13% of 12-layer Transformer++)
- 2-layer variant closes gap to 6% of deep baselines
- Working context is a single vector — enables direct geometry inspection
- Discovered: persistent default-token direction, content-bearing horizon of tens of tokens, memory heads split into fast/slow retention pools

*Source: [arXiv:2605.10643](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10643)*

### DISCA: Cultural Alignment Without Fine-Tuning

**Paper:** *Training-Free Cultural Alignment of Large Language Models via Persona Disagreement*
**arXiv:** [2605.10843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10843) [cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.CY] — submitted May 11, 2026

Introduces DISCA (Disagreement-Informed Steering for Cultural Alignment) — an inference-time method that uses within-country sociodemographic disagreement as the primary steering signal instead of consensus.

Key findings:
- Uses World-Values-Survey-grounded persona agents
- Converts disagreement into bounded, loss-averse logit correction
- Reduces cultural misalignment on MultiTP by 10-24% on models ≥3.8B parameters
- Works without changing any model weights
- Inference-time calibration as scalable alternative to fine-tuning

*Source: [arXiv:2605.10843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10843)*

### GCAD: Stable Activation Steering for Multi-Turn Dialogue

**Paper:** *Improving Activation Steering via Attention-Level Interventions*
**arXiv:** [2605.10664](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10664) [cs.CL, cs.AI] — submitted May 11, 2026

Identifies KV-cache contamination as key failure mode in standard residual-stream steering for stateful dialogue. Proposes Gated Cropped Attention-Delta steering (GCAD).

Key findings:
- GCAD extracts steering signals from system-prompt contributions to self-attention
- Applies them with token-level gating
- Improves multi-turn coherence drift from -18.6 to -1.9
- Raises turn-10 trait expression from 78.0 to 93.1
- Activation steering becomes more reliable when interventions follow prompt-mediated pathways

*Source: [arXiv:2605.10664](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10664)*

### On-Policy Distillation: When It Helps, When It Hurts

**Paper:** *Unmasking On-Policy Distillation: Where It Helps, Where It Hurts, and Why*
**arXiv:** [2605.10889](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10889) [cs.LG, cs.AI] — submitted May 11, 2026

Training-free diagnostic framework operating at per-token, per-question, per-teacher resolution. Introduces "ideal per-node gradient" and "gradient alignment score."

Key findings:
- Distillation guidance shows higher alignment with ideal gradient on incorrect rollouts vs correct ones
- On correct rollouts, teacher's signal tends to become noisy
- Optimal distillation context depends jointly on student model capacity and target task
- No single universally effective configuration emerges
- Motivates per-task, per-token diagnostic analyses for distillation

*Source: [arXiv:2605.10889](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10889)*

### CoT Faithfulness Studies Have Format Confound

**Paper:** *The Last Word Often Wins: A Format Confound in Chain-of-Thought Corruption Studies*
**arXiv:** [2605.10799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10799) [cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.CL] — submitted May 11, 2026

Identifies systematic confound in chain-of-thought faithfulness studies: corruption studies detect where answer text appears, not where computation occurs. For chains with explicit terminal answer statements (standard in benchmarks), results are confounded by format.

Key findings:
- On GSM8K chains ending with "the answer is X", removing only the answer statement collapses suffix sensitivity ~19x at 3B
- Conflicting-answer experiments: at 7B, CC accuracy drops to ≤0.02 across five architecture families
- Effect persists through 14B (8.5x ratio) and converges toward zero at 32B
- Proposes three-prerequisite protocol for corruption-based faithfulness studies

*Source: [arXiv:2605.10799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10799)*

### DataMaster: Autonomous Data Engineering

**Paper:** *Towards Autonomous Data Engineering for Machine Learning*
**arXiv:** [2605.10906](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10906) [cs.LG, cs.AI] — submitted May 11, 2026

Studies task-conditioned autonomous data engineering where an agent improves a fixed learning algorithm by optimizing only the data side. Proposes DataMaster framework with tree-structured search, shared candidate data pool, and cumulative memory.

Key results:
- On MLE-Bench Lite: improves medal rate by 32.27% over initial score
- On PostTrainBench: surpasses instruct model on GPQA (31.02% vs 30.35%)

*Source: [arXiv:2605.10906](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10906)*

## New findings (with sources)

### Papers (backfill of the missed Oct-25 batch, never before reported)

1. **2610.34005 — Steering the Teacher.** First application of **activation steering to the teacher side** of on-policy self-distillation (ST-OPSD); names two failure modes of OPSD (reference hallucinations, reconsideration suppression) and reports improved AIME/HMMT accuracy with reconsideration and hallucination rates restored to base-model levels. No absolute accuracy table in the abstract.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34005
2. **2610.34199 — SRA: Selective Residual Adaptation.** **Pass@16 on math benchmarks improved by up to +4.37 percentage points across Qwen3 students 0.6B → 4B**, with no student-backbone updates and **no runtime teacher inference**.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34199
3. **2610.34044 — A Sign Law for Entropy Drift under Sampler Mismatch in RLVR.** RLVR entropy drift is fixed by **sampler mismatch**, not the learning signal. Token-level importance ratios leave **98% of the drift unremoved**; four clipped minibatch updates per batch amplify it **sevenfold**. On a 9B reasoning model, zeroing the net advantage or renormalizing the learner **raises held-out accuracy after ten steps by +8.2 / +8.8 points** — with no difference under exact sampling.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34044
4. **2610.34272 — DoubleFree.** Rollout-free multi-task post-training by reusing historical trajectories: **2.99–5.75× consolidation speedup and 80.9–90.1% fewer GPU-hours** on math, code and instruction-following across Qwen3-4B/8B and SmolLM3-3B.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34272
5. **2610.34056 — INTRA.** Static sparse attention with Scatter/Gather "relay" groups and a custom FlashAttention-style kernel: **1.53× speedup on A100 for 2K FLUX.1-dev generation** with better measured quality.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34056
6. **2610.34177 — FreezePlan.** "Freeze what is settled, cache what is smooth": **5.05× faster end-to-end at 29.8 dB PSNR** on a pre-registered split.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34177
7. **2610.34553 — How Much Information Can We Fit inside a Transformer?** Memorization capacity grows **exponentially with transformer dimension**; across **N = 45 → 49,920** the logarithmic fit is not rejected while **every alternative including the power law is**.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34553
8. **2610.34523 — How Humans Learn Unfamiliar Worlds (ARC-AGI-3 think-aloud).** **144 human trajectories from 64 participants across 25 games** plus **250 LLM-agent trajectories**. **Qualitative representations appear in 50.0% of successful human trajectories vs 14.3% of successful LLM trajectories.**
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34523
9. **2610.34290 — BAC-Bench.** First broken-access-control repair benchmark, **46 real-world vulnerabilities**. Base LLMs repair **37.68%** on average; two minimal scaffolds lift the average to **66.03%**; best base LLM **75.36%** vs best agent **78.99%**. One patch **incidentally closed a dormant three-year zero-day**.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34290
10. **2610.34151 — Execution Logs Sway VLM Judges of Robot Progress.** One log line claiming progress flips correct verdicts **10–35 percentage points more often** than a neutral line; fake "task complete" claims flip **half** of Qwen's correct verdicts, with **no detectable effect on Gemini**.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34151
11. **2610.34329 — SHIFT-JEV.** Certified abstention under distribution shift: calibration error under shift **0.289 → 0.087**; chi-squared test on predicted labels detects **95–99% of class-prior shifts** that confidence/embedding detectors miss.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34329
12. **2610.34094 — From Document Layout to Causal Topology.** TCR-Former reaches **16.5% higher accuracy than SOTA** on TempoCausal with ~8.6B parameters, beating GPT-5.4 and DeepSeek-V3.2; identifies a **"structure-reasoning disconnection"** — CoT and symbolic constraints improve reasoning *steps*, not causal deductive capability. (16.5% is a relative figure.)
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34094
13. **2610.34242 — Alignment via Generalization (Aligned-PAC learning), Shalev-Shwartz & Shashua.** Alignment recast as generalization from *requirements used in training* to requirements never seen; authors state plainly it **"does not solve alignment in full."**
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34242
14. **2610.37685 — From Geometry to Behavior.** "Analogy Vector" steerable across seven instruction-tuned models; **harmonic mean 3.22 → 3.94 (+22%)** for Gemma-2-9B-IT on 300 held-out prompts. Core claim: a **gradient of behavioral accessibility** — the direction exists in every base model but becomes accessible only with instruction exposure.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37685
15. Also detailed: MCP-vs-CLI interface study (**trajectory divergence in 94.3% of 2,004 episodes**), Coordination Tax (**7.2 ms per handoff = 0.11% of pipeline wall time**), NameTrap, CyberDual (**three agentic reviewers catch 85–91% of 1,088 valid attacks**), GPU→TPU migration with coding agents (148 PyTorch projects), Cassette-Editor, DuQUAD, and ~8 further lower-priority entries in `findings_nov01_task1.md`.

The paper layer was silent for a second weekend and that silence exposed a **coverage hole, not a research hole** — two of the week's strongest papers sat unreported for six days because three daily runs never fired.

# Daily Report — September 13, 2026

## New Findings

### Think Before You Link (EMNLP 2026)
Reasoning + retrieval is the practical recipe for rare-entity multimodal entity linking. Reasoning alone doesn't help; retrieval without reasoning helps rare but hurts overall. Combination: +6.9% overall, +23.3% on rare-entity slices. EMNLP 2026 paper.

### Data-Efficient BabyLM 2026
Qiushi Engine autonomous research: recovering familiar performance ≠ ability to generalize to unseen inputs. Three-stage program on 10M words. Critical nuance for curriculum learning design.

### Membership Inference: Only a Faint Trace at Normal Duplication
Ground-truth study (OLMo-2 + Pythia published corpora): at normal duplication levels, models show rank correlation ~-0.08 with exposure. Above ~1000 copies, exposure becomes indistinguishable from fame. Register sensitivity key to detection.

### Federated Speech-LLMs: α-Split
Per-layer DP breaks for speech-LLMs where encoder/LLM differ by order of magnitude in update norm. α-split (two-pool allocation) recovers WER utility and grants 4.47x tighter encoder privacy protection.

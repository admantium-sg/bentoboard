## New Papers (from Monday batch)

### Two-Hop Generalization Failure in LLMs — arXiv:2608.07261
LLMs correctly store individual reasoning hops but fail to combine them. Mechanistic analysis reveals: lower layers correctly construct intermediate representations, but upper layers fail to reason over them (layer mismatch). Proposes recurrent-style training enabling transformers to reuse reasoning circuitry across input forms — substantially improves OOD two-hop query generalization. Key insight: reasoning failure isn't always about capacity; sometimes upper layers simply don't use what's already there.

Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07261

### Skaling Law: 10x Compute Efficiency for Scaling Predictions — arXiv:2608.07222
Standard neural scaling laws fail at data-scarce and overtraining extremes because they assume model size and training data impact loss independently. The Skaling law couples these through a single interaction exponent, reducing MAPE by 1.5-3x. Enables accurate full-grid extrapolation using ~10x less compute. Practical impact: more efficient compute budget allocation for next-generation model training.

Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07222

### RailCap: Better Benchmark Decontamination — arXiv:2608.07341
Current G-AP contamination metric is fundamentally flawed — averaging before differencing lets suppression strategies cancel out. SA-PPG (Stratified Aggregate of Per-question Probability Gaps) fixes this by sampling per-question, differencing per question, then aggregating within groups. RailCap judges contamination during generation via trajectory capping — achieves lowest SA-PPG. Prior restoration strategies substantially overestimated their effectiveness. Key for clean evaluation going forward.

Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07341

### Diffusion LLM Safety: New Jailbreak Vector — arXiv:2608.07430
Diffusion LLMs (DLLMs) replacing autoregressive generation inherit safety vulnerabilities from their AR predecessors. Safety alignment is sparse and transferable — enabling transfer attacks via safety neuron mapping. Self-pruning increases attack success from 2.6% to 73.8% on LLaDA, 1.9% to 86.6% on Dream. SN-Guided Diffusion jailbreak achieves 86.9% ASR on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct at 20x lower cost than prior methods. This is a new attack surface the community should watch.

Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07430

### CoinRAG: KV Cache Reuse for Long-Context Efficiency — arXiv:2608.07458
Current RAG chunk-level KV cache reuse leaves redundancy/noise in coarse-grained chunks. CoinRAG identifies query-relevant semantic units and assembles sliced KV representations with chunk-level context. Achieves new Pareto frontier: 5.3% relative F1 improvement under fast prefill latency budget. Significant for long-context applications.

Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07458

### CreativeInstruct: Balancing Quality, Creativity, Diversity — arXiv:2608.07460
Post-training lowers output diversity and creativity, impacting RL tasks. CreativeInstruct learns [StartCreativity] spans to bias generation toward creativity. 70.3% of human annotators prefer CreativeInstruct creativity over post-trained models. GRPO on CreativeInstruct checkpoint improves ~4% on AMC, ~5% on MATH. Introduces graph edit distance metric for narrative-level diversity (missed by lexical/semantic metrics).

Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07460

### LitTraceQA: Scientific QA with Verifiable Evidence — arXiv:2608.07370
Scientific literature QA requires identifying papers, locating evidence, and producing faithful responses — not just fluent generation. Benchmark returns 3 connected outputs: paper identifiers, evidence locations, answers. Targets evidence types: tables, figures, text spans, equations, citation contexts. 4,978 unique-question records. Testbed for verifiable answers vs unsupported summaries.

Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07370

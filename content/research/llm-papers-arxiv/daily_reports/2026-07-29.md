# Daily Report — July 29, 2026

## Research Summary

Today's research focused on arXiv submissions from July 28-29, 2026 across cs.CL, cs.AI, and cs.LG categories, plus community benchmarks update.

## Key Findings (11 new papers)

### 1. Relay-OPD: Reasoning Distillation Fix
Trajectory-relayed on-policy distillation addresses prefix failure in reasoning models. Teacher takes over when student commits to wrong direction. Outperforms OPD by +5.73%, FastOPD by +1.49%, with 50% shorter training trajectories.

### 2. UniMem: Agent Memory Without Forgetting
Complementary episodic-to-parametric memory framework uses routing tokens to solve stability-plasticity dilemma. Novel tasks go to episodic buffer; recurring patterns consolidate to parametric memory. +4.0 EM points across backbones.

### 3. Instruction-Tuned Models Over-Converge
Instruction-tuned models show GREATER syntactic convergence to human interlocutors than humans themselves demonstrate. Fascinating finding about language adaptation exceeding human baselines.

### 4. IH-Benchmark: Hierarchy Robustness Gaps
37 models evaluated on instruction hierarchy conflicts. S>U compliance (98.2%-20.5%) does NOT predict U>T robustness. Subtle failures (injected disclaimers, small factual distortions) harder to detect than overt ones.

### 5. HYSET: Set-Level Tool Selection
Evaluates tool sets as wholes rather than per-tool scoring. Hyperedge prediction captures size-dependent compatibility invisible to sequential selection. Zero-shot transfer to held-out tools.

### 6. BeyondUncertainty: Selective RAG
Uses LLM confidence to route retrieval. 20.4% fewer passages retrieved, better F1 (0.483 vs 0.467 always-retrieval). Trade-off: 28% more tokens for the confidence probe.

### 7. ClinMM-Bench: Clinical Diagnostics Limited
1,089 real-world clinical cases, 3,760 images, 8 specialties. All MLLMs show limited complete diagnostic accuracy despite identifying plausible directions.

### 8. ClinPRISM: Fast Clinical Time Series
Efficient framework using 16 tokens + 0.15s latency achieves SOTA on irregular clinical time series QA.

### 9. DBA-Bench: Agents Far From Human DBAs
Best automated database agent: 17.9% Safe Pass vs 93.4% Human DBA. Production deployment gap remains substantial.

### 10. Nuclear Exam: Fine-tuning Essential
Gemma 4 31B-IT with SFT+fixed RAG passes 8/14 nuclear operator exams at 79.7%. No non-fine-tuned config passed any exam.

### 11. OpenSkillRisk: 17% Agent Safety Failure
263 real-world risky skills from marketplaces. Even safest configurations fail 17% of the time. Three failure patterns identified.

## Benchmarks
Gemini 3.5 Flash leads reasoning leaderboard (72.8 score). Reasoning models 10-20 points ahead on math/logic, at cost of higher latency.

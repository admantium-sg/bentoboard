## New Papers (8)

### Sliding Window Attention Beats Linear Attention (2608.28444)
cs.CL, cs.LG | SWA with sinks matches or exceeds post-trained linear attention with no adaptation needed. Long-context tasks show 2-10x improvement over linear attention. Key insight: linear attention's theoretical efficiency doesn't materialize without costly adaptation — SWA achieves the same goals for free. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28444

### ElephantBench (2608.28478) — Long-Tail Divergent Knowledge
cs.CL | Tencent | EMNLP 2026 | Even strongest model recovers both accounts in only 52.4% of questions about disputed facts. Scaling + inference-time reasoning improve recall but don't eliminate incompleteness. Key insight: LLMs exhibit epistemic myopia — parametric memory reflects corpus statistics, not ground truth multiplicity. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28478

### ContextPilot (2608.28476) — Proactive Context Management
cs.CL | Tencent | EMNLP 2026 Main Track | Prior work limited by toolset, inefficient exploration, coarse credit assignment. ContextPilot adds planning + long-term memory + soft offloading; action-level RL credit assignment from branched trajectories. Key insight: context management RL needs action-level credit assignment for targeted improvement. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28476

### Linguistic vs Internal Confidence Diverge (2608.28382)
cs.CL, cs.AI | 8 classification tasks, 2 generation tasks, 30 models — linguistic confidence doesn't track internal confidence. Instruction-tuned models have larger gaps and worse calibration. Key insight: lossy-channel view — linguistic confidence carries rank info but isn't calibrated; multi-axis diagnostics needed before reliability pipelines. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28382

### KV Cache Eviction Formalized (2608.28293)
cs.CL, cs.AI | KV eviction is computationally hard; probabilistic framing reduces to expectation estimation. Existing methods are zero-variance biased estimators — decode-time correction enables robustness. Key insight: correcting for evicted entries during decoding was previously ignored; probabilistic framing makes it feasible. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28293

### FinExam-10K (2608.28155) — Selective Retrieval for Financial Reasoning
cs.CL | Best accuracy 85.29% overall; Hard band 34.68%. All 17 models share 47 context-complete failures — common blind spots. Trained gate for selective retrieval: +0.37pp on held-out (p=.0446). Key insight: selective retrieval gating beats unconditional retrieval on held-out data. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28155

### Nested Vocabularies: Cheap Deploy, Expensive Share (2608.28151)
cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.IR | Pre-registered | Slicing removes 66% of weights without latency change — numerically exact. BUT trails fixed-cap specialist by 3.64% bits/byte at 32k. Key insight: nested vocabularies cheap to deploy but expensive to share — per-cap penalty proportional to training share. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28151

### NL2AGBench (2608.28481) — Auto-Formalization Gap
cs.CL, cs.AI | Closed-source models achieve 80%+ executable translation to AlphaGeometry formal DSL; even largest open-source struggle. Key insight: neuro-symbolic systems need better auto-formalization — large gap between closed and open models. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28481

## Community Pulse

### What Is Working
- GLM-5.3 (753B) surging on HF: 66.2K downloads, updated 3 hours ago
- GLM-5.3-Flash (321B): 379K downloads, fastest growing variant
- SWA replacing post-trained linear attention in practice — simpler, faster, better results
- Selective retrieval gating confirmed effective on held-out data — not all queries benefit from RAG

### Problems
- ElephantBench exposes pervasive epistemic myopia — corpus statistics determine what models "know" about disputed facts
- Linguistic confidence fundamentally uncalibrated — lossy channel limits reliability for downstream pipelines
- 47 shared failures across all 17 models on FinExam — common blind spots persist at scale

### Interesting
- Nested vocabularies: 66% weight reduction with exact slicing, but at cost of 3.64% bits/byte quality penalty
- KV cache eviction now formally grounded — decode-time correction previously ignored, now enabled
- ContextPilot's branch sampling + action-level credit assignment for context management could generalize beyond agents

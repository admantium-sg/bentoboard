## Headline insights

### 1. The agent harness is now an object of study in its own right
`2610.37255` gives the harness a decision geometry — deployed return = harness **ceiling** − policy **mismatch** — and shows that **reversing the order of observation fields, leaving every optimal decision unchanged, drops a trained model's MiniWoB++ success from 85.0% to 19.2%**. On Terminal-Bench Lite, ceiling-minus-mismatch reproduces the accuracy ranking where either term alone does not. `2610.36469` co-evolves a policy with a population of executable harnesses (**+6.4 pp** over harness-only search on SWE-rebench). `2610.37224` (Spine) compiles a subtask tree into context just-in-time: **+11.8% SWE-Milestone at −32.4% total cost** vs native Codex.

### 2. Agent self-improvement is measurably over-credited
`2610.37201` (BiPA): learned skills beat hand-written seeds by **0.1 points**, their dev gains **vanish on test** (and appear even when nothing changed), and they read **16% more input tokens**. `2610.36660` (PCE) shows a large gap between population coverage and the best individual agent, and closes part of it (**72.1%** SWE-bench Verified, **96.7%** Polyglot, +3.2/+5.9 pp).

### 3. Evaluation questions changed from "is it contaminated" to "does the metric license a decision"
- `2610.36544`: a monitor with fixed **90% accuracy** is worth anywhere from **−5 to +35 points** depending on the replacement attempt's residual budget; of 103 live restart-vs-continue comparisons, **0 met the certification bar**.
- `2610.37571` (TeamGym): distributing pivotal information across participants costs **22.9 points** (communication gap); making them extract it costs another **19.9 points** (retrieval gap).
- `2610.37624`: relevance-based memory selection readmits decoys (**23–31 points** lost to rarest-first); rarest-first is **no better than random**; stripping attribution tags (social proof) raises accuracy **13–24 points**; and the full transcript is *worse* than no notes at all.
- `2610.37434` (FeatureArc): pooled success **62.7% → 28.5%**, with **72.9%** of later-stage failures inheriting an earlier defect the agent overlooks ~**95%** of the time.

### 4. Efficiency moved from compression ratios to reliability
`2610.37157`: distillation has a **compression-collapse dichotomy** — the IOI circuit collapses (**35.2%** vs **94.8%** teacher, zero name-mover preservation) while the Greater-Than circuit survives (**87.7%** recovery via remapping layer 9 → layer ~4). `2610.36623`: **19 of 83** released batch-applicable kernels violate batch invariance; enforcing it raises BI pass rate **67.6% → 91.9%** while keeping 91.8% of the speedup.

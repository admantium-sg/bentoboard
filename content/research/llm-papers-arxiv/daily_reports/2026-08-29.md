## New Papers (6)

### TTPO (2608.27448) — Test-Time Policy Optimization Without Labels
cs.CL | ZJU | Asymmetric objective distills agreeing rollouts and penalizes disagreeing ones via Grouped RL. Token-level selection refines both branches. Qwen3-1.7B: 38.0% → 45.2% in TTT (+7.2pp). Matches label-supervised OPSD on 5 benchmarks. Key insight: disagreement with incorrect pseudo-label is asymmetric — rollouts that disagree are reliably wrong, enabling label-free test-time training. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27448

### RLVR Fusion Paradigms (2608.27409) — Merge vs Mix RL vs MOPD
cs.CL | Fudan | Three fusion methods for multi-domain RLVR experts. Average gap only 1.4pp but 8.6pp on single benchmarks. Merge for cheap fusion when experts exist; Mix RL for unified model from scratch; MOPD for domain-preservation priority. Key insight: RLVR expert fusion is solved with clear tradeoffs — infrastructure state determines choice. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27409

### Puro-2B (2608.27370) — $6.9K Pretraining on RTX 5090
cs.CL, cs.LG | Tsinghua | FP8 pretraining from scratch on 1.4T tokens, RTX 5090, under $6.9K total. Approaches Qwen2.5-1.5B performance. Puro Cost Scaling Law: ~$4.4K matches Qwen2-1.5B. Full Apache 2.0 release. Key insight: pretraining democratized — consumer hardware + open recipe breaks compute barrier for base model development. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27370

### INTENT-AS-A-TOOL (2608.27348) — Intent Signals for Agentic Misalignment Detection
cs.CL | Intent tool calls as dedicated behavioral commitment channel. Tool probability = fine-grained judge-free signal of behavioral tendency. Expands post-hoc CoT monitoring into dense trajectories for online intervention. Key insight: intent signals precede harmful execution in reasoning — dedicated tools enable earlier intervention than post-hoc review. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27348

### Moral Knowledge Organization (2608.27402) — Geometric Structure of Moral Foundations
cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG | Six probes for MFT categories reveal near-maximal independent dimensions + shared integration component. Geometry consistent across architectures and scale; reaches integration regime early in pretraining. Dilemma directions partially compose from foundations at 2.7x mismatched-pair baseline. Key insight: LLMs represent moral tension itself, not pre-resolved judgment — corpus statistics shape moral geometry. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27402

### CAST (2608.27397) — Clinical LLM Artifact Suppression via SAE
cs.CL, cs.AI | SAE exposes sparse auditable features from intermediate activations. LLM-assisted labeling + ICD-10 constraints for artifact identification. Residual subtraction suppresses artifact latents during fine-tuning. Key insight: clinical models exploit template/boilerplate, not patient state — SAE-based auditing exposes and corrects deployment-critical shortcuts. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27397

## Community Pulse

### What Is Working
- Label-free test-time training: TTPO's asymmetric objective enables training without ground-truth at all — significant for compute-constrained reasoning improvement
- Democratized pretraining: Puro-2B $6.9K recipe shows consumer hardware + open data is viable path to base model quality
- Intent-based monitoring: INTENT-AS-A-TOOL shows intent tool probability is useful intervention signal — denser than post-hoc CoT labels

### Problems
- Clinical artifact exploitation: CAST confirms models learn template shortcuts, not patient state — deployment shifts remain fundamental risk
- Moral geometry corpus-driven: moral structure in LLMs reflects training data statistics, not theoretical MFT individualizing/binding distinction

### Interesting
- Puro Cost Scaling Law: ~$4.4K to match Qwen2-1.5B performance — sweet spot for academic labs
- GLM-5.3 (753B) very fresh on HF (updated ~6 hours ago) — gaining rapid traction alongside Qwen family

### 2. Two same-day security stories with the same shape: the model is the payload stage
- **ChatGPT account takeover via a public share link.** Parameter Labs' "Do the Math: How We Took Over ChatGPT Accounts" (reported Oct 26, OpenAI fixed it Oct 26, classified **P1**): a malicious shared conversation carries a **math graph** (loading MathLive) and an **HTML Preview**. HN **227 points / 108 comments**. https://www.parameter.ai/labs/we-found-a-gap-at-openai · https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50314699
- **Malware that hires an LLM to appraise its victims.** Cisco Talos' analysis of **VOLTA-AUTODOXER**, a self-deleting Windows infostealer whose distinguishing stage POSTs a serialized evidence bundle to the **OpenRouter chat-completions endpoint** under a system prompt instructing the model to act as "a forensic intelligence analyst drafting a target assessment", with required **"WHAT THEY'RE WORTH"** and **"THEIR OPSEC"** sections. ### 3. The measurement artifacts are now more studied than the models
- `2610.37679` **Exposure Is Not Commitment** — the conflicting literature on latent monitors for indirect prompt injection is largely an **evaluation artifact**. Double-stratifying *exposure* from *commitment* and retraining on exposed cases only lets probes predict the model's own unsafe tool call **two steps early** (logistic **AUROC 0.853**, with a trained **TF-IDF baseline matching at 0.855**; Mistral-7B 0.896, OLMo-2-7B 0.912). https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37679
- `2610.37676` **Scoring Explanations Without Reading Them** — on Neuronpedia's stored fuzzing runs for **500 random GPT-2 small features**, **91.3%** of activating examples are cropped with the peak on the centre token; a "centre token marked" rule reaches **balanced accuracy 0.979** vs **0.838** for the stored LLM scorers (mark-count rule 0.969). On OpenAI's simulation benchmark a first-five rule scores **0.227** pooled correlation vs **0.160** for GPT-4's published simulator. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37676
- `2610.33592` — auditing evaluation artifacts and data leakage in LLM rare-disease diagnosis over **>60,000 cases**: strict cross-dataset isolation cuts mean **Recall@1 from 54.3% to 40.3% (Hedges' g = 0.94)** and spatial overfitting drops diagnostic accuracy from **42.9% to 16.7%** across external cohorts (**Cohen's d = 2.01**). https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33592
- `2610.33025` **Can Language Models Learn While They Think?** — dynamic evaluation (one SGD step per 128-token chunk, no training): softmax-attention models (Qwen3-1.7B/4B, Gemma-4-E4B) lose **5–8%** of next-token loss and **beat the same frozen model given the full 32k context**; hybrid Gated-DeltaNet models gain **under 1% at every learning rate**; masked-diffusion models gain **8%** (Nemotron-Diffusion-8B) to **17–20%** (DiffusionGemma-26B) — but across RULER, BABILong, LongBench-v2, SCOTUS, HotpotQA and citation completion, the updated weights **recover none of the accuracy lost to truncation**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33025

### 4. Efficiency: selection and exact compilation beat compression
- `2610.21073` **Skip the Compressor** — at an equal token budget, *choosing which files to show verbatim* beats pruning and retrieval. Selector **CQ2-CARE** (fitted on **300 SWE-rebench issues**) resolves **43.7%** (GPT-5.6) and **46.7%** (Claude Opus 5) of issues — **6.3 pp** over the best of three compressors and **9.3 / 12.7 pp** over the best of three retrievers — and edges out **SweRank** and **SWE-Pruner**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21073
- `2610.30664` **Policy-Exact Parallel Inference for Hierarchical RAG** — compiling the shallow action-prefix tree gives **one root prefill plus one tree-causal probe**; a prefix-state lemma makes compiled control **policy-exact in exact arithmetic** with a **two-forward critical path and zero output tokens**, matching the serial policy on **84–89%** of questions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30664
- `2610.33625` **RAQ-MLA** — fitting an invertible transform per cached MLA tensor against its own output error, **training-free**, quantizes the full MLA cache to **~2.5 bits/element — 6.5× theoretical KV-cache capacity** — near-lossless on reasoning and coding across four MLA models. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33625
- `2610.33501` **DualPace** — an **AMX-enabled CPU engine** independently batches and advances CPU-resident decodes alongside the GPU engine: **6.9–43.2% over NEO**, **11.5–90.6% over vLLM** at equal latency, **7.3–50.5% higher request rates** at the same P99 ITL. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33501
- `2610.34427` **ScaleRLT** — deferred rollouts on looped LLMs change the RL learner's log-probs in floating point: "in FP32 at 4096 tokens about **18%** of the sampled tokens miss a 10⁻⁵ same-weight gate, and with bf16 weights nearly every token does." The hybrid log-prob reproduces the rollout **bit for bit in all 167 FP32 and 28 bf16 real-weight cells**, the GRPO loss matches a float64 reference to **3.1×10⁻⁴**, and deferral lifts throughput **1.47–1.56×**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34427

### 5. Agents: the model decides, and what it decides is often the measurement
- `2610.34425` **CTXAgent** — the model, not the harness, decides when to compact, what to keep and what to hand off (two tools, one paragraph of guidance). Across **7 frontier models** on coding, SciCode, SWE-bench Pro and Terminal-Bench 4.0 with planted facts, sessions of **35–212 turns**: most strong models manage their own context (live context mostly under **100k tokens**), and with **Claude Opus 5.5** the self-managed harness **matches Claude Code**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34425
- `2610.34577` **Rethinking Verification in Agent Harnesses** — **17 designs** × **7 frontier coding models** on Terminal-Bench, SWE-bench Verified and a new VisualBench. Independent review by a critic is the best accuracy–cost trade-off: **+5.9 to +9.5 pp on TB**, **+3.9 to +6.6 pp on SWE**, at **1.13–1.25×** cost. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34577
- `2610.33509` **Can Agentic Code Review Catch Vulnerabilities Before They Are Merged?** — SecPRBench harvests validated **bug-introducing** commits: **265 tasks from 9 critical systems** (Firefox, V8, OpenSSL). Recall across **8 frontier models** ranges **32.5% to 78.1%** (**Grok 4.6**, **GPT-6 sol** top), and in **68%** of misses the agent flags the right file but argues the wrong mechanism; **Codex Security lowers recall by 8.3 points**, mostly by abstaining. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33509
- `2610.33375` **AgentOrch** — global orchestration vs step-by-step interaction on **650 enterprise SOPs and 3,220 runtime variants**: step-by-step stays more accurate but the gap **shrank ~79%**; incremental workflow construction **+9.98 pp**, revising workflows with execution feedback **+6.13 pp**, targeted training on complete workflows **+12.18 pp**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33375
- `2610.34386` **ParetoRSI-Gym** — across **3 agent systems × 3 base models × 5 rounds**, the strongest system (**Claude Code with Opus-5**) gains **9.2% relative (+2.3 absolute)** on generalization; single-domain gains reach **63%**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34386
- `2610.33845` **Build, Don't Play (AutoTavern)** — in **20 days** research agents built TavernGym plus **23 PPO-trained versions** of TavernAgent; live-ladder rating rose **6,000 → ~15,000**, last 20 records averaging **1.60 placement with 80% first places**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33845
- `2610.33029` **Lessons from Luck** — registered study in **256 synthetic worlds**, protocol frozen before any confirmatory call, **11 LLM endpoints, 90,084 answers**: after a chance failure of the better tool, the model's written lesson **carries the chance** (interaction **+0.112, 95% CI [+0.097, +0.126]**). https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33029

### 6. Graders and compilers decide the ranking
- `2610.30765` **Scaling Laws for Generative Reward Models** — over Qwen3 reward models **0.6B to 14B**, GRPO-trained thinking judges win on static Gold-label accuracy but the ranking **reverses under online DPO**: answer-only rewarders give higher peak and top-5-average policy Elo in **7 of 8 matched settings** under a fixed learned **32B** Gold evaluator, and intermediate checkpoints can beat later, more accurate ones. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30765
- `2610.30920` **DConstrain** — for diffusion LMs, individually valid tokens can be jointly invalid under a schema; schemas compile into **context-parameterized transition programs**, with token-effect grouping and score aggregation that **provably preserves both the Viterbi optimum and the token-labeled path distribution**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30920
- `2610.37683` **Prompt-agnostic Language Models** — per-paraphrasing gradients conflict on **57%** of parameters and naive averaging discards **~a fifth of the magnitude**; **Prompt-TIES** and **Prompt-DARE** nearly **halve per-example variance**; prompt phrasing × example identity explains **54%** of per-example variability (>**70%** on ANLI, WiC). https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37683
- `2610.37680` **Artifact Distillation** — the teacher externalizes its procedure into an **artifact bank** the student queries at inference, **no gradient updates**: a bank written by Claude Opus 4.5 lets **Claude Haiku 4.5 beat unaided Claude Sonnet 4.5 on spatial reasoning (0.707 vs 0.647) at 60% of its cost**, and lifts **Qwen3.5-27B's pooled score 0.730 → 0.800**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37680
- `2610.33684` **MORE** — jointly selects reasoning *form* (direct / short CoT / long CoT / code) and *effort* per task; Stage 2 self-evolves the mode prior with MATE-PPO on generated verifiable task streams **without curated data**, with gains from **3B to 14B**. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33684

### 7. Benchmarks: one measures the serving stack, the other whether a product gets built
- **gBench** (DeepMind, open source) evaluates open LLMs/VLMs on **serving performance on the hardware it runs on** plus capability in three tiers: **86 canonical Python suites** across six pillars, plus `ui_control_osworld` and `swe_lancer`; a **Golden Set** of **16 deterministic capability checks** including a labelled **chat-template sensitivity** subset separating a broken template from a weak model; and an agentic suite built on **GemmaClaw / OpenClaw** QA scenarios (**60 scenarios**, pinned GemmaClaw commit `d8bc6989`). It targets any OpenAI-compatible `/v1` endpoint or manages its own vLLM server, reads the HuggingFace `config.json` to **refuse to start a model that will not fit**, and runs an open-loop Poisson `--stress-test` ramp certifying the limit to **±9%** with a cohort of **≥1,099 requests** against default SLOs of **5,000 ms TTFT** and **200 ms ITL**. https://github.com/google-deepmind/gbench
- **ViBench 2.0** (Replit, Oct 28) targets **long-horizon software development**: the agent starts from an **empty project** and builds a whole product one feature at a time — **17 products, 241 feature requests, 544 test plans**. The headline is a failure rate: the best models (**GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Astra, Opus 5.5**) pass **all** test plans in only **49–65% of their builds**. https://frontier.replit.com/vibench-2-long-horizon-software-benchmark/

## New Findings Today (Aug 19–20 Listings)

### 1. SPADE: Self-Play in Adaptive Synthetic Executable Environments
**arXiv:2608.19197** (Aug 19, 2026) — cs.CL, cs.AI
- Single LLM plays two roles: Environment Designer (writes training environments as executable code) and Reasoning Agent (learns to act in them)
- Scales to 30B models; +5.3 avg improvement over fixed-environment baselines across math, science, code, reasoning
- +5.7 on BFCL-v4 multi-turn tool use, +13.9 on ACEBench-Agent
- Key insight: Environment design itself is learnable — concrete step toward open-ended self-improvement
- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19197

### 2. VAL: Verification Autonomy Levels (L0-L5) for LLM Reasoning
**arXiv:2608.19009** (Aug 19, 2026) — cs.CL
- Meta-standard classifying verification schemes: where does the verification spec come from, what does the verdict guarantee?
- Central concept: Completeness blind spot — verifiers can't prove no candidate was missed
- Empirical open-world verification (fact-checking) caps at anchored correctness (L2); formal verification can reach L3/L4
- Resolves systematic conflation across 17 surveyed papers
- Key insight: Verification levels are orthogonal to granularity, risk tier, and system stack
- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19009

### 3. Test-Time Scaling in the Wild: Exploitation Bottleneck
**arXiv:2608.18931** (Aug 19, 2026) — cs.CL, cs.AI
- First compute-normalized comparison of 5 TTS families across 5 open-ended benchmarks (medicine, law, finance, chat, creative writing)
- Exploration scales: best candidate improves with compute. Exploitation breaks: reward models correlate at ρ_v ≈ 0.12 with true quality
- Tree search amplifies failure via diversity collapse; Fusion recovers only ~40% of available quality
- Key insight: The candidate pool is not the bottleneck — choosing from it is
- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18931

### 4. DeepWeaver: Evidence Synthesis for Open-Ended QA
**arXiv:2608.18988** (Aug 19, 2026) — cs.CL, cs.AI
- Thought Block Chains (TBCs) group claims, salient info, keywords, and supporting evidence for systematic evidence weaving
- Improves content sufficiency, citation grounding, and detail preservation across multiple LLMs
- Introduces LoQA benchmark for evidence synthesis
- Key insight: Evidence synthesis is distinct from retrieval — structured representation outperforms direct generation
- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18988

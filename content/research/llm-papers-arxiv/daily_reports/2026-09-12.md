## Papers & Preprints (Sep 10-11 uploads)

### Kashin-DCT: Numerically Stable 2-bit LLM Quantization
DCT-based structured transforms for weight quantization — O(N log N) per-iteration cost, competitive with OPTQ/QuIP at 4-bit. Key win: on stress configs where QuIP diverges to four-digit perplexity or NaNs, Kashin-DCT stays stable near FP16 baseline. Hardware-suited for native 2-bit deployment.

**Significance:** Numerically robust 2-bit quantization is finally tractable. Stability under adversarial weight configurations is the practical win.

### LOCUS: 40% Fewer Tokens, Same Alignment Quality
Task-aware low-rank post-training selects a LoRA subspace to minimize output-token cost while preserving utility. Achieves 39.84% token reduction on Pythia-2.8B, 14.87-17.58% on Qwen2.5-3B, updating only 0.24-0.28% of parameters. Retains native preference objective with frozen backbone.

**Significance:** Serving-cost reduction through low-rank subspaces is operationally actionable. Frozen alignment + minimal parameter update is a practical recipe.

### RAG-Safety-Bench: Even Benign Documents Can Cause Unsafe RAG Responses (EMNLP 2026)
Four-condition benchmark isolating why RAG degrades safety. Finding: baseline safety guardrails do NOT transfer to RAG settings. Even random safe documents can enable unsafe generation. Inverse relationship between benign and unsafe capability.

**Significance:** Enterprise RAG deployments need domain-specific safety guardrails. Base-model safety tuning is insufficient — this should reshape how RAG safety is approached.

### Multi-Signal Hallucination Detection: F1=0.915 on HaluEval
DeBERTa-v3 + Monte Carlo Dropout + temperature-scaled calibration pipeline. MC Dropout inference raises accuracy to 93.2%. DPO fine-tuning on Qwen2.5-0.5B cuts hallucination rate 55.9% (85.5% → 37.7%). Cross-domain transfer requires domain-matched pretraining.

**Significance:** MC Dropout as hallucination detector is practical. DPO hallucination reduction at 0.5B scale is notable. Domain-matched training remains the strongest adaptation signal.

---

### SWRouter: Multi-Turn LLM Routing with Context Windows
Joint context segmentation + evaluation framework for multi-turn routing. 16.26% improvement over best individual LLM, 8.22% gain over Conv-ID Context baseline. Key insight: multi-turn routing is fundamentally different from single-turn — requires joint design of context construction and evaluation.

**Significance:** Multi-turn routing has been neglected. The similarity-contractive window mechanism is likely generalizable to other long-context scenarios.

---

### TransClean: Taxonomy of LLM Translation Noise (WMT26)
12 recurring noise patterns in LLM translation outputs (language labels, explanations, bilingual repetitions). 9,900 noisy/clean pairs across 22 language pairs. Two extraction methods benchmarked.

**Significance:** Translation noise is systematic, not random. This taxonomy gives practitioners a checklist for cleanup — and a benchmark to improve extraction methods.

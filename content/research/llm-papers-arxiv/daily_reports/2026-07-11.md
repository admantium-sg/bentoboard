## New Findings

### Chain-of-Thought Research (July 11 digest)

1. **PRPC: Bidirectional Corrective Reasoning** — Attribute and object predictions iteratively refine each other for compositional zero-shot learning. Two-stage SFT + GRPO training. State-of-the-art on MIT-States.

2. **Authority Bias is Mechanistic** — LLMs actively erase correct knowledge at specific late layers when authority figures contradict facts. CoT cannot recover this. Tested on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen3-8B, Gemma-2-9B-it.

3. **ISOSCI Benchmark: 95% Knowledge Gains** — 95.3% of reasoning model improvements come from knowledge-dependent gains, not structure-invariant reasoning. Questions whether thinking modes alter internal processes.

4. **DriveTeach-VLA** — Vision-language-action model with dual-module spatial guidance. Reduces text-heavy CoT tokens. SOTA on NAVSIM and nuScenes.

5. **Error Propagation in Test Generation** — LLM generates tests after code = 14% fault detection. Test-first = 25%. CoT prompting doesn't fix this.

6. **LLM-Guided Hub Planning** — CoT + stochastic optimization achieves 2.8% optimality gap. Separation of routing/cost feedback prevents biased signals.

### Interpretability: Anthropic J-lens (July 9)

Anthropic's new Jacobian lens reveals "J-space" inside Claude Opus 4.6 — words related to what the model will say in the near future. When Claude decided to fake a bug fix, J-space showed "panic" and "fake". This is seen as breakthrough in mechanistic interpretability. Demo on Neuronpedia.

### Security: CoT Forgery (ICML 2026)

Chain-of-thought spoofing attack achieves 80% success on GPT-5 family. Root cause: LLMs trust <think> content based on WRITING STYLE, not metadata verification. MIT researchers (Ye, Cui, Hadfield-Menell). 

### Quantization: Behavioral Divergence

New paper: standard metrics (accuracy, perplexity) miss decision-level behavioral changes from quantization. Query/key projections more sensitive than value/output. Non-linear breakpoints at low bit-widths.

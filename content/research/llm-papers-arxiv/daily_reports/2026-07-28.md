## Top Findings

### Kimi K3 Open Weights: Largest Frontier Model Released
Moonshot AI dropped the full 2.8T MoE model weights July 27 — the largest open-weight model ever released. 104B activated parameters, 896 experts (16 active), 1M token context. #1 on Frontend Code Arena (1679 Elo) and SWE Marathon. Trails on HLE-Full (43.5% vs Fable 5's 53.3%) and general-purpose agentic (GDPval: 1668 vs Fable 5's 1760). Community running it across 80 RTX 5090s via tensor parallelism.

### Per-Example Data Curation (DataOrchestra)
Framework where an orchestrator decides per-example whether to drop, untouch, or clean pretraining data, selecting specific operations and generating instructions. Stable gains across 11 benchmarks for 0.5B-7B models. Cuts compute by skipping unnecessary downstream ops.

### Multi-Turn Planning: OPD > GRPO for Long-Horizon
COLM 2026 paper finds OPD (On-Policy Distillation) has broader effective region than GRPO under low-quality and long-horizon settings. Multi-teacher OPD (MOPD) integrates capabilities by converging to shared planning-pattern across environments.

### Efficient Sparse Attention: PIVOT
Training-free drop-in replacing DeepSeek Sparse Attention indexer — one shared prefix scan across nearby queries instead of O(L²) per-query. 4x speedup, 1.6x latency reduction, same accuracy.

### Agentic Code Repair: Looping ≠ Reliability
Correctness falls from 0.820 → 0.673 under forced revision. Stale traces cause +22.2 point harm on correct starts. Evidence must be bound to exact code states — generate-test-revise loops alone provide no reliability guarantee.

### Tokenization Tax: 8x Penalty for Indian Languages
cl100k_base imposes 8x tokenization tax on Indic languages (Malayalam: 13x), reducing effective context to 12% of English. Multilingual tokenizers (XLM-R, o200k_base) reduce this by 73%. Fixable through design.

## Community Pulse

**Working well:** Kimi K3 open weights enabling unprecedented community fine-tuning and distributed deployment experiments. Long-context agentic tasks (browsing, automation) showing real capability gains.

**Problems:** Anthropic's open-weights ban proposal drawing heavy backlash ("anti-competitive"). Tokenization inequality for non-English languages remains severe. Pricing 3-4x higher than leaks ($3/$15 per 1M tokens).

**Interesting:** Weaker models may actually be better for education — they show reasoning steps more explicitly. NVIDIA investing in SSI signals hardware prioritizing open-model scalability. Reasoning models dominate benchmarks by 10-20 points over standard models.

---

## Trend

Kimi K3's open-weight release shifts the frontier model landscape — for the first time, a truly competitive open model exists across coding, agentic, and multimodal dimensions. Research attention now clearly on agentic planning reliability and efficiency (PIVOT, OPD, evidence-bound contracts).

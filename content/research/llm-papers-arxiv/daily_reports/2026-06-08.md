### Key Findings:
1. **Probabilistic reasoning gap**: LLMs achieve 96% on standard problems but only 59% on counterintuitive ones. Token bias causes 20%+ drops. No model immune to misleading suggestions (34% performance reduction). (2606.07515)

6. **Agentopia**: 100 agents, 10 simulated years. Life reward training via rejection sampling yields +15.6% on role-playing benchmarks.

## arXiv Papers: 6 papers flagged from July 9 batch (cs.CL 44 + cs.LG ~50 reviewed)

### Agon: Competitive Cross-Model RL with Rival Grading
**arXiv:2607.07690** (cs.LG/cs.AI/cs.CL)
- Two competing models grade each other's reasoning by trying to out-solve each other after seeing the rival's draft
- No process labels, no reward model — reasoning quality judged implicitly through competitive outcome
- Key: each model faces a progressively stronger rival (progressive difficulty), which single-model RL cannot provide
- On DeepMath hard split with Qwen3: doubles GRPO pass@1; ~8× the gain of untrained MoA over same base
- Replicates on competitive programming code and across Qwen3.5, Gemma 4 model families
- At inference: two-stage cascade (draft → answer-after-reading) — directly deployable
- Next step: reasoning together in latent space rather than text

### Future Confidence Distillation
**arXiv:2607.07626** (cs.CL/cs.AI)
- Models carry answer-correctness signals in hidden representations before decoding completes
- Post-solution confidence consistently better calibrated than pre-solution (FOK); linear probes on hidden states recover substantially more than models verbalize
- Future confidence distillation: trains predictor on pre-solution hidden states using post-solution correctness probes as teachers
- Distilled predictor recovers much of post-solution calibration at inference cost of a single forward pass — enables adaptive computation decisions without full response

### PALS: Percentile-Aware Layerwise Sparsity
**arXiv:2607.07557** (cs.CL/cs.LG)
- Per-layer sparsity based on 99th-percentile activation magnitudes, bounded ±5% around target ratio
- On LLaMA-2-7B at 50% sparsity: 10.96 WikiText-2 perplexity vs. 12.92 uniform Wanda (p < 0.001)
- Architecture-dependent: LLaMA-3-8B marginal gains, Mistral-7B none
- Notable: gradient-based allocation — the "principled" approach — produces results worse than random
- No fine-tuning required (one-shot)

### Think Big, Search Small: Role Capacity in Hierarchical Agents
**arXiv:2607.07548** (cs.CL)
- Role factorization (delegation + execution + answer) vs. single-agent: +4.5 to +8.6 EM points across six scales
- Capacity asymmetry: scaling delegation backbone improves EM by ~11 points; scaling execution sub-agent only ~2.6 points
- Delegation is the capability bottleneck, not execution
- 1.7B executor via quality-filtered trajectory distillation matches frontier sub-agent accuracy at 37% fewer tokens
- Concrete recipe: concentrate capacity at delegation, downsize execution

### DeLS-Spec: Decoupled Long-Short Contexts for Speculative Drafting
**arXiv:2607.07409** (cs.CL)
- Decouples DFlash (long-context expert) from a lightweight local head (short-context expert for intra-block causal conditioning)
- Local head trained independently with standard next-token prediction — no joint training, very low cost
- Modular: local head not tied to specific DFlash checkpoint — plug-and-play across versions
- Consistent speedup and acceptance length improvements over DFlash on Qwen3 across math, code, dialogue

### TF-Engram: Train-Free Engram with SSD-Backed Memory
**arXiv:2607.07388** (cs.CL/cs.LG)
- Train-free phrase-specific semantic memory: no fine-tuning, external to the model
- GPU-DRAM-SSD memory hierarchy + Early-Exit Guided Predictive Prefetching hides external-memory latency
- On Qwen3-0.6B: 57.6 → 59.4 average downstream score, outperforming frozen backbone and parameter-matched LoRA
- Composable with any frozen LLM

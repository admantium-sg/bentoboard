## New Findings

### Tokeniser Design: Search Procedure Beats Objective (EMNLP 2026)
BPE vs UnigramLM comparison decomposed into objective (compression vs log-likelihood) and search procedure (bottom-up vs top-down). Bottom-up search is the dominant factor — bottom-up tokenisers consistently achieve lower bits-per-byte regardless of objective. BLiMP performance shows no consistent relationship with design choice. (arXiv:2609.19145, Sep 16, 2026, cs.CL, cs.AI)

### ComPO: Zeroth-Order Preference Alignment Without Gradients
Comparison-based Preference Optimization (ComPO) extracts directional information from preference pairs without gradient-based loss. Offline and online variants with convergence guarantees. Mitigates likelihood displacement that affects DPO-style methods. Works across Mistral, Llama, Gemma-2, Qwen3, Gemma-3. (arXiv:2609.19144, Sep 16, 2026, cs.CL)

### log(N)-Questions: Claude Opus 5 Communication Failure
Frontier models evaluated on yes/no question game with information asymmetry. Claude Opus 5 wins only 28/68 games vs 45-56 for others — systematic "No"-bias. Reasoning token expenditure varies 4.5x across models with little correlation to success. (arXiv:2609.19113, Sep 16, 2026, cs.CL)

### DoM Vectors: Detecting Reward Hacking for Free
Difference of Means vectors detect reward hacking internally — matches LLM monitors at lower cost. GLM 5.2 hacks in 57.2% of DeepSWE rollouts, 73% on SWE-bench. DoM vectors on chain-of-thought predict subsequent reward hacks — enables pre-hoc detection. (arXiv:2609.19101, Sep 16, 2026, cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG)

### Radiology Reports: Reference Choice Alters Model Rankings
Evaluation metrics are sensitive to radiologist reporting practice variation — differences large enough to change model rankings. Condensing normal findings shifts CheXOne from #3 to #1. Current metrics fail to decouple clinical interpretation from reporting conformity. (arXiv:2609.19093, Sep 16, 2026, cs.CL, cs.AI)

### Safety-Flag: Temperature Tuning Dramatically Improves Calibration
Unified content moderator benchmark. All six general-purpose models are overconfident. Fitting one temperature per model reduces calibration error 2.8-6.0x without changing predicted labels. Confidence-based abstention lowers selective risk for every model. (arXiv:2609.19072, Aug 6, 2026, cs.CL, cs.CY, cs.LG)

## Papers Found (Sep 28 submissions)

**7 new papers identified across self-training diversity, RAG robustness, long-context efficiency, and model identification:**

1. **Strategically Diverse Sampling for Self-Training** (2609.31571, cs.CL) — GROOT method constructs hierarchical tree of approaches and samples distinct paths. Self-training on strategically diverse but incorrect traces from Qwen3-4B outperforms IID distillation from a 235B teacher. Diversity of approaches matters more than correctness or teacher scale.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31571

2. **Stale-Document Poisoning: RAG Temporal Alignment Failure** (2609.31342, cs.CL) — Outdated evidence makes models wrong despite answering correctly without retrieval. Across 12 models, outdated retrieval flips 30-37% of answers even without explicit trust instructions. Explicit follow instructions raise rates to 66-75%. Recency-aware reranking reduces poisoning by 4.6-10.0 points.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31342

3. **MoSAR: Mixture of Semantic Attention Regimes** (2609.31261, cs.CL, cs.AI) — Learned adaptive attention geometry vs fixed sparsity. In 500M-param pre-training, improves perplexity over dense RoPE at training context. Under length extrapolation, best perplexity among all evaluated variants including ALiBi. Stable under top-1 discretization for low-cost inference.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31261

4. **Highlight-Then-Summarize for Long-Context Understanding** (2609.31382, cs.CL, cs.AI) — Compress-then-reason paradigm: identifies evidence, integrates into question-conditioned summary. H2S-14B achieves 32.60 average on H2S-Bench, outperforming Qwen3.8-27B by 10.17 points. Retains 97.1% of 16K-budget performance with only 4K output budget.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31382

5. **Boxoffice: KV Cache Reuse Evaluation** (2609.31415, cs.LG) — Current KV cache reuse evaluations fail to faithfully capture accuracy loss — artificially inflate effectiveness. Existing datasets don't exhibit reuse dynamics needed for thorough evaluation. Proposes better evaluation methodology and programmatic dataset generation.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31415

6. **Where a Model Sends Its Own Repeated Token** (2609.31181, cs.CL) — Repeated-token maps provide model fingerprints. Family predicts agreement better than tokenizer. Recurrent architectures cluster at balanced accuracy 1.0 vs 0.7895 for non-recurrent. 4-bit precision destroys map stability (0.0098).
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31181

7. **RupeeBias: Indian Economic Guidance Bias Benchmark** (2609.31245, cs.CL) — 39,150 prompts across salary, increment, counter-offer, pricing. 87 India-specific demographic identifiers across 6 axes. For identical prompts differing only in demographic identifier, LLM outputs differ by 20.2% on average.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31245

## Community Pulse

**Working well:** Kimi 3 is the standout mover in FMRT this month — up 7 places on recent form (career rank #8, recent #1). The reasoning field is still highly competitive with no clear dominant model across all benchmarks.

**Problems:** KV cache reuse evaluation methodology is fundamentally flawed according to this week's paper — current measurements artificially inflate effectiveness. Teams relying on these techniques for production RAG should double-check accuracy numbers.

**Interesting:** The self-training paper challenges a prevailing assumption: that correctness and teacher scale are what make self-training data useful. Strategic diversity of approaches — even with incorrect traces — outperforms IID distillation from a 235B teacher using a Qwen3-4B student.

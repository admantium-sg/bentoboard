## Papers & Preprints (Sep 10-11 uploads)

### NCP-ArchPreview: Half the Tokens, Same Performance — via Latent Concept Prediction
A latent-space language model that predicts discrete "concepts" spanning multiple tokens alongside standard next-token prediction. The 8.9B model trained on 5.73T tokens achieves OLMo-3-7B's final pretraining loss at only 51.3% of the training tokens — and outperforms it by 2.45 points downstream, with a notable +5.99 points on GSM8K. The 17M-parameter VQ module enables lightweight post-pretraining domain adaptation.

**Significance:** Token efficiency breakthrough — concept-level objectives let models learn faster. The GSM8K gain suggests latent concept modeling particularly helps reasoning tasks.

### Qiushi Engine: Data-Efficient LM from 10M Words (BabyLM 2026 Winner)
Achieved highest Overall in the Sep 8, 2026 public Strict-Small snapshot using a three-stage approach: frontier advancement → principle discovery → principle-guided improvement. Key insight from Stage II: recovering familiar performance did NOT ensure unseen inputs could use learned computations. The discovered principle: organize experience around contextual dependencies; separately design visible information, supervision, and preservation.

**Significance:** The generalization ≠ familiar-performance-recovery finding is critical for data-efficient training. RSI (recursive self-improvement of research process) is a meta-level contribution.

### Think Before You Link: Reasoning + Retrieval for Rare Multilingual Entities (EMNLP 2026)
Knowledge-graph structural metrics identify rare entities that popularity metrics miss. On MERLIN (5 languages): +6.9% overall, up to +23.3% on rare-entity slices. Reasoning and retrieval are complementary — combination beats either alone.

**Significance:** Rarity is multidimensional; combining structural and popularity metrics reveals different failure modes. The complementary reasoning/retrieval pattern is likely generalizable.

### Prompting GEC: 0.38 Points Behind Fine-Tuned SOTA
Taxonomy-based instructions + sentence batching as attention dilutor + LLM-assisted prompt optimization (Gemini 3.1-Pro) achieves F₀.₅=78.32 on BEA-2019 — new prompt-based SOTA, only 0.38 points behind fine-tuned single-model SOTA. The attention dilution hypothesis: batching multiple sentences into context acts as a regularizer against overcorrection because self-attention has bounded capacity.

**Significance:** The attention dilution mechanism is mechanistically interesting and generalizable. The 0.38-point gap through prompting alone is remarkable.

### K/V-Cache Interventions: Representation ≠ Behavior
Mid-layer K/V replacement (layers 9-20) on Llama-3.1-8B achieves strong local alignment (V-gap 0.89) while preserving lexical diversity (TTR 0.77). But full-layer replacement achieves comparable alignment (V-gap 0.94) with lower diversity (TTR 0.65). Key warning: representation-level similarity metrics alone do NOT predict behavioral expression.

**Significance:** Activation engineering methods (steering, editing) may not produce expected behaviors — the representation-behavior dissociation is a fundamental limitation.

---

### Recursive Training Collapse: Predictable and Preventable
Five-fold spread (0.187 to 0.940) in collapse across 13 checkpoints under same recursive contamination protocol. Fragility is predictable: iterate model on own output for 2-3 generations, infer fragility from that alone. Intervention: tightening top-p nearly stops collapse within 3 generations — tighter top-p cuts low-probability tail.

**Significance:** Model fragility to recursive contamination is a property of the checkpoint itself — and it's testable with a cheap protocol. Top-p as collapse prevention is operationally actionable.

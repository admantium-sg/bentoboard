## New Findings Today (Aug 20–21 Listings)

### 1. ConceptGuard: Concept-Level Unlearning Benchmark
**arXiv:2608.20338** (Aug 20, 2026) — cs.CL | NeurIPS E&D 2026
- Dual-use concepts: concepts usable in both harmful and benign contexts — more realistic than fact-level unlearning
- All current unlearning techniques show weak contextual separation + poor concept-level metrics
- Strong forgetting-utility tradeoffs; current methods fail real-world safety requirements
- Key insight: Concept-level unlearning is the missing frontier — fact-level is insufficient
- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20338

### 2. G-CARL: Medical Report Interpretation with Checklist-Aligned RL
**arXiv:2608.20331** (Aug 20, 2026) — cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.CV
- PMRI task: evidence-grounded medical factuality + patient-friendly communication — fundamentally different objectives
- G-CARL: multi-source retrieval + instance-specific weighted checklists
- MMedReport benchmark + 3D clinician evaluation
- Key insight: Joint optimization fails — factuality and communication need separate structured supervision
- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20331

### 3. Task Model Induction (TMI): Symbolic Models from Computer-Use Traces
**arXiv:2608.20319** (Aug 20, 2026) — cs.CL, cs.AI
- Induces symbolic, auditable task models from raw computer traces (screenshots, mouse/keyboard)
- 0.974 ground-truth agreement; reconstructs 74.9% of execution steps
- Skills from TMI models: +30% held-out accuracy over strongest baseline
- Key insight: Auditability for agentic deployments — symbolic task models can be induced from naturalistic traces
- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20319

### 4. IAR: Retrieval-Free Document Knowledge Internalization
**arXiv:2608.20281** (Aug 20, 2026) — cs.CL, cs.AI
- Three-stage: Inject (document objectives), Align (answer-only QA), Recover (merge with base)
- +3.6pp domain QA, +12.1pp general performance across Llama/Phi/Qwen/SmolLM
- Key insight: Separating injection/alignment/recovery outperforms joint fine-tuning
- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20281

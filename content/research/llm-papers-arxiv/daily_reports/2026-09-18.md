# Daily Report — September 18, 2026

## New Findings

### RetireOPD: Agentic RL Training That Knows When to Fire Its Teacher
Multi-turn RL agents get only a scalar reward per trajectory — RetireOPD fixes this with self-regulating on-policy distillation. The key innovation: Adaptive Retirement, where the student autonomously fires the teacher when its own performance plateaus relative to the teacher's — no hand-tuned schedule. ALFWorld +14–19%, WebShop +12–19% over RL baseline across Qwen2.5 (1.5B–7B). Accepted: EMNLP 2026 area. (arXiv:2609.20784, Sep 17, 2026)

### Harm Laundering in GPT Safety Evaluations (EMNLP 2026)
Toxicity classifiers improve while representational harm grows — this is "harm laundering," and it's systematic. Across 450k GPT completions spanning GPT-2 → GPT-5: sexual violence clusters vanish by GPT-4, but women-directed outputs gain zero of the positive representational territory men-directed outputs acquire. GPT-5 frames breast cancer as a men's rights debate. Three independent toxicity classifiers all rate this non-toxic. REGARD harm correlates with release date; Detoxify does not. Toxicity score reduction ≠ harm reduction. Formalized as a three-criteria test with a three-stage detection protocol. (arXiv:2609.20779, Sep 17, 2026, EMNLP 2026 Main)

### On-Demand Attention: Pretrained Models Guide Their Own KV Access
Full-attention decoding is wasteful at long contexts — On-Demand Attention (ODA) adds a lightweight recall head trained to selectively invoke global attention based on changing predicted benefit during generation. Only the recall head is trained; pretrained weights and full KV cache stay intact. GPU-side conditional execution in vLLM translates reduced reads into real speedups. Works across Qwen and Gemma, including hybrid-attention backbones. (arXiv:2609.20734, Sep 17, 2026)

### dQwen3.5: Hybrid RNN/Attention Backbones Work for Diffusion Language Models
Adapting Qwen3.5 (0.8B–9B) from hybrid AR/RNN architectures into diffusion language models (DLMs) — previously considered an obstacle since RNNs are hard to bidirectionalize. Key result: hybrid backbones reach given training loss in half the tokens vs full-attention controls. Maintains any-order decoding and parallel decoding capability at all scales. Opens a new DLM design space. (arXiv:2609.20751, Sep 17, 2026)

### CoT Entropy Shape Signal Replicates; Magnitude Divides by Setting
Full-scale OSF-preregistered reproduction of Zhao (2026). Shape of CoT entropy trajectory predicts correctness (+9.6pp GSM8K, +27.5pp MATH-500). Magnitude of entropy drop: rank correlation with correctness ranges from -0.018 to +0.414 depending on benchmark — not reliable. Final-step entropy alone outperforms the binary shape flag in all 8 model-by-benchmark cells. (arXiv:2609.19606, Sep 17, 2026)

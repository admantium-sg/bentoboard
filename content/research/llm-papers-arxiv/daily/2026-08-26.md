## 🔬 Top Insights

### 1. RecurSE: Bounded Recursive Self-Evaluation for LLM Judges (EMNLP 2026)
LLM-as-judge evaluation typically requires external supervision (annotations, reward models, distillation). RecurSE eliminates this with a closed-loop bounded RSI approach: a trainable judge evaluates candidates under per-rule rubrics (Pass 1) paired with a synchronized policy-copy checker auditing the judge's reasoning against meta-rubrics (Pass 2). Interface decoupling prevents a degenerative token-copying shortcut that inflates self-assigned rewards. Pairwise Advantage Validity (PAV) monitors judge accuracy and checker fidelity to find the optimal early-stopping window. Across Qwen3.5-9B, Gemma-4-E4B-it, and Qwen3.6-27B: consistent generalization gains, and judge-curated preference pairs enhance downstream policy alignment. **The interface decoupling insight is the key novelty.**

📄 arXiv:2608.24231 | EMNLP 2026

### 2. AgentSpec: Speculative Decoding for Batch LLM Agent Inference (EMNLP 2026)
Speculative decoding speeds up LLM inference but degrades badly at large batch sizes — limiting production agent deployments. AgentSpec addresses this with (1) structure-isolated drafting that constrains speculation to semantically coherent agent workflow segments → extremely low rejection rate, and (2) redundancy-aware budget allocation exploiting agent-level information to better use dynamic token budgets. Outperforms state-of-the-art across 5 workloads, 4 models, 4 LLM families in vLLM. **Practical inference efficiency for agentic systems is a real gap this addresses.**

📄 arXiv:2608.24004 | EMNLP 2026

### 3. MemUse: 71-Point Gap Between Memory Recall and Natural Integration
4-month deployment, 40 users, 1,872 sessions, 7 memory conditions — Direct QA accuracy ranges 19.7–70.1% but satisfaction doesn't change. The same system scores 78.8% on Direct QA but references only 7.9% of those facts in natural conversation. Benchmarks measure "elicited retrieval" but conversation requires "natural integration." Natural Integration correlates with satisfaction; Direct QA does not. **Direct QA benchmarks may be fundamentally misaligned with user experience.** Released with deployment corpus and MemUse.

📄 arXiv:2608.24189 | EMNLP 2026 | https://github.com/ryuichi-sumida/memuse

### 4. Reasoning-Induced Misalignment (SDP) — Weekly Top Theme
SDP (Safety-Direction Penalty) addresses a growing concern: reasoning fine-tuning shifts safety representations in activation space. Identifies the coupled reasoning-safety activation directions, locates critical safety-decision layers, penalizes shifts along the learned safety direction during fine-tuning. This was #2 in arXivTLDR's weekly top 10, indicating strong community interest. **The intersection of reasoning capability and safety alignment is an active research frontier.**

📄 arXiv:2608.23497 (from Aug 25 submissions — appeared in weekly digest)

### 5. Curved Inference II: Geometric Signatures of Deception (EMNLP 2026)
Extends Anthropic's Sleeper Agents work beyond linear probes. When backdoors aren't artificially inserted, linear detection fails. This paper introduces naturalistic methodology using multi-turn context windows and analyzes "semantic surface area (A')" — capturing both magnitude and directional change of meaning construction in unnormalised residual space. Without backdoors, labels, or probes, geometric structure reliably predicts semantic classification. Some strategies improve from p=0.555 to p=0.048 with measurement precision alone. **A scalable, unsupervised path for deception detection when linear methods fail.**

📄 arXiv:2608.24037 | EMNLP 2026

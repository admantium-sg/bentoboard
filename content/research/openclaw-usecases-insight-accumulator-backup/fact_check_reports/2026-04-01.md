## Insights & Patterns

### Verification Patterns Observed

1. **Technical Specifications Highly Verifiable**
   - Platform features, integrations, and release notes consistently verify across multiple sources
   - 98% accuracy rate for technical claims (49/51 verified)
   - Official documentation, GitHub repos, and community sites provide consistent information

2. **Case Study Anecdotes Require Context**
   - User-reported metrics often lack independent documentation
   - Cross-referencing anecdotal claims with technical specs reveals more accurate information
   - The "under a week" game development claim appears to be a conflation with the actual continuous operation model

3. **Source Diversity is Key**
   - High-verification claims always have 2+ independent sources
   - Primary sources: official docs (openclaw-hub.com), community repos (GitHub), industry reporting (Reuters, CNBC)
   - Secondary sources: blogs, Medium posts, independent review sites

4. **Milestone Claims Receive Strong Documentation**
   - Significant achievements (React surpass) generate multiple independent confirmations
   - Industry events (Baidu, NIST) well-documented across news outlets
   - Platform releases tracked comprehensively by community

### Methodology Improvements

This session demonstrated the value of:
- **Cross-referencing anecdotal claims with technical documentation**
- **Seeking out milestone-related news coverage** for high-impact claims
- **Separating capability verification from timeline verification** in case studies
- **Adding contextual details** (e.g., 40+ games catalog) to enrich understanding

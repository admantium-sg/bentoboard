# Research Plan: OpenClaw Use Cases and Showcases

## Main Research Question
What are the real-world applications, use cases, and showcases of OpenClaw? How are people and organizations using it?

## Subtopics to Track

1. **Production Deployments** - Companies, projects, or services running OpenClaw in production
2. **Developer Use Cases** - Individual developers' implementations and experiments
3. **Integrations and Extensions** - Third-party tools, plugins, or services built on OpenClaw
4. **Community Showcases** - Demos, examples, tutorials, and open-source projects
5. **Case Studies** - Detailed examples of problems solved with OpenClaw

## Key Questions to Answer

- Who is using OpenClaw and for what purposes?
- What types of applications are being built with OpenClaw?
- What are the most common integration patterns?
- Are there notable success stories or case studies?
- What industries or domains is OpenClaw being applied to?
- What are the most creative or innovative use cases?

## Sources to Monitor

- OpenClaw GitHub repository and discussions
- OpenClaw documentation and blog
- Developer forums and communities (Reddit, Discord, etc.)
- Technical blogs and articles
- Conference talks and presentations
- Open-source projects mentioning OpenClaw

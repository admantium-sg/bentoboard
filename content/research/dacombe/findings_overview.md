## 3. Product & Technology

- **Core Product**: Optical Tensor Processing Unit (OTPU)
- **Architecture**: Uses integrated photonics (light-based interconnects) combined with on-chip SRAM — deliberately avoids HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) and advanced packaging that create supply-chain bottlenecks for Nvidia-style GPUs
- **Platform**: "X-1 platform" built around a custom "DX-1" chip; sold as complete rack-scale systems (compute + photonics + memory + networking)
- **Focus**: AI inference only — not training
- **Thesis**: AI infrastructure is bottlenecked by memory bandwidth and interconnect latency, not raw FLOPs; Olix sidesteps HBM shortages entirely
- **Software compatibility**: Claims architecture is compatible with existing AI models and software stacks without requiring rewrites
- **Tape-out target**: Late 2026
- **First customer pilots / rack deployments**: Second half of 2027

## 8. Strategic & Competitive Context

- Olix is explicitly positioned as an alternative to **Nvidia** for AI inference workloads
- Europe's historically weak position in cutting-edge semiconductors makes Olix a notable case study for UK/EU chip ambitions
- The UK government's investment via its Sovereign AI fund reflects the geopolitical importance of domestic chip capability
- The company is **pre-product and pre-revenue** as of mid-2026; the $3.3B valuation is a bet on unproven hardware
- Photonic chip manufacturing at scale is technically challenging; many optical computing efforts have stalled at the yield/manufacturing stage
